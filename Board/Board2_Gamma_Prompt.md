# Revenue Terminology -- Booking vs. Backlog vs. Revenue

The Board mandates strict, non-interchangeable use of three terms across all materials.

## Definitions (visual: three-column flow)

**Booking** = Total contract value at signature. CRITICAL: equals zero if no confirmed start date.

**Backlog** = Signed revenue remaining to be recognized. Formula: Booking minus already-recognized revenue.

**Revenue (CA)** = Accounting revenue, recognized per applicable standards (IFRS / French GAAP).

## Applied example: ICON 1st contract
- Contract signed but no confirmed start date at signature
- Booking reported: EUR 0 (conservative rule applied)
- Booking updated to TCV only when deployment began
- Revenue recognition: monthly over service period

# Deferred Revenue (PCA) -- Policy Decision

## Option A: Managed PCA
- Maintain deferred revenue accounting with stricter collection enforcement
- Risk: "artificial" EBITDA if invoices raised but uncollected
- Requires robust AR management processes

## Option B: Abandon PCA
- Pure accounting reality -- cash-based or strict recognition only
- Cleaner working capital and EBITDA picture
- Simpler audit, less process overhead
- May understate near-term performance

## Impact comparison table
| Dimension | Option A | Option B |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| EBITDA | Higher (but may include unpaid) | Lower but realistic |
| Working capital | Requires active AR | Cleaner |
| Audit readiness | Standard but complex | Simpler |

# Appendix

## Glossary
- **Booking:** Total contract value at signature (= 0 without confirmed start date)
- **Backlog:** Signed revenue remaining to recognize
- **Revenue (CA / Chiffre d'affaires):** Accounting revenue per standards
- **PCA (Produits Constates d'Avance):** Deferred revenue
- **CAC (Commissaire aux Comptes):** Statutory auditor (not to be confused with Customer Acquisition Cost)
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** Total S&M spend / new logos acquired
- **NRR:** Net Revenue Retention
- **Rule of 40:** Revenue growth % + EBITDA margin % (benchmark: >= 40%)
- **MRR:** Monthly Recurring Revenue
- **ARR:** Annual Recurring Revenue
