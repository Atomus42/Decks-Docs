# Revenue Terminology -- Booking vs. Backlog vs. Revenue

The Board mandates strict, non-interchangeable use of three terms across all materials. This discipline is now enforced.

## Definitions (visual: three-column flow)

**Booking** = Total contract value at signature. CRITICAL: equals zero if no confirmed start date.

**Backlog** = Signed revenue remaining to be recognized. Formula: Booking minus already-recognized revenue.

**Revenue (CA)** = Accounting revenue, recognized per applicable standards (French GAAP).

## Applied example: ICON 1st contract ($650k)
- Contract signed but deployment start date not immediately confirmed
- Booking reported: **EUR 0** until deployment began (conservative rule applied)
- Once deployment started: booking updated to $650k TCV
- Revenue recognition: monthly over service period
- This discipline prevented a misleading $650k booking from inflating our numbers prematurely

# Deferred Revenue (PCA) -- Policy Decision

## Context
The CS raised that clients frequently exceed 60-day payment windows, creating risk of "artificial" EBITDA from unpaid invoices.

## Recommendation: Option B -- Abandon PCA, adopt strict recognition

| Dimension | Rationale |
|-----------|-----------|
| **EBITDA** | Lower but honest -- reflects actual cash reality, not invoiced-but-uncollected amounts |
| **Working capital** | Cleaner picture; no AR inflation from slow-paying pharma clients |
| **Audit readiness** | Simpler for upcoming CAC (statutory auditor) appointment and accounting firm transition |
| **Investor trust** | Sophisticated Board prefers conservative, auditable numbers over optically higher EBITDA |

## Impact analysis
- EBITDA will appear lower by the amount of invoiced-but-uncollected revenue (estimated 50-100k EUR at any point)
- Working capital improved: no deferred revenue liability on balance sheet
- Aligns with the conservative booking rule (zero without start date) for a consistent financial philosophy
- Makes FY2025 financial statement transition to new accounting firm cleaner

This approach means every euro of reported revenue has been both recognized AND collected or is within normal payment terms.

# Payback Period & Path to Growth Margin

## Layer 1 -- Gross margin recovery
- Average initial contract value: **~$100k/year** (blended across tiers)
- Real gross margin: **~90%** (incorporating infrastructure + support allocation)
- Annual gross margin per customer: **~$90k**
- CAC: ~$150k (converted from EUR)
- **CAC payback: ~20 months on gross margin**

## Layer 2 -- Full cost overlay (path to growth margin)
- R&D allocation per customer (13 FTE IT @ 120k / 20 clients): ~78k EUR/year
- G&A allocation per customer: ~30k EUR/year
- Total cost per customer: 150k CAC + 108k/year R&D+G&A
- **Growth margin inflection: ~30 months** -- where cumulative customer contribution covers all allocated costs

## Key insight
Contract duration must exceed 30 months for full unit profitability. This is why:
- Multi-year discounts (10% for 2yr, 15% for 3yr) are strategically sound -- they lock in duration
- Land-and-expand (AKIMBO at $10-35k, then upsell to full BRA) reduces effective CAC on expansion revenue
- NRR > 110% would compress payback dramatically -- which is why retention is our #1 priority

# Appendix

## Glossary
- **Booking:** Total contract value at signature (= 0 without confirmed start date)
- **Backlog:** Signed revenue remaining to recognize
- **Revenue (CA / Chiffre d'affaires):** Accounting revenue per applicable standards
- **PCA (Produits Constates d'Avance):** Deferred revenue
- **CAC (Commissaire aux Comptes):** Statutory auditor -- distinct from Customer Acquisition Cost
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** Total S&M spend / new logos acquired
- **NRR:** Net Revenue Retention
- **Rule of 40:** Revenue growth % + EBITDA margin % (benchmark: >= 40%)
- **MRR / ARR:** Monthly / Annual Recurring Revenue
- **BRAT:** Benefit-Risk Action Team (EMA framework)
- **Production Immobilisee:** Capitalized R&D costs (French GAAP)
- **CIR (Credit d'Impot Recherche):** French research tax credit
