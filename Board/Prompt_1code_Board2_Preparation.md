## CS (Strategic Committee) directives to integrate -- these are the 8 key topics

### 1. HR Management -- US Sales

The Board moved from subjective assessment ("ressenti") to results-based evaluation. Concrete short-term objectives have been set for the US sales hire: number of meetings booked, R1-to-R2 conversion rate, new deals signed, over a 1-month trial period. **For the deck:** show actual performance data for the US sales rep against these KPIs. Present the decision framework: what thresholds trigger confirmation vs. separation. If a second US hire was made, present the same framework for that person.

### 2. SaaS Dashboard for the Board (single-slide KPI view)

Build one unified, readable slide that Clement can connect to live data with instant updates. It must contain:

- **MRR tracking:** monthly snapshot, YoY evolution, decomposition into New MRR, Expansion MRR, Churn MRR, Downgrade MRR. Churn must be calculated on a rolling 12-month basis.
- **Gross margin scope:** integrate infrastructure costs (cloud, hosting, compute) and support costs (CSM, deployment team time) to produce a real gross margin figure, not just a software margin.
- **Financial indicators:** EBITDA (YoY and YTD vs Budget), net cash position, and the "Rule of 40" calculation (revenue growth rate % + EBITDA margin %). Reference: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rule_of_40

### 3. Revenue recognition and terminology -- strict definitions

The Board insists on clear, non-interchangeable use of three terms across all materials:

- **Booking:** total contract value at signature date. CRITICAL RULE: if a contract has no confirmed start date, its booking value must be shown as zero in the reporting until the deployment actually begins (cf. ICON 1st contract precedent).
- **Backlog:** signed revenue remaining to be recognized (i.e., booking minus already-recognized revenue).
- **CA (Chiffre d'affaires / Revenue):** accounting revenue, recognized per applicable standards.

No mixing of these three concepts anywhere in the deck or financial package.

### 4. CAC & Payback Period

- **CAC:** calculated on a rolling 12-month basis. Must include the entirety of Sales & Marketing costs (salaries, commissions, tools, travel, events, content, paid ads -- everything).
- **Payback:** calculated on gross margin (not revenue). This measures how quickly a new customer's gross margin contribution covers the cost of acquiring them.
- **Visualization approach:** first show the CAC recovery curve (time to recoup acquisition cost from gross margin). Then layer in the additional company costs (R&D, G&A) that impact profitability, until the cumulative contribution turns positive -- this positive inflection represents the "growth margin" point.

### 5. Churn signals and NRR (Net Revenue Retention)

- Calculate **NRR by cohort** to validate retention quality across different vintage groups.
- Beyond NPS, implement monitoring of **usage rate** as the leading indicator of churn risk.
- Caveat to document: for on-premise / deported infrastructure deployments, connection/usage data may be considered sensitive information by the client, making usage tracking difficult. Acknowledge this limitation explicitly and propose alternative proxy metrics where direct usage data is unavailable.

### 6. Budget and Treasury

- Every budget line must be systematically paired with its **treasury plan** (clear distinction between: invoicing date, revenue recognition date, and cash collection date).
- **Conservative rule:** any contract without a firm start date must be set to zero in the financial trajectory (do not include in projections). This aligns with the booking rule in point 3.
- Distinguish clearly between: invoicing schedule, revenue recognition timing, and actual cash inflows.

### 7. PCA (Produits Constates d'Avance / Deferred Revenue) and Risk Management

Two options were discussed:

- **Option A:** Properly manage deferred revenue (PCA) with a stricter collection enforcement process to prevent "artificial" EBITDA caused by unpaid invoices accumulating as recognized revenue.
- **Option B (potentially preferred):** Abandon PCA entirely and base the financial analysis purely on accounting reality (cash-based or strict recognition), which may be more appropriate given that clients frequently exceed the 60-day end-of-month payment windows.

Whichever option is chosen, present the rationale and its implications on EBITDA, working capital, and audit readiness.

### 8. Ops Implementation -- per-contract tracking

Set up contract-level tracking to automate dashboard production as much as possible. Each contract should feed: booking date, start date, invoicing schedule, recognition schedule, collection status, renewal date, expansion events, churn risk score.
