# CureVac -- Stakeholder Intelligence

> Research Date: March 2026
> Methodology: Public sources only (corporate website, press releases, SEC filings, news articles)
> IMPORTANT NOTE: BioNTech completed its acquisition of CureVac in December 2025 (86.75% of shares tendered in an all-stock deal valued at ~$1.25B). CureVac's leadership team and organizational structure may be undergoing integration into BioNTech. The individuals listed below were verified as CureVac leaders prior to the acquisition close. Current roles post-acquisition should be verified.

## Notes on Organizational Context

**BioNTech Acquisition (December 2025):** BioNTech closed its acquisition of CureVac in December 2025. BioNTech CEO Ugur Sahin described the transaction as "another building block in BioNTech's oncology strategy." This fundamentally changes the stakeholder landscape:
- CureVac's mRNA oncology programs (CVGBM, CVHNLC, personalized immunotherapy) are now part of BioNTech's broader oncology pipeline.
- Budget authority for technology investments likely shifts to BioNTech leadership.
- CureVac's key scientific talent (especially Mendila's mRNA oncology expertise and Jaegle's regulatory capabilities) may be retained but integrated into BioNTech's organizational structure.
- ArcaScience should consider whether to pursue CureVac contacts as entry points into BioNTech's much larger organization, or to approach BioNTech directly.

**Pre-Acquisition Leadership Gaps:** CureVac did not publicly list a dedicated Head of Pharmacovigilance or Head of Drug Safety separate from the CDO/CSO role. This reflects the company's pre-commercial, early-stage pipeline status (all programs in Phase 1/1b). Myriam Mendila's explicit pharmacovigilance experience from Novartis suggests she oversees this function directly.
