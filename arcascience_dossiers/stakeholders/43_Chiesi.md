# Chiesi Farmaceutici — Stakeholder Intelligence

> Research Date: March 2026
> Methodology: Public sources only (corporate websites, press releases, conference listings, leadership pages)
> Note: Chiesi is a family-controlled, B Corp-certified Italian pharmaceutical company (founded 1935, Parma). Revenue EUR 3.41B (FY2024). Organized around three global franchises: AIR (respiratory), CARE (neonatology), and RARE (rare diseases via Chiesi Global Rare Diseases). R&D investment of EUR 829M (24.3% of revenue).

## Key Organizational Insight

Chiesi's R&D organization integrates clinical development, regulatory affairs, pharmacovigilance, and quality assurance under the EVP of Global R&D (Diego Ardigo). Within this structure, Stefan Herdinius leads the combined Global Regulatory Affairs and Patient Safety function -- making him the single point of authority for both regulatory strategy and drug safety/pharmacovigilance. Chiesi Global Rare Diseases operates as a distinct business unit with its own R&D (Mitch Goldman) and Medical Affairs (Rachele Berria) leadership, reflecting the specialized nature of rare disease development. The company's B Corp certification and strong ESG commitments mean that patient-centricity and sustainability considerations are explicitly part of the benefit-risk framework.
