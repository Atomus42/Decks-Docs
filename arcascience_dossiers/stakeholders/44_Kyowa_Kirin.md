## Key Organizational Insight

Kyowa Kirin is in the midst of a significant leadership and structural transformation. The March 2026 appointment of Abdul Mullick as the first non-Japanese CEO signals an acceleration toward becoming a truly global specialty pharma. The new C-suite Executive framework (replacing the Executive Officer system) positions the CMO (Takeyoshi Yamashita), Global Development Head (Ernesto Aycardi), and Global Medical Affairs Head (Eslie Dennis) as key decision-makers in a more agile governance structure. The company's PV organization has been global since 2020 with a matrix structure across four functions and four regions. Kyowa Kirin's portfolio spans four different modalities (antibodies, small molecules, cell/gene therapy, and the recently regained rocatinlimab program), creating a uniquely complex benefit-risk landscape.
