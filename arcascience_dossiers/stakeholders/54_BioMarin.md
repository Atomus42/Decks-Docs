## Identified Leaders (Publicly Verified)

- **Title:** Executive Vice President, Chief Research & Development Officer
- **Function:** R&D Leadership / Clinical Development / Regulatory Affairs / Medical Affairs
- **Geography:** San Rafael, CA, United States (global scope)
- **Seniority Tier:** 1 (Executive)
- **Source:** https://www.biomarin.com/company/leadership/greg-friberg/
- **Strategic Relevance:** Appointed September 2024, succeeding Hank Fuchs after a 15-year tenure. A BRA platform would support his oversight of BioMarin's "Core 5" portfolio evaluation framework and the company's disciplined approach to program prioritization (4 programs discontinued in 2024).
