## Key Organizational Insight

Aurobindo Pharma's stakeholder landscape for ArcaScience is concentrated in the CuraTeQ Biologics subsidiary, which operates the 14-product biosimilar pipeline with $50B+ addressable market. The parent company's core generics business (848+ ANDA filings) has limited need for sophisticated B-R tools, but the biologics division is the company's primary growth engine. Dr. Satakarni Makkapati serves as the bridge between the parent company board (as Non-Executive Director) and the biosimilar operations (as CEO of Biologics, Vaccines, and Peptides), making him the single most important stakeholder. The upcoming denosumab biosimilar EMA submission (April 2026) and 4 ongoing global Phase 3 studies create immediate demand for B-R evidence integration. Aurobindo's entry into biologics CDMO (Merck & Co. partnership, TheraNym facility) further signals quality and capability ambitions. Extreme cost-consciousness of the generics-first culture requires ROI-focused positioning of any B-R platform.
