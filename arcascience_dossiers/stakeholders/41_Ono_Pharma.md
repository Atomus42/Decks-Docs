## Key Organizational Insight

Ono Pharmaceutical is in the midst of a transformational reorganization. As of July 2025, Deciphera Pharmaceuticals (Waltham, MA) serves as the global business hub for all R&D, commercial, and medical affairs operations in the U.S. and Europe. Japan-based operations continue from Osaka headquarters. This dual structure means benefit-risk governance spans two centers of gravity: Yasushi Hirota's Corporate Regulatory Compliance, Safety and Quality Assurance division in Osaka, and Matthew Sherman's CMO organization at Deciphera in the U.S. The company manages safety for Opdivo (via BMS partnership), QINLOCK, ROMVIMZA, and 11+ global clinical-stage candidates.
