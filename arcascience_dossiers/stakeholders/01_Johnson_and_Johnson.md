## Key Organizational Insight

J&J's Office of the Chief Medical Officer (OCMO) is the primary organizational home for benefit-risk assessment, safety science, and epidemiology. The OCMO operates as a functionally independent body across both Innovative Medicine and MedTech. J&J has publicly stated it is "a pioneer in structured benefit-risk assessments" and is actively leveraging AI-enabled technologies for safety signal management, dose selection, and real-world evidence integration.
