## Key Organizational Insight

Grifols is a plasma-derived medicines specialist with a unique organizational structure. The Executive Committee (8 members) is led by CEO Nacho Abia and organized around business units (Biopharma, Diagnostic, Plasma Procurement) and corporate functions. Clinical development and regulatory affairs sit below the Executive Committee level. The Chief Scientific Innovation Officer (Schüttrumpf) oversees the full development cycle from discovery through clinical trials. Grifols' innovation pipeline is focused on plasma-derived therapies (immunoglobulins, fibrinogen, trimodulin) and leverages a repository of over 100 million plasma samples linked to real-world health data. The company has publicly stated it uses AI and advanced technologies as part of its innovation strategy.
