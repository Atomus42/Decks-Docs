## Notes on Organizational Structure

Amphastar is a founder-led, family-controlled company (Jack Zhang and Mary Luo are co-founders and married, with combined ~25% insider ownership). Decision-making is highly centralized. The company does not publicly list a dedicated Chief Medical Officer, Head of Pharmacovigilance, or Head of Drug Safety, reflecting its historical orientation as a specialty generic/manufacturing company. As Amphastar transitions toward proprietary drug development (AMP-105, AMP-107), these functions may expand or be formalized. The Regulatory Affairs and Clinical Operations functions are combined under Tony Marrs, consistent with a lean mid-cap organization.
