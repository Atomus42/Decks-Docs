## Key Organizational Insight

Bausch Health's R&D and clinical organization underwent significant transformation with the appointment of Jonathan Sadeh as CMO/Head of R&D in December 2024. The CMO role serves as the organizational nexus for clinical development, regulatory affairs, and pharmacovigilance. The company's expanding hepatology portfolio -- with two concurrent late-stage programs (RED-C for rifaximin SSD and larsucosterol Phase 3 under FDA Breakthrough Therapy Designation) -- creates an acute need for integrated benefit-risk assessment capabilities. The potential Bausch + Lomb separation adds complexity, as B-R governance infrastructure may need to serve both entities. The VP-level pharmacovigilance and global regulatory affairs leadership roles are not prominently disclosed in public sources, suggesting these functions report within the CMO's organization.
