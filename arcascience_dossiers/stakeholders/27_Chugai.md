## Key Organizational Insight

Chugai's organization is structured around an integrated lifecycle management system where Drug Safety, Medical Affairs, Regulatory Affairs, and Clinical Development are coordinated at the project level under the Project & Lifecycle Management Unit (led by Kusano). Drug Safety and Medical Affairs fall under Shinji Hidaka's supervisory portfolio, while Clinical Development and Research fall under Hitoshi Iikura. Regulatory Compliance falls under Kaori Ouchi. This distributed model means ArcaScience must engage multiple executive stakeholders to achieve cross-functional adoption. The "AI Everywhere" transformation strategy and the SoftBank generative AI partnership signal strong institutional receptivity to AI-powered tools, but the MALEXA platform's prominence may also create "not invented here" dynamics. The Roche alliance adds complexity -- co-developed products require dual-company alignment on B-R tools.

**Priority Contacts for ArcaScience:**
1. Tsukasa Kusano (EVP, Head of Project & Lifecycle Management) -- operational B-R decision-making for all pipeline assets
2. Shinji Hidaka (EVP, Drug Safety & Medical Affairs) -- supervisory authority over safety and medical evidence functions
3. Hitoshi Iikura (Director, EVP, Research & Clinical Development) -- R&D budget authority and AI transformation champion
4. Kaori Ouchi (EVP, Quality & Regulatory Compliance) -- regulatory-grade B-R output requirements
5. Kazuhiko Nishi (VP, Head of Medical Affairs) -- medical evidence generation and B-R data consumption
