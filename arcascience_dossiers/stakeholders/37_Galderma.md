## Key Organizational Insight

Galderma operates a **Product Quality and Safety Sub-Committee** — a multi-disciplinary management board that includes the Global Head of R&D, Global Head of Operations, Chief Scientific Officer, and Global Heads of Quality and Safety. This sub-committee is responsible for product quality, patient/consumer safety, monitoring safety KPIs, and endorsing mitigation actions. Galderma's vigilance activities span Pharmacovigilance, Cosmetovigilance, and Medical Devices Vigilance across the full portfolio.

Notably, Galderma has been actively recruiting for a **Global Head of Clinical Safety** role (based in Barcelona, Spain), suggesting this is either a recently created position or a vacancy. This role would manage global PV staff focused on clinical development safety activities and would report to senior R&D leadership.

Galderma's expanding biologics pipeline (nemolizumab for atopic dermatitis and prurigo nodularis; relabotulinumtoxinA) is increasing the complexity of the company's benefit-risk assessment needs, creating a growing use case for structured B-R platforms.
