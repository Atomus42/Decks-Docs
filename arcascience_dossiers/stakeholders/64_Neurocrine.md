- **Title:** Chief Medical Officer
- **Function:** Clinical Development / Medical Affairs
- **Geography:** San Diego, CA, USA (global scope)
- **Seniority Tier:** 1 (Executive)
- **Source:** https://neurocrine.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/neurocrine-biosciences-appoints-dr-sanjay-keswani-chief-medical
