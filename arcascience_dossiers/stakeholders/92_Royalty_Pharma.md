# Royalty Pharma -- Stakeholder Intelligence

> Research Date: March 2026
> Methodology: Public sources only (corporate website, press releases, SEC filings, investor presentations)
> Note: Royalty Pharma has NO traditional pharma functions (no Clinical Development, Regulatory Affairs, Pharmacovigilance, or Medical Affairs). The company acquires royalty streams on products developed by others. However, its investment team performs de facto benefit-risk assessment as core to every capital deployment decision. Identified leaders are those whose roles most closely align with B-R analytical needs.
