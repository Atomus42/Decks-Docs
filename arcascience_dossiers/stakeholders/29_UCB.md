# UCB S.A. -- Stakeholder Intelligence

> Research Date: March 2026
> Methodology: Public sources only (corporate website, press releases, annual reports, conference transcripts)
> Note: UCB underwent a strategic executive leadership rotation effective January 1, 2026, and the long-serving CMO (Prof. Iris Loew-Friedrich) retired in mid-2024. The CMO role was not directly replaced; responsibilities were absorbed into the restructured "Patient Evidence" EVP role.

- **Title:** Executive Vice President, Head of Patient Evidence
- **Function:** Clinical Development / Regulatory Affairs / Medical Affairs / Market Access / Benefit-Risk Governance
- **Geography:** Brussels, Belgium
- **Seniority Tier:** Tier 1 -- Budget holder / Executive authority

At the H2 2025 earnings call (February 2026), Caeymaex discussed bepranemab (Alzheimer's) B-R risk mitigation strategies, stating UCB is "focused on unlocking and addressing critical path questions of CMC and regulatory nature." Primary decision-maker for clinical development and regulatory B-R assessments.

## Key Organizational Insight

UCB underwent a significant governance transformation in 2024-2026. The retirement of long-serving CMO Prof. Iris Loew-Friedrich (who led development, regulatory, medical affairs, safety/PV, and data strategy for 20+ years) in mid-2024 was followed by a restructuring that created the "Patient Evidence" EVP role -- consolidating clinical development, regulatory, medical affairs, and market access under a single executive. The January 2026 strategic rotation swapped Emmanuel Caeymaex (now Patient Evidence) and Fiona du Monceau (now Chief Commercial Officer). This means the Patient Evidence role is now held by an executive with deep commercial experience, which may create stronger emphasis on evidence that serves both regulatory and market objectives. UCB's pharmacovigilance function (internally called "Medical Safety and Pharmacovigilance") falls within the Patient Evidence organization but the VP-level safety leadership is not publicly disclosed. The simultaneous launch of five growth drivers (Bimzelx, Evenity, Fintepla, Rystiggo, Zilbrysq) across multiple indications and geographies creates an acute need for dynamic, multi-product B-R monitoring.
