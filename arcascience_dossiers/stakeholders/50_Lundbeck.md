## Key Organizational Insight

Lundbeck is the only fully integrated pharmaceutical company exclusively focused on brain diseases — every pipeline asset, every commercial product, and every employee operates within the CNS domain. This creates deep domain expertise but also means ArcaScience must demonstrate CNS-specific B-R assessment value (generic oncology/immunology use cases are irrelevant). R&D functions — including regulatory affairs, pharmacovigilance, and clinical development — all report into Johan Luthman's EVP R&D organization. The $2.6B Longboard acquisition (bexicaserin for rare epilepsy with Breakthrough Therapy Designation) is the single most important pipeline investment, targeting global peak sales of $1.5-2.0B. Lundbeck's lean Executive Leadership Team (6 members), Foundation ownership (~70%), and 20% R&D reinvestment create an organization that makes disciplined, long-term investment decisions. The "Focused Innovator Strategy" creates organizational receptivity to tools that sharpen innovation focus. Record 2025 financial performance provides investment capacity.

**Priority Contacts for ArcaScience:**
1. Johan Luthman (EVP, Head of R&D) — all B-R functions report to him; pipeline authority
2. Ona Kinduryte Schorling (VP, Global Medical Safety) — direct operational leader for safety/B-R
3. Doug Williamson (CMO, VP US Medical) — U.S. medical governance and evidence strategy
4. Thomas Gibbs (EVP, Head of U.S.) — largest market authority, bexicaserin launch preparation
5. Charl van Zyl (President & CEO) — strategic investment authority for enterprise tools
