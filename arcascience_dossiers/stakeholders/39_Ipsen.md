## Key Organizational Insight

Ipsen organizes its medical and safety functions under the **Chief Medical Officer** (Sandra Silvestri), whose portfolio explicitly includes **Patient Safety** alongside Global Medical Affairs and Patient Affairs. This is a distinctive organizational choice — many pharma companies separate the CMO from the safety/PV function. At Ipsen, the CMO has direct line authority over patient safety, making Silvestri the single most important contact for benefit-risk discussions.

The **SVP, Head of Global Patient Safety** role — which reports into the CMO's organization — appears to be **currently vacant** (active job posting as of early 2026). This vacancy suggests Ipsen is in a period of transition for its safety leadership, which could create openness to new safety and benefit-risk tools and platforms.

Ipsen's pipeline includes multiple Breakthrough Therapy Designation candidates (e.g., IPN60340 in AML) and marketed products across oncology (Onivyde, cabozantinib franchise), rare disease (Somatuline, Increlex), and neuroscience (Dysport). The complexity and therapeutic breadth of this portfolio drives substantial benefit-risk assessment needs across development and lifecycle management.
