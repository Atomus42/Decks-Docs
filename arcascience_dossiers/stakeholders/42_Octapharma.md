# Octapharma — Stakeholder Intelligence

> Research Date: March 2026
> Methodology: Public sources only (corporate websites, press releases, conference listings, annual reports)
> Note: Octapharma is a privately held, family-owned Swiss company (founded 1983 by Wolfgang Marguerre). Leadership disclosure is more limited than at publicly traded companies. The company does not publicly designate a Chief Medical Officer; R&D and clinical development are led by board-level executives.

## Key Organizational Insight

Octapharma's family-owned, private structure means decision-making is concentrated among a small management board chaired by founder Wolfgang Marguerre. The company does not publicly designate a Chief Medical Officer -- R&D leadership is held at the board level by Wolfgang Frenzel, with Sigurd Knaub leading clinical R&D operations in haematology. Quality and compliance (including drug safety oversight) sits with Josef Weinberger at the board level. For plasma-derived products, benefit-risk assessment uniquely integrates manufacturing quality data (viral clearance, protein integrity, lot consistency) with clinical efficacy and safety data -- making the quality/compliance function as important as clinical development for B-R governance. The company's AI-powered Advanced Analytics initiative (focused on production optimization) provides a natural alignment point for AI-driven B-R platforms.
