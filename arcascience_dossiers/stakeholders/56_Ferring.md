## Identified Leaders (Publicly Verified)

### 1. Tamara Vukmirovic
- **Title:** Executive Vice President, Head of Clinical & Pharmaceutical Development
- **Function:** Clinical Development / Pharmaceutical Development
- **Geography:** Saint-Prex, Switzerland (Global HQ)
- **Seniority Tier:** 1 (Executive Committee member)
- **Source:** https://www.ferring.com/ferring-announces-new-ambition-enterprise-model-and-executive-committee-composition-to-accelerate-growth-and-innovation/
- **Strategic Relevance:** Appointed to the Executive Committee in September 2025, Vukmirovic is responsible for Global Project and Portfolio Management, Global Clinical Delivery, Global Biometrics, Global Pharmaceutical Development/CMC, Early Sciences, Clinical Quality Assurance, and Global Life Cycle Management. As the leader of Ferring's clinical and pharmaceutical development organization, she is the primary decision-maker for tools that support clinical trial execution and benefit-risk assessment across the reproductive medicine, uro-oncology, and microbiome portfolios. A Ferring veteran since 2005 with deep regulatory and CMC expertise.

### 2. Dr. Michael Rosenblatt
- **Title:** Board Member; Chair, Science, Medicine and Development Council
- **Function:** Benefit-Risk Governance / Clinical Development Oversight
- **Geography:** United States (Board-level role)
- **Seniority Tier:** 1 (Board-level executive authority)
- **Source:** https://www.ferring.com/dr-michael-rosenblatt-joins-ferring-pharmaceuticals-board-of-directors/
- **Strategic Relevance:** Former CMO of Merck & Co. and former Dean of Tufts University School of Medicine, Dr. Rosenblatt chairs the newly formed Science, Medicine and Development Council, which provides advice and oversight for clinical development, medical affairs, regulatory, and pharmacovigilance under the Board's authority. This council is the central B/R governance body at Ferring. His Harvard Medical School and NIH pedigree, combined with 180+ peer-reviewed publications and 19 patents, make him a high-credibility champion for evidence-based B/R platforms.

### 3. Olga Tarasova, MD, MBA
- **Title:** Chief Medical Officer, US
- **Function:** Medical Affairs (US)
- **Geography:** Parsippany, New Jersey, USA
- **Seniority Tier:** 2 (US-level CMO; influential but not global Executive Committee)
- **Source:** https://ferringusa.com/?team=olga-tarasova-md-mba
- **Strategic Relevance:** Leads the Medical Affairs organization in the United States with 25+ years of pharmaceutical industry experience across Abbott, BMS, Sanofi, Teva, and Pfizer. She has been quoted in 2025 press releases on follitropin delta data (ASRM 2025) and uro-oncology grants (SUO NMIBC). As the US CMO, she oversees medical evidence generation for Adstiladrin, Rebyota, and the reproductive medicine portfolio — all products where real-world B/R data is critical for market access.
