> Research Date: March 2026
> Methodology: Public sources only (corporate websites, press releases, conference listings, business databases)

## Key Organizational Insight

Eisai's organizational structure is built around its unique "Deep Human Biology Learning" (DHBL) framework, which unifies drug discovery and development across therapeutic areas. The company operates with dual therapeutic focus areas — Neurology Business Group and Oncology Business Group — each with dedicated clinical development and medical affairs leadership. The Chief Clinical Officer (Kramer) oversees the full product creation cycle across both areas. Eisai's Global Safety Board is the central pharmacovigilance governance body, composed of Regional Safety Officers and General Safety Officers. The complexity of Leqembi's safety profile (amyloid-related imaging abnormalities, or ARIA) makes pharmacovigilance and benefit-risk assessment a particularly high-priority function at Eisai.
