## Identified Leaders (Publicly Verified)

### 1. Lydia Abad-Franch, MD, MBA
- **Title:** Senior Vice President, Chief Medical Officer, Head of R&D and Medical Affairs
- **Function:** Clinical Development / Medical Affairs / R&D / Pharmacovigilance (Global)
- **Geography:** Basel, Switzerland
- **Seniority Tier:** 1 (Executive Committee member)
- **Source:** https://www.sobi.com/en/press-releases/sobi-appoints-lydia-abad-franch-head-rd-and-chief-medical-officer-2162906
- **Strategic Relevance:** Appointed permanently as CMO and Head of RDMA in October 2023 after serving as acting CMO since June 2023. She oversees an R&D organization of approximately 220 colleagues spanning clinical science, clinical operations, medical writing, regulatory affairs, pharmacovigilance, and portfolio management across Stockholm, Basel, Durham (NC), and Tokyo. Her career trajectory through Novo Nordisk, Baxter-Baxalta-Shire-Takeda in hematology gives her deep rare disease experience. She presented alongside the CEO at the $950M Arthrosi acquisition announcement (December 2025) and represented Sobi at the Nordic Rare Disease Summit 2025 and WFH 2025 Humanitarian Aid Summit. As the single leader overseeing all of R&D, medical affairs, regulatory, and PV, she is the primary decision-maker for a benefit-risk platform at Sobi.

### 2. Daniel Rankin, PhD
- **Title:** Head of Strategy & Corporate Development, Member of the Executive Committee
- **Function:** Strategy / Business Development / M&A
- **Geography:** Zurich, Switzerland
- **Seniority Tier:** 1 (Executive Committee member)
- **Source:** https://www.sobi.com/en/executive-committee
- **Strategic Relevance:** Leads all corporate development, business development, and strategy activities for Sobi. His role is central to Sobi's acquisition-driven growth model — he led the evaluation and execution of the $950M Arthrosi Therapeutics acquisition for gout and has overseen the portfolio strategy that led to the Sanofi, Apellis, and other partnerships. PhD in biology (University of Helsinki) with prior McKinsey consulting experience, currently holding 8,500 Sobi shares. B/R evaluation of acquisition targets is a core part of his mandate. A BRA platform positioned as a due diligence tool would directly serve his deal-evaluation function, making him a natural strategic champion.

### 3. Duane H. Barnes
- **Title:** President, Head of North America; Member of the Executive Committee
- **Function:** General Management (North America)
- **Geography:** Boston, Massachusetts, USA
- **Seniority Tier:** 1 (Executive Committee member; US subsidiary President)
- **Source:** https://www.sobi.com/usa/en/who-we-are/leadership
- **Strategic Relevance:** Leads all strategy and business operations for Sobi North America, the company's key commercial market. 30+ years of executive experience including President, Head of US Operations at UCB Biopharma, and prior roles at Amgen, Prime Therapeutics, and Aetna. His leadership has helped grow Sobi's US business more than twentyfold over the past decade. As the US market head, he oversees the commercial and medical affairs functions where benefit-risk evidence drives market access, payer engagement, and competitive differentiation for products like ALTUVOCT and Gamifant. MBA and MS from Indiana University Kelley School of Business.

### 4. Michael Vredenburg, PhD
- **Title:** Vice President, Medical Affairs — Hematology and Oncology (North America)
- **Function:** Medical Affairs (US Hematology & Oncology)
- **Geography:** United States
- **Seniority Tier:** 2 (VP-level; US Medical Affairs functional leader)
- **Source:** https://www.sobi.com/usa/en/who-we-are/leadership
- **Strategic Relevance:** Leads medical affairs strategy and execution for Sobi's hematology and oncology franchise in North America, including ALTUVOCT (efanesoctocog alfa), Elocta, Alprolix, and Doptelet. He also serves as Head of North America Medical Information and Pharmacovigilance for seven products, giving him direct operational responsibility for PV in the US market. He joined Sobi through the Dova Pharmaceuticals acquisition in 2021, where he served as VP and Head of Medical Affairs. His combined medical affairs and PV responsibility makes him a natural user and advocate for a benefit-risk platform that integrates safety and medical evidence data.

### 5. Laura Saltonstall, MD
- **Title:** Vice President, Medical Affairs — Immunology (North America)
- **Function:** Medical Affairs (US Immunology)
- **Geography:** United States
- **Seniority Tier:** 2 (VP-level; US Medical Affairs functional leader)
- **Source:** https://www.sobi.com/usa/en/who-we-are/leadership
- **Strategic Relevance:** Leads medical affairs for Sobi's immunology franchise in North America, including Gamifant (emapalumab) for primary HLH — a product requiring vigilant safety monitoring in a critically ill, often pediatric population. Immunology is one of Sobi's three strategic pillars and includes the complement pathway program (pegcetacoplan for C3G/IC-MPGN with Apellis). As an MD leading immunology medical affairs, she is directly involved in benefit-risk evidence generation and KOL engagement for rare immunological conditions where B/R profiles are the primary differentiator.

### 6. Olivia Maurel, PharmD
- **Title:** Head of Global Regulatory Affairs
- **Function:** Regulatory Affairs (Global)
- **Geography:** Zurich, Switzerland
- **Seniority Tier:** 2 (Senior functional leader; global RA head)
- **Source:** https://rocketreach.co/olivia-maurel-email_13978526
- **Strategic Relevance:** Leads global regulatory affairs for Sobi, managing filings across EMA, FDA, and other markets with partner coordination (Sanofi, Apellis). Her experience at Takeda and Shire provides deep rare disease regulatory expertise. She holds a Doctorate in Pharmacy from the University of Montpellier. Sobi's complex regulatory landscape — with territorial divisions from the Sanofi partnership (Sobi in Europe/MENA, Sanofi in North America for hemophilia) and multi-partner filings — creates significant demand for systematic B/R data management. She oversees the regulatory strategy for both established products and pipeline filings including pegcetacoplan (C3G) and pegadricase (gout).
