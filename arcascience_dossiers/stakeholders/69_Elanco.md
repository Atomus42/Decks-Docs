## Identified Leaders (Publicly Verified)

### 1. Ellen de Brabander, Ph.D.
- **Title:** Executive Vice President, Research, Development and Regulatory Affairs
- **Function:** R&D / Regulatory Affairs (Global)
- **Geography:** Greenfield, Indiana, USA
- **Seniority Tier:** 1 (Executive)
- **Source:** https://www.elanco.com/us/leadership/executive-committee/ellen-de-brabander
- **Strategic Relevance:** Joined Elanco in October 2021, responsible for the entire global R&D organization and regulatory affairs. Previously SVP R&D at PepsiCo and led R&D organizations at Merial (now Boehringer Ingelheim Animal Health) and Intervet (now Merck Animal Health). Developed the blockbuster parasiticide NexGard at Merial. She oversees 100+ active R&D programs, the monoclonal antibody platform (Zenrelia, Befrena), and the Elanco Innovation Laboratories at the Indiana headquarters. As the single executive accountable for both R&D and regulatory affairs, she is the primary decision-maker for any B/R analytics platform adoption across the portfolio.

### 2. Jeffrey N. Simmons
- **Title:** President and Chief Executive Officer
- **Function:** Corporate Leadership
- **Geography:** Greenfield, Indiana, USA
- **Seniority Tier:** 1 (Executive)
- **Source:** https://www.elanco.com/en-us/about-us/our-people
- **Strategic Relevance:** CEO since 2018, prior to which he served as President of the animal health division since 2008. Champions the innovation strategy and Elanco Ascend restructuring, including the $400M total U.S. manufacturing/R&D investment and closure of the German R&D facility. The Zenrelia boxed warning is a visible strategic challenge under his leadership, and the mAb platform expansion (Befrena, future candidates) makes B/R optimization a C-suite priority. Sets the strategic direction for innovation infrastructure investments.
