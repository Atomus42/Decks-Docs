## Key Organizational Insight

Jazz Pharmaceuticals organizes its safety and regulatory functions under a combined **Global Regulatory Affairs & Drug Safety (GRADS)** organization, which sits within the broader R&D function led by CMO Robert Iannone. This integrated structure means regulatory strategy and drug safety/pharmacovigilance are managed in close coordination. Jazz's therapeutic focus on neuroscience (narcolepsy, epilepsy) and oncology (hematologic malignancies, solid tumors) involves complex benefit-risk profiles, particularly for controlled substance products (Xywav, Xyrem) and oncology agents (Rylaze, Zepzelca). The company's ~$4.3B revenue base and 50+ country commercial footprint create significant demand for structured benefit-risk assessment capabilities.
