# Sandoz -- Stakeholder Intelligence

> Research Date: March 2026
> Methodology: Public sources only (corporate website, press releases, annual reports, conference listings, news articles)
> Note: Sandoz spun off from Novartis in October 2023 and is building independent organizational infrastructure. The company does not have a traditional "CMO" at the global Executive Committee level; medical and scientific functions report through the Chief Scientific Officer. Pharmacovigilance is led by an SVP-level Global Head of Patient Safety. Regulatory affairs leadership below the CSO level was not fully identifiable in public sources at the VP+ level for the global function.

---

## Identified Leaders (Publicly Verified)

### 1. Claire D'Abreu-Hayling
- **Title:** Chief Scientific Officer (Member, Sandoz Executive Committee)
- **Function:** Global Product Development / Biosimilar & Generics Pipeline / Clinical Development
- **Geography:** Basel, Switzerland (global scope)
- **Seniority Tier:** Tier 1 -- Executive decision authority
- **Source:** https://www.sandoz.com/people-culture/sandoz-leadership/claire-dabreu-hayling/ ; https://www.emjreviews.com/emj-gold/podcasts/sandozs-claire-dabreu-hayling-on-leading-with-love/ ; https://www.statnews.com/2025/12/05/biosimilar-medicines-regulatory-reform-sandoz/
- **Strategic Relevance:** As CSO and Executive Committee member, D'Abreu-Hayling oversees the global product development network for both small molecules and biosimilars, managing infrastructure strategy, pipeline execution, and talent acquisition. Over 30 years of pharmaceutical industry experience. She has been vocal on regulatory reform, arguing that "extensive biosimilar comparative efficacy trials add little scientific value while increasing costs and delaying patient access." Her function encompasses clinical development oversight for the 28-molecule biosimilar pipeline including the strategically critical pembrolizumab and nivolumab programs. The primary decision-maker for development-side B-R assessment tools at Sandoz.

### 2. Shaloo Pandhi, M.D.
- **Title:** Senior Vice President, Global Head of Patient Safety
- **Function:** Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety / Patient Safety
- **Geography:** Basel, Switzerland (global scope)
- **Seniority Tier:** Tier 1 -- Executive decision authority
- **Source:** https://theorg.com/org/sandoz/org-chart/shaloo-pandhi ; https://www.pharma-iq.com/contributor/shaloo-pandhi
- **Strategic Relevance:** Leads global patient safety for a portfolio serving 800M+ patient treatments annually across approximately 1,500 products. In role since August 2021, previously held patient safety, clinical development, and pharmacovigilance leadership roles at Novartis (2007-2022). Led one of the largest post-approval biosimilar pharmacovigilance reviews to date (covering 8 Sandoz biosimilars with 1.3 billion patient-days of exposure), confirming comparable safety with reference biologics. Has delivered keynotes on safety digitization, indicating openness to technology-enabled PV solutions. Her team's work directly validates biosimilar benefit-risk profiles -- a core use case for ArcaScience's platform.
