# Emergent BioSolutions -- Stakeholder Intelligence

> Research Date: March 2026
> Methodology: Public sources only (corporate website, press releases, SEC filings, news articles)
> Note: Emergent is in the midst of a multi-year turnaround under CEO Joseph Papa (appointed Feb 2024). The CMO role was filled in November 2024 with Dr. Simon Lowry, who oversees regulatory affairs, medical affairs, clinical/biostatistics, and patient safety. The former SVP of Global Regulatory Affairs (Manish Vyas) departed in 2024, and the company has been recruiting for a Head of Patient Safety role, indicating organizational gaps in PV leadership. VP+ roles with global scope in the target functions are limited at this company.

## Organizational Gaps Noted

- **VP/SVP Regulatory Affairs:** Manish Vyas (former SVP, Global Head of Regulatory Affairs, 2016-2024) departed in 2024. The function is now consolidated under CMO Simon Lowry. No publicly announced VP-level replacement has been identified.
- **VP/SVP Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety:** The company has been actively recruiting for a "Head of Patient Safety" role. The most senior publicly identified PV professional is at the Director level (Matthew Garza, Director of Pharmacovigilance). This function also reports up to CMO Simon Lowry.
- **Clinical Operations:** No separately identified VP of Clinical Operations in public sources; this function is consolidated under the CMO.
