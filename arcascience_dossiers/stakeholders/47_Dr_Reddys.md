## Key Organizational Insight

Dr. Reddy's is undergoing a significant transformation from a pure generics company to a diversified pharma enterprise with biosimilars, proprietary products (via Aurigene Oncology subsidiary), and digital health. The biosimilar portfolio is the highest-growth and most clinically complex segment: abatacept (first interchangeable biosimilar BLA accepted by FDA), denosumab (AVT03 BLA accepted by FDA), pembrolizumab (co-development with Alvotech), rituximab, trastuzumab, tocilizumab, and bevacizumab. Jayanth Sridhar, the former Global Head of Biologics, resigned effective January 31, 2026 — no public successor has been announced, creating a leadership gap in the highest-priority business segment for ArcaScience engagement. The combined Quality & Pharmacovigilance function under Krishna Venkatesh (Management Council member) reflects the company's integrated approach to product quality and safety.
