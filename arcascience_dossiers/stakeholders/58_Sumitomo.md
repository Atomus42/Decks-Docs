- **Function:** Clinical Development / R&D (Global and US)
- **Geography:** Boston, Massachusetts, USA / Osaka, Japan (dual scope)

- **Title:** Chief Medical Officer — Oncology, Sumitomo Pharma America
- **Function:** Clinical Development / Medical Affairs (Oncology)
- **Geography:** United States
- **Seniority Tier:** 1 (C-suite at SMPA; leads oncology development, Sumitomo's priority pipeline area)
- **Source:** https://www.us.sumitomo-pharma.com/about-us/

Board-certified in hematology and oncology with extensive clinical trial PI experience.
