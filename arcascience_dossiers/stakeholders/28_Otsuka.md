## Key Organizational Insight

Otsuka's U.S. pharmaceutical operations are run through OPDC (development & commercialization) and OAPI (commercial), with the CMO (John Kraus) and SVP of Global R&D Evidence (Mary Hobart) serving as the primary clinical and regulatory decision-makers. The Lundbeck alliance is central to CNS product governance -- Otsuka and Lundbeck co-develop and co-commercialize core CNS products (Abilify Maintena, Rexulti), meaning B-R decisions for these assets involve joint steering committees. The nephrology pipeline (Jynarque, sibeprenlimab) is fully under Otsuka's control and represents the primary near-term growth opportunity. The corporate leadership transition at Otsuka Holdings (Makoto Inoue became President & CEO in January 2025, succeeding Tatsuo Higuchi) signals strategic continuity but new leadership dynamics. Note: The former SVP & Chief Global Pharmacovigilance Officer (Dr. Mirza I. Rahman) departed Otsuka for Organon; the current global PV leadership in the U.S. was not publicly identified at the time of this research.
