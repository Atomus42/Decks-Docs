## Key Organizational Insight

Moderna is in a period of significant leadership transition. The CMO role (held by Jacqueline Miller from November 2024 through March 2, 2026) is now vacant, and it is unclear whether a direct successor will be named or whether the Chief Development Officer (David Berman) will absorb CMO-like responsibilities. President Stephen Hoge serves as the integrating executive across R&D, medical affairs, and commercial. The company's platform-centric approach means cross-program safety learnings about mRNA technology are particularly important for B-R assessment -- all programs share core mRNA technology, creating shared safety signals. Moderna's pharmacovigilance function has scaled enormously due to billions of Spikevax doses administered globally, but the specific VP-level PV leadership is not publicly disclosed. The company's 69% mid/late-stage success rate (vs. 19% industry average) suggests strong internal B-R governance, which could be positioned as a foundation that ArcaScience enhances. The aggressive cost reduction program creates both an obstacle (spending scrutiny) and an opportunity (tools that reduce clinical attrition save money). The February 2026 FDA refusal-to-file for the flu vaccine and broader regulatory uncertainty add urgency to regulatory strategy tools.
