## Identified Leaders (Publicly Verified)

### 1. Peter Sallstig, MD, MBA
- **Title:** Global Chief Medical Officer; Head of Product Development Division; President and CEO, Santen Inc. (USA)
- **Function:** Clinical Development / Medical Affairs / R&D (Global)
- **Geography:** Emeryville, California, USA (oversees Product Development Division globally)
- **Seniority Tier:** 1 (Corporate Officer; heads global product development and serves as US CEO)
- **Source:** https://www.santen.com/en/about/leadership/sallstig
- **Strategic Relevance:** Sallstig holds a triple role as Global CMO, Head of Product Development Division, and President/CEO of Santen Inc. (USA), making him the single most important decision-maker for clinical development tools at Santen. He joined Santen in 2018 and was appointed CMO in April 2022, bringing 20+ years of pharmaceutical industry experience from Novartis, BMS, Alcon, and Insmed. In 2025, he led landmark approvals including SETANEO (sepetaprost) for glaucoma in Japan (August 2025) and the NDA filing for netarsudil mesylate (July 2025). His combined medical, development, and business leadership make him the primary target for a B/R platform that integrates clinical and regulatory data across Santen's pure-play ophthalmology portfolio.
