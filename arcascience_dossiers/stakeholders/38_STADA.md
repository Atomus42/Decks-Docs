## Key Organizational Insight

STADA Arzneimittel operates a different organizational model than traditional R&D-driven pharmaceutical companies. As a generics, specialty pharma, and consumer healthcare company, STADA does not maintain a traditional Chief Medical Officer or Chief Safety Officer role at the executive level. Instead:

- **Development, Regulatory, and Portfolio** functions are consolidated under Yann Brun (EVP, Head of Global Development, Portfolio, Regulatory and BD&L).
- **Quality and pharmacovigilance quality systems** fall under Miguel Pagan Fernandez (CTO) through the Global Quality function.
- **Drug safety officer** roles and local pharmacovigilance units report through medical affairs teams at the country level, with global coordination from headquarters.
- The **STADA Executive Committee** does not publicly list a dedicated VP/Head of Global Pharmacovigilance or Drug Safety by name.

STADA's expansion into specialty pharma (biosimilars in oncology, immunology; specialty products in neurology and nephrology) is creating increased complexity in benefit-risk assessment needs. The company's six marketed biosimilars and growing specialty portfolio require robust safety monitoring and benefit-risk frameworks, particularly as STADA enters more complex therapeutic areas.

The forthcoming IPO (Andreas Fibig designated as independent Board Chairman) will increase scrutiny on product quality, safety, and governance processes — creating potential demand for enterprise-grade benefit-risk assessment capabilities.
