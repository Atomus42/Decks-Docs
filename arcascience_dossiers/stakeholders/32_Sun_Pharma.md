## Key Organizational Insight

Sun Pharma is structured with a Managing Director (Ganorkar) overseeing all functions, supported by a COO (Aalok Shanghvi) and regional CEOs. The company's specialty portfolio (dermatology, ophthalmology, onco-dermatology) accounts for over 18% of sales and is the primary growth engine. Medical affairs and clinical development functions appear to sit below the C-suite level; a VP Head of Medical Affairs position reporting to the CEO was posted as open, suggesting this function is being rebuilt or restructured. The former CMO (Simon Lowry) departed to Emergent BioSolutions. Sun Pharma Advanced Research Company (SPARC) operates as a separate clinical-stage entity for innovative pipeline assets. The company's pharmacovigilance and regulatory affairs leadership at the VP+ level is not prominently disclosed in public sources, which is typical of Indian-headquartered generics companies with centralized governance.
