## Identified Leaders (Publicly Verified)

### 1. Ugo Di Francesco
- **Title:** Group Chief Executive Officer
- **Function:** Executive Leadership
- **Geography:** Barga (Tuscany), Italy
- **Seniority Tier:** Tier 1 -- Executive decision-maker
- **Source:** https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/permira-funds-and-the-marcucci-family-complete-investment-in-kedrion-and-bpl-and-announce-appointment-of-ugo-di-francesco-as-ceo-301616440.html
- **Strategic Relevance:** Di Francesco was appointed CEO in January 2023 following the Permira/Marcucci family investment and Kedrion-BPL combination. His growth-oriented leadership style and PE-backed mandate suggest receptivity to technology investments that support portfolio expansion and operational scaling across 100+ country markets.

- **Title:** Chief Clinical and Regulatory Affairs Officer (also referenced as Chief Medical and Regulatory Affairs Officer)
- **Function:** Clinical Development / Regulatory Affairs
- **Geography:** Italy (headquarters-based)
- **Seniority Tier:** Tier 1 -- Executive decision-maker
- **Source:** https://theorg.com/org/kedrion-biopharma/org-chart/paloma-de-miguel

Her experience navigating FDA, EMA, and multi-jurisdictional filings across 100+ countries for plasma products makes her the ideal evaluator of B/R analytics that support complex global filing strategies, including the Yimmugo launch and aceruloplasminemia orphan drug program.
