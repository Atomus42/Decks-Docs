## Key Organizational Insight

Shionogi's April 2024 reorganization created a Drug Discovery Research Division and a Pharmaceutical Technology Research Division, with the Drug Development and Regulatory Science Division under Takeki Uehara driving clinical and regulatory execution. The company is at an inflection point: the December 2025 appointment of Ann Howell as Global Head of Regulatory Affairs and the $375M BARDA partnership (January 2025) signal accelerating U.S. development ambitions. Shionogi maintains an unusually high in-house drug discovery ratio (70%+ vs. 20-30% industry average), meaning internal B-R decisions are especially consequential. The cefiderocol PROVE study (~1,000 patients RWE) and ensitrelvir NDA for COVID-19 prevention (accepted September 2025) create immediate demand for integrated B-R evidence. AMR products face unique B-R challenges — regulatory agencies increasingly require benefit-risk frameworks that account for population-level public health benefit alongside individual patient-level safety. The HIV franchise (ViiV Healthcare/GSK, 26.6% royalty increase) provides financial stability that supports R&D investment.
