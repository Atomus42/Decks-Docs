## Identified Leaders (Publicly Verified)

### 1. Hafrun Fridriksdottir
- **Title:** President, US and Global Head of R&D
- **Function:** R&D Leadership / Clinical Development
- **Geography:** United States (global scope)
- **Seniority Tier:** 1 (Executive)
- **Source:** https://www.hikma.com/who-we-are/leadership/hafrun-fridriksdottir/
- **Strategic Relevance:** Oversees all of Hikma's R&D globally, including the biosimilar pipeline (STARJEMZA, denosumab biosimilars, liraglutide), complex generics, and the ~15-product RTU pipeline. As the senior-most executive directly responsible for clinical development and regulatory filings, she would be the key champion for any benefit-risk assessment platform integrated into R&D workflows. Her dual US and global scope makes her the gateway to both FDA and multi-jurisdictional regulatory strategy.

### 2. Said Darwazah

- **Function:** Corporate Leadership
- **Geography:** London, UK / Amman, Jordan (global)
- **Seniority Tier:** 1 (Executive)
- **Source:** https://www.hikma.com/news/hikma-delivers-group-revenue-and-profit-growth-and-announces-share-buyback-and-leadership-changes/

As budget authority for enterprise-level investments, his sponsorship would be essential for any large-scale platform adoption.
