# Servier — Stakeholder Intelligence

> Research Date: March 2026
> Methodology: Public sources only (corporate websites, press releases, conference listings, business databases)
> Note: Servier is an independent international pharmaceutical group headquartered in Suresnes, France, governed by a non-profit Foundation. World leader in cardiology with a strategic pivot to oncology (>70% of R&D budget). Revenue ~5.3B EUR. Unique governance: no shareholders, all profits reinvested. U.S. subsidiary (Servier Pharmaceuticals, Boston) is now the Group's largest entity.

## Key Organizational Insight

Servier is governed by a non-profit Foundation with all profits reinvested into R&D, enabling a long-term vision unusual in the pharmaceutical industry. The 12-member Executive Committee, chaired by President Olivier Laureau, steers the Group's strategy. Medical affairs, patient affairs, regulatory affairs, and pharmacovigilance are unified under the EVP Global Medical & Patient Affairs (Lallouette), while R&D and clinical development fall under the EVP R&D / CSO (Bertrand). The U.S. subsidiary is now Servier's largest entity, focused on oncology. VP-level heads of pharmacovigilance and regulatory affairs sit below the ExCom level and are not prominently disclosed in public sources; regulatory affairs reports through the Global Medical & Patient Affairs function. Servier allocates >70% of its R&D budget to oncology with a focus on hard-to-treat cancers.
