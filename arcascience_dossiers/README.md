# ArcaScience Account Intelligence Dossiers

## Overview
This directory contains strategic account intelligence dossiers for the Top 100 global pharmaceutical companies, tailored for ArcaScience's AI-powered Benefit-Risk Assessment (BRA) platform sales targeting.

## Contents

### /companies/
100 individual company dossiers (one per file), each containing:
1. **Company Overview** — Revenue, HQ, therapeutic focus, pipeline, strategic priorities
2. **Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping** — Structure of clinical development, regulatory affairs, pharmacovigilance, and benefit-risk governance
3. **Target Role Archetypes** — Tiered role titles to target (no personal data)
4. **Buying Pathway Hypothesis** — Who initiates, validates, and owns budget
5. **Strategic Entry Angle** — Tailored ArcaScience approach recommendation

### COMPANY_LIST.md
Master ranked list of all 100 companies with revenue data

### master_targeting_table.csv
Consolidated CSV with key targeting data across all 100 companies

## Methodology
- Companies ranked by approximate FY2024 revenue
- Data sourced from Pharm Exec Top 50, ChemAnalyst Top 100, Fierce Pharma Top 20, individual company reports
- Organizational mapping based on publicly available information (annual reports, job postings, press releases)
- No personal contact data collected — role-based targeting only

## Important Notes
- No personal emails or contact data are included
- All information is from publicly available sources
- Role archetypes are based on typical pharma org structures; actual titles may vary
- Revenue figures are approximate and may vary by source/methodology
- Last updated: March 2026

## About ArcaScience BRA Platform
ArcaScience develops an AI-powered dynamic Benefit-Risk Assessment platform that:
- Continuously updates drug benefit-risk profiles
- Integrates clinical trial and real-world evidence
- Supports regulatory strategy decisions
- Reduces clinical attrition risk
- Enables probabilistic approval prediction
