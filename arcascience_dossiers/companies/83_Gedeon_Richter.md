# Gedeon Richter

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** EUR 2.14B (HUF 845B pharma revenues; 13% YoY growth; all business units achieved double-digit growth)
- **Headquarters:** Budapest, Hungary
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** CNS/neuropsychiatry (cariprazine/Vraylar/Reagila), women's healthcare (EVRA, Drovelis, Ryeqo, Lenzetto; contraception, fertility, endometriosis, menopause), biosimilars (teriparatide, FSH, denosumab, ustekinumab, tocilizumab), general medicines (cardiovascular, NOACs)
- **Pipeline Size:** Preclinical CNS original research (2 new projects initiated in 2024, AbbVie discovery collaboration); WHC original research hub in Belgium; biosimilar pipeline with denosumab MAA accepted by EMA, ustekinumab licensed from Bio-Thera, tocilizumab in Phase 1; largest R&D unit in Central Eastern Europe
- **Strategic Priorities:** Maximize cariprazine value before LOE (late 2020s); build next-generation CNS blockbuster pipeline (AbbVie collaboration); double WHC and GenMed revenues by 2035; establish as reliable biosimilar player with CDMO; new 2025-2035 strategy balancing "Innovative" and "Affordable" pillars

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Gedeon Richter organizes clinical development across four business units: CNS, Women's Healthcare, Biosimilars/Biotech, and General Medicines. The company operates the largest R&D unit in Central Eastern Europe, with CNS original research as its historical core strength. Clinical development spans preclinical drug discovery (CNS, WHC), biosimilar development (analytical, PK, clinical comparability), and lifecycle management. The AbbVie collaboration in neuropsychiatry adds co-development activity. A new WHC original research hub in Belgium extends R&D beyond Hungarian headquarters.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs manages filings across EU (EMA), with the denosumab biosimilar MAA acceptance by EMA being a key 2024 milestone (first biosimilar mAb in the portfolio). The team supports multi-country filings for WHC products, CNS products (Reagila marketed via partners across Europe), and biosimilars. Regulatory expertise spans both innovative drug and biosimilar pathways, with expanding geographic reach including China, Latin America, and Australia.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV covers a diverse marketed portfolio spanning CNS (cariprazine global safety monitoring in collaboration with AbbVie for Vraylar and with partners for Reagila), WHC products (hormonal contraceptives, fertility drugs), biosimilars, and general medicines. The PV organization must manage safety data from products marketed through its own network and through numerous partner organizations globally.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance operates at the business unit level (CNS, WHC, Biosimilars, GenMed) with strategic portfolio decisions elevated to corporate leadership. The 2025-2035 strategy requires careful B/R-driven resource allocation between innovative (CNS discovery, WHC original research) and affordable (biosimilars, GenMed) pillars. Cariprazine LOE planning demands sophisticated B/R assessment of follow-on CNS pipeline candidates.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
Gedeon Richter faces an existential strategic challenge: replacing the cariprazine franchise (generating ~USD 2.7B for AbbVie as Vraylar) before its LOE window. The AbbVie discovery collaboration and preclinical CNS pipeline must yield a new blockbuster candidate. ArcaScience should position the BRA platform as a critical decision support tool for: (1) CNS pipeline candidate selection, where probabilistic B/R modeling at the preclinical-to-clinical transition can identify the candidate with the highest probability of blockbuster success, (2) the AbbVie collaboration interface, where structured B/R assessment provides a common framework for co-development decisions, and (3) the biosimilar pipeline (denosumab, ustekinumab, tocilizumab), where B/R profiling supports totality-of-evidence regulatory strategy. The urgency of the cariprazine LOE creates a natural time pressure that favors adoption. A pilot focused on the CNS preclinical-to-clinical transition would demonstrate the highest strategic value.
