## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Gilead's R&D organization is structured across three therapeutic pillars: virology, oncology, and inflammation. The company appointed Dietmar Berger, MD, PhD (formerly CMO and Global Head of Development at Sanofi) as Chief Medical Officer on January 2, 2025, signaling a new era of clinical development leadership focused on portfolio diversification. Berger has stated he is building a "balanced" and "partially derisked portfolio" combining direct cytotoxic mechanisms with immuno-oncology. The Kite Pharma division (acquired 2017 for $11.9B) operates with significant autonomy in CAR T cell therapy development, and in February 2026, Gilead announced the $7.8B acquisition of Arcellx to gain full control of anito-cel (anitocabtagene autoleucel), its lead BCMA-directed CAR T for multiple myeloma. Clinical development has nearly doubled in scale since 2019 (32 to 60 programs), reflecting the diversification strategy beyond Gilead's traditional virology core. The lenacapavir development program spans both treatment and prevention across multiple clinical studies (PURPOSE 1, PURPOSE 2, PURPOSE-365, BIRCH, CAPELLA, OAKTREE, ARTISTRY-1, ARTISTRY-2) and represents a paradigm-shifting approach to HIV care. The oral BIC/LEN combination (bictegravir 75mg/lenacapavir 50mg) met primary endpoints in both ARTISTRY-1 and ARTISTRY-2 Phase 3 trials and is targeted for launch in H2 2026. The PURPOSE-365 study is evaluating a once-yearly lenacapavir injection for HIV prevention, with potential availability as early as 2028. Gilead also licensed investigational HSV helicase-primase inhibitor programs (ABI-1179, ABI-5366) from Assembly Biosciences.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Global Regulatory Affairs manages filings across the virology, oncology, and inflammation portfolios. Key regulatory milestones include: FDA approval of Yeztugo (lenacapavir) for PrEP in June 2025; European Commission approval of lenacapavir PrEP (as Yeytuo) in August 2025; regulatory filings submitted in Australia, Brazil, Canada, South Africa, Switzerland, with additional filings in preparation for Argentina, Mexico, and Peru; Livdelzi's accelerated approval (now >50% second-line PBC market share with 3-year Phase 3 ASSURE long-term data supporting safety/efficacy); the BLA filing for bulevirtide (chronic HDV, potential decision 2026); and the FDA acceptance of the anito-cel BLA for fourth-line relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma (PDUFA date December 23, 2026), which followed the $7.8B Arcellx acquisition. Anito-cel has received Fast Track, Orphan Drug, and Regenerative Medicine Advanced Therapy Designations. The regulatory function also manages the complex global filing strategy for lenacapavir -- including royalty-free licensing agreements with six generic manufacturers covering 120 LLMICs, with technology transfers completed and first deliveries made to Eswatini and Zambia through the PEPFAR partnership. The company faces no major LOE until 2036 (updated from prior 2033 estimate), giving the regulatory function a planning horizon focused on new approvals rather than patent defense. Four new product launches are planned for 2026, including BIC/LEN oral HIV treatment and potential cancer therapies.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
Gilead's PV function manages safety monitoring across a diverse portfolio that includes antivirals, CAR T cell therapies, antibody-drug conjugates, and small molecules. CAR T safety monitoring is particularly intensive, requiring REMS programs and long-term follow-up for cytokine release syndrome, neurotoxicity, and secondary malignancies. Anito-cel's pivotal data (117 patients, 15.9-month median follow-up) showed CRS in 86% of patients -- though 83% experienced Grade 1 or lower -- and ICANS in 8%, with only one Grade 3 case. The Yeztugo PrEP launch (90% payer coverage, $0 co-pay for most) introduces PV requirements at commercial scale: monitoring safety in a healthy, HIV-negative population receiving a twice-yearly injection -- demanding the highest safety standards comparable to vaccine PV. The PEPFAR-supported global rollout (targeting 2M people over 3 years) will test PV infrastructure across diverse healthcare systems. The liver disease portfolio adds additional complexity with Livdelzi's accelerated approval requiring post-marketing confirmatory studies (3-year ASSURE data presented at AASLD) and bulevirtide's anticipated approval for chronic HDV. The once-yearly lenacapavir injection (PURPOSE-365) and oral BIC/LEN will further expand the PV workload as they advance toward launch.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance operates through R&D leadership and portfolio committees, with CMO Dietmar Berger playing a central role since January 2025. B/R decisions are especially consequential for: (1) Lenacapavir PrEP at commercial scale -- managing the B/R profile of Yeztugo across a rapidly growing U.S. patient base (90% payer coverage) and 120 LLMICs via the PEPFAR partnership; (2) Next-generation lenacapavir formulations -- the once-yearly injection (PURPOSE-365) and oral BIC/LEN require distinct B/R frameworks for different patient populations; (3) CAR T therapies -- the Arcellx acquisition brings anito-cel's B/R profile under full Gilead/Kite governance, including comparative positioning against approved BCMA-targeted therapies (Abecma, Carvykti); (4) Accelerated approvals (Livdelzi) -- confirming clinical benefit through post-marketing studies while the product has become market leader in second-line PBC. The $32B planned U.S. investment and $7.8B Arcellx acquisition signal a company investing aggressively in expanded pipeline capacity, which will increase B/R governance complexity across the 60-program portfolio.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
ArcaScience should lead with the lenacapavir PrEP story, now strengthened by the FDA approval (June 2025), EU approval (Aug 2025), and commercial launch. Yeztugo represents a paradigm shift: a twice-yearly injection administered to healthy, HIV-negative individuals for prevention, now prescribed at scale (90% payer coverage). The B/R framework for preventive medicine in healthy populations is fundamentally different from treatment of sick patients -- the safety bar is orders of magnitude higher, and the benefit must be framed in probabilistic terms (infections prevented per 1000 person-years vs. adverse events). This is exactly the scenario where ArcaScience's probabilistic, continuously updated BRA adds transformative value. The global access dimension is now operational: Gilead has completed technology transfers to six generic manufacturers, delivered first doses to Eswatini and Zambia, and partnered with PEPFAR to reach 2 million people over three years. B/R monitoring across 120 LLMICs with diverse populations and varying PV infrastructure is a massive data integration challenge that ArcaScience can address.

The timing is strengthened by the pipeline expansion: the once-yearly injection (PURPOSE-365, targeting ~2028) and oral BIC/LEN (launching H2 2026) create parallel B/R assessment needs for different lenacapavir formulations in different populations. ArcaScience can model the comparative B/R of twice-yearly injection vs. once-yearly injection vs. daily oral across patient subgroups.

The CAR T angle is now a stronger entry point following the $7.8B Arcellx acquisition (Feb 2026). Gilead now has full control over anito-cel (BLA accepted, PDUFA Dec 2026) and must demonstrate best-in-class B/R versus approved BCMA-targeted therapies (Abecma, Carvykti). The pivotal data (96% ORR, 74% sCR/CR, 95% MRD negativity, manageable CRS profile) need dynamic BRA to position anito-cel competitively. With cell therapy sales at $1.8B in 2025 (down 7%) and the Arcellx D-Domain platform enabling next-generation programs, Gilead needs to demonstrate clear differentiation.

Propose a dual pilot: (1) lenacapavir PrEP post-marketing surveillance across the PEPFAR rollout countries, and (2) anito-cel competitive B/R modeling ahead of the December 2026 PDUFA date. The CMO, one year into the role and focused on "balanced, partially derisked" portfolio strategy, is a receptive audience for a platform that unifies B/R governance across these diverse programs.
