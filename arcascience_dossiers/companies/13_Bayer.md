# Bayer (Pharma)

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** $17.8B (pharma division, within EUR 46.6B group revenue)
- **Headquarters:** Leverkusen, Germany
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Oncology (precision oncology, targeted radionuclide therapy), cardiovascular disease, immunology, neurology & rare diseases, women's health
- **Pipeline Size:** 31+ clinical development projects across four core therapeutic areas; 9 positive Phase III trials completed in 2024; 20+ clinical programs advanced in 18 months
- **Strategic Priorities:** "Dynamic Shared Ownership" (DSO) organizational transformation; managing Xarelto loss of exclusivity; growth via Nubeqa (prostate cancer), Kerendia (heart failure/CKD), Beyonttra/acoramidis (ATTR-CM), elinzanetant (women's health); precision oncology research center in Boston-Cambridge; cell and gene therapy expansion; radical de-layering and restructuring (11,000+ jobs eliminated)

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Bayer Pharma's clinical development is organized under the Head of R&D for the Pharmaceuticals division. The division is structured across four core therapeutic areas: cardiovascular diseases, oncology, immunology, and neurology & rare diseases. In 2024, Bayer underwent massive restructuring under its "Dynamic Shared Ownership" model, eliminating management layers and reducing executive positions by nearly 50%. A new Global Commercialization organization was formed, merging former Oncology, Global Marketing, and Digital & Commercial Innovation units. Clinical development teams operate within this flattened structure with greater autonomy and faster decision-making. The precision molecular oncology research center in Boston-Cambridge integrates discovery with early clinical development for novel targeted cancer therapies.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs operates within the Pharmaceuticals division under the R&D organization. Given the DSO restructuring, regulatory teams have been reorganized with fewer hierarchical layers. The function manages critical multi-market submissions including Nubeqa label expansion (third indication via ARANOTE data), Kerendia expansion (MOONRAKER and THUNDERBALL studies), Beyonttra EU approval, and elinzanetant filings post-OASIS trials. The Xarelto loss of exclusivity creates a period where regulatory focus shifts from lifecycle management to new product approvals.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
Pharmacovigilance is organized as a global function within the Pharmaceuticals division, supporting both marketed products and clinical programs. The PV workload is significant given the large cardiovascular portfolio (Xarelto post-marketing surveillance during LOE period, Kerendia in new indications), the oncology portfolio (Nubeqa, Xofigo, Stivarga), and emerging cell and gene therapy programs (bemdaneprocel for Parkinson's) which require novel safety monitoring approaches. The DSO model has likely flattened PV reporting lines, giving safety leads more direct access to decision-making.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance occurs through the R&D portfolio review process and at the division level through the Pharmaceuticals Division leadership. The current leadership (Stefan Oelrich as President, Pharmaceuticals Division) explicitly references the DSO model as enabling faster growth and efficiency decisions. B/R assessments are particularly critical during the Xarelto-to-new-products transition period, where maintaining safety vigilance on declining products while rapidly advancing new ones creates competing demands on B/R governance resources.
