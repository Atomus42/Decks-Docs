# Celltrion

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** KRW 3.56 trillion (~$2.48 billion; up 63.5% YoY); biologics division at KRW 3.11T (up 57.7%); Q4 revenue exceeded KRW 1 trillion for the first time
- **Headquarters:** Incheon, South Korea
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Biosimilars (immunology, oncology, hematology, endocrinology), novel biologics (antibody-drug conjugates, bispecific antibodies, FcRn inhibitors, obesity treatments)
- **Pipeline Size:** 11 commercialized biosimilars (plan to expand to 22 by 2030, 41 by 2038); Phase 3 biosimilar candidates for Darzalex, Keytruda, Ocrevus, Cosentyx; 16 novel drug assets including ADCs, bispecifics, FcRn inhibitors; 13 IND applications planned by 2028; CT-P70 (ADC) received FDA Fast Track Designation
- **Strategic Priorities:** Biosimilar portfolio expansion to 22 products by 2030, novel drug pipeline acceleration (ADC platform with PBX-7016 payload), US manufacturing expansion (Branchburg, NJ facility from Eli Lilly), European market leadership (Vegzelma market leader), revenue target of KRW 5.3T for 2026, transition from biosimilar company to integrated biopharmaceutical company

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Celltrion's clinical development organization supports both biosimilar and novel drug programs. For biosimilars, development involves Phase 3 comparative efficacy/safety trials to demonstrate biosimilarity. For novel drugs, the company is building clinical capabilities with 4 candidates already in clinical trials (ADCs CT-P70, CT-P71, CT-P73; bispecific CT-P72). The ADC platform leverages the proprietary PBX-7016 payload demonstrating low toxicity and high tumor growth inhibition. The post-merger integration with Celltrion Healthcare (December 2023) unified development and commercial operations. Manufacturing capacity of 250,000L supports clinical supply.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs manages FDA, EMA, and global filings for the biosimilar portfolio. The company has achieved FDA approvals for 10+ biosimilar products. CT-P70 received FDA Fast Track Designation, signaling proactive regulatory engagement for novel programs. Regulatory strategy for Phase 3 biosimilar candidates (CT-P44/daratumumab, CT-P51/pembrolizumab, CT-P53/ocrelizumab) involves demonstrating biosimilarity to high-value reference products. The transition to novel drug development requires building regulatory capabilities for IND/NDA pathways.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV supports 11 commercialized biosimilars across global markets, with particularly strong presence in Europe and North America. Biosimilar PV involves demonstrating comparable safety profiles to reference products through post-marketing surveillance. The novel drug pipeline (ADCs with novel PBX-7016 payload) requires building first-in-human safety monitoring capabilities. The Zymfentra (subcutaneous infliximab) program requires PV for a novel formulation of an established biologic. The Eli Lilly contract manufacturing relationship adds quality and safety oversight complexity.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance at Celltrion operates at two levels: (1) biosimilar B/R, where the goal is demonstrating no clinically meaningful differences from reference products, and (2) novel drug B/R, where the company is establishing evaluation frameworks for first-in-class ADC and bispecific candidates. The transition from biosimilar-focused to integrated biopharmaceutical company requires evolving B/R governance from comparative assessment to de novo evaluation.
