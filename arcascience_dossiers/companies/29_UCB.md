# UCB

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** EUR 6.15B (~$6.7B)
- **Headquarters:** Brussels, Belgium
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Immunology (psoriasis, psoriatic arthritis, HS, axSpA, SLE), neurology (epilepsy, myasthenia gravis, Alzheimer's, Parkinson's)
- **Pipeline Size:** 10 clinical programs (1 Phase IV, 1 regulatory review, 4 Phase III, 4 Phase II), plus earlier research activities
- **Strategic Priorities:** "Decade+ of growth" strategy; five growth driver products (Bimzelx, Evenity, Fintepla, Rystiggo, Zilbrysq); new growth organization model (implemented mid-2024); R&D ratio at 29% of revenue; Alzheimer's and SLE pipeline expansion

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
UCB operates as a single biopharmaceutical segment with clinical development organized along its two therapeutic pillars — immunology and neurology. The Executive Committee serves as the Chief Operating Decision Maker. UCB implemented a new "growth organization model" in mid-2024, restructuring how clinical development teams interact with commercial and regulatory functions. With 10 clinical programs spanning multiple indications, the clinical development organization manages both proprietary programs and collaborations (with Biogen, Roche/Genentech, and Novartis).

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs is integrated within the single-segment organizational model, supporting global submissions across EMA, FDA, PMDA, and NMPA. In 2024, UCB achieved five regulatory approvals in the first half alone across the EU, Japan, and China, indicating a well-functioning regulatory machine. The regulatory team manages complex multi-indication strategies (e.g., Bimzelx across psoriasis, HS, and axSpA).

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV operates under the medical/clinical organization, managing safety across both mature products (Keppra/levetiracetam legacy) and newly launched biologics (Bimzelx, Rystiggo, Zilbrysq). The transition from a small-molecule company to a biologics-heavy portfolio requires PV to adapt to new safety monitoring paradigms, including immunogenicity surveillance and biologic-specific safety signals.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance sits within the Executive Committee structure. Given UCB's "growth organization model" restructure in 2024, B/R decision-making likely involves cross-functional teams that span clinical development, regulatory, safety, and commercial functions. The rapid launch cadence of five growth drivers simultaneously demands rigorous, real-time B/R assessment capabilities.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
ArcaScience should target UCB through the Bimzelx multi-indication expansion strategy. Bimzelx is being launched across psoriasis, hidradenitis suppurativa, and axial spondyloarthritis simultaneously across multiple geographies — this creates a perfect use case for dynamic B/R assessment that integrates clinical trial data from multiple indication-specific trials with accumulating real-world evidence from launched markets. The pitch should emphasize: (1) managing B/R across multiple indications and geographies in real-time as Bimzelx expands, (2) supporting the dapirolizumab pegol Phase III program in SLE — a high-risk, high-reward indication where probabilistic approval prediction is especially valuable, and (3) enabling UCB's "decade+ of growth" strategy by systematically de-risking the pipeline of 10 clinical programs. The 2024 organizational restructure creates a natural window for introducing new tools as part of the new operating model. UCB's substantial R&D investment (29% of revenue) and stated commitment to innovation make it receptive to platform investments that improve development efficiency.
