# Aurobindo Pharma

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** ~$3.6B (INR 317B; +9.4% YoY; U.S. formulations account for ~48% of revenue)
- **Headquarters:** HITEC City, Hyderabad, India (U.S. HQ in East Windsor, NJ)
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Generics (antibiotics, anti-retrovirals, cardiovascular, CNS, gastroenterology, anti-allergics), APIs (semi-synthetic penicillin market leader), biosimilars (oncology and immunology; 14 products in development with $50B+ market potential), injectables/peptides, specialty products
- **Pipeline Size:** 848 cumulative ANDA filings (676 final approvals, 26 tentative); 14 biosimilar products under development; 3 EMA-approved biosimilars via CuraTeQ (Dazublys/trastuzumab, Dyrupeg, Zefylti); pipeline covering 80%+ of near-term patent expirations; strong injectable and peptide pipeline
- **Strategic Priorities:** Biosimilar portfolio expansion (14 products, $50B+ addressable market); backward integration (Pen-G facility, 6-APA plant); complex generics and peptide injectables; CuraTeQ Biologics (biosimilar subsidiary) expansion; TheraNym Biologics (CMO facility for biologics); Merck & Co. CDMO partnership; China market entry; European market growth (+19% YoY)

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Aurobindo's clinical development operates through its core generics business (bioequivalence studies for ANDAs), the CuraTeQ Biologics subsidiary (biosimilar clinical development), and the specialty/injectables pipeline (complex generics requiring clinical studies). The company has 9 R&D centers (5 in India, 4 in the U.S.) and a global workforce of 33,000+. Biosimilar clinical development is the most intensive, with Phase 3 comparability studies conducted by CuraTeQ. The TheraNym Biologics subsidiary is establishing a large CMO facility with 2x 15KL bioreactors for mammalian cell culture products, potentially offering clinical manufacturing services to partners. The Merck & Co. partnership for biologics CDMO further strengthens clinical manufacturing capabilities.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs is a massive operation managing 848+ cumulative ANDA filings with the FDA, plus international filings across EU (EDQM), UK (MHRA), Japan (PMDA), Canada, South Africa (MCC), and Brazil (ANVISA). The CuraTeQ subsidiary manages biosimilar regulatory submissions — achieving 3 EMA approvals (Dazublys, Dyrupeg, Zefylti). The regulatory team handles generics, biosimilars, APIs, and complex generics regulatory pathways across 150+ countries. Operations across 29+ manufacturing facilities require extensive regulatory compliance management.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV manages safety across one of the largest generic pharmaceutical portfolios globally, including antibiotics, cardiovascular products, CNS products, and anti-retrovirals. For biosimilars, PV handles immunogenicity monitoring and post-marketing comparability surveillance. The scale of operations (150+ countries, millions of patients) requires highly scalable PV systems. API manufacturing PV focuses on impurity profiling and quality-related safety considerations.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance operates under the Vice Chairman/MD (K. Nityananda Reddy) and the promoter group, with Board oversight. For generics, B/R decisions are straightforward (referencing originator data). For biosimilars, B/R decisions involve comparability assessment complexity. The family-controlled promoter group (significant shareholding) provides strategic continuity. The organizational structure is divided between India (majority of manufacturing/R&D), U.S. (major market), Europe (growing), and rest of world.
