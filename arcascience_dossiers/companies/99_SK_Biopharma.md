# SK Biopharmaceuticals

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** KRW 547.6B (~$378M; 54.3% YoY growth; first-ever annual net profit KRW 227B; US cenobamate revenue KRW 438.7B up 62%)
- **Headquarters:** Pangyo, Seongnam, South Korea (US subsidiary SK Life Science in Paramus, New Jersey)
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** CNS — epilepsy (cenobamate/XCOPRI/ONTOZRY, highest new Rx share in US epilepsy), sleep disorders (solriamfetol), oncology (emerging pipeline); AI-based epilepsy management
- **Pipeline Size:** 8 compounds in development across CNS and oncology; cenobamate approved in US, EU, Israel, Canada and recently in Korea and China; Phase 3 for primary generalized tonic-clonic (PGTC) seizures indication expansion; pediatric oral suspension formulation in development; early-stage oncology and Parkinson's disease programs; AI/EEG digital health platform (Mentis Care JV with Eurofarma)
- **Strategic Priorities:** Cenobamate global maximization (XCOPRI US revenue guidance $420-450M in 2025); indication and age range expansion (PGTC seizures, pediatric); China market entry (NMPA approval via Ignis Therapeutics JV); AI-based digital health in epilepsy management (Mentis Care); pipeline expansion into oncology and Parkinson's; first Korean company to independently develop and commercialize a drug in the US

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
SK Biopharmaceuticals' clinical development is organized around its core CNS expertise. The US subsidiary (SK Life Science) manages North American clinical operations including cenobamate lifecycle management (PGTC seizure indication expansion Phase 3, pediatric formulation development). Global clinical development is supported through a network of commercialization partners (Angelini/Arvelle for EU, ONO for Japan, Dong-A ST, Eurofarma, Hikma, Ignis). The company employs target-based drug discovery, high-throughput screening, computer-aided drug design, and combinatorial chemistry. Extension into oncology and Parkinson's disease represents pipeline diversification beyond epilepsy.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs manages multi-jurisdictional filings through a hub-and-spoke model: direct filings in US (FDA) and Korea (MFDS), with partner-managed filings in EU (EMA/Angelini), Japan (PMDA/ONO), China (NMPA/Ignis), and other markets (Eurofarma for LatAm, Hikma for MENA). The team secured NDA approvals in Korea and China for cenobamate (November-December 2025), demonstrating coordinated global regulatory execution. Regulatory capability includes sNDA/variation filings for indication expansion.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV monitors cenobamate safety across 100,000+ patients treated globally (across XCOPRI and ONTOZRY brands). Multi-partner safety data harmonization is a significant challenge with 6+ commercialization partners in different regions. The epilepsy-focused safety monitoring requires expertise in CNS adverse events, titration-related effects, and drug-drug interactions with other antiepileptic medications.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance is centralized at SK Biopharmaceuticals headquarters with input from SK Life Science (US) and regional partners. The indication expansion strategy (PGTC seizures, pediatric) involves B/R assessment of cenobamate in new patient populations. The Mentis Care AI platform adds a digital health dimension to epilepsy B/R monitoring through real-time seizure detection and EEG analysis.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
SK Biopharmaceuticals' transition from a single-product CNS company to a diversified CNS+oncology pipeline creates a natural inflection point for B/R governance maturation. ArcaScience should position the BRA platform around: (1) cenobamate lifecycle optimization — supporting PGTC seizure indication expansion and pediatric development through integrated B/R modeling that synthesizes data from 100,000+ treated patients across multiple partner territories, (2) multi-partner B/R harmonization — providing a unified B/R framework across 6+ commercialization partners (Angelini, ONO, Ignis, Eurofarma, Hikma, Dong-A ST), solving a real operational challenge of disparate safety and efficacy data streams, and (3) pipeline diversification support — enabling rigorous B/R assessment as the company enters oncology and Parkinson's disease, therapeutic areas requiring different B/R expertise than epilepsy. The natural synergy with Mentis Care's AI-based epilepsy platform should be highlighted — ArcaScience's B/R modeling could integrate with the real-time seizure detection and EEG data from Mentis Care to create a uniquely data-rich epilepsy B/R monitoring system. The US subsidiary (SK Life Science) is likely the most accessible entry point.
