# Astellas Pharma

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** $13.2B (JPY ~1.9T; record-high revenue and core operating profit in 20-year history)
- **Headquarters:** Tokyo, Japan
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Oncology (prostate cancer, bladder cancer, solid tumors), ophthalmology (IZERVAY for geographic atrophy), urology, transplant & immunology, women's health (VEOZAH for vasomotor symptoms)
- **Pipeline Size:** Active pipeline across four primary R&D focuses (Immuno-Oncology, Targeted Protein Degradation, Genetic Regulation, Blindness & Regeneration); 5 Strategic Brands driving growth (PADCEV, IZERVAY, VEOZAH, VYLOY, XOSPATA); ~2,500 R&D employees across 15 R&D sites; R&D investment of JPY 327.7B (17.1% of sales)
- **Strategic Priorities:** PADCEV franchise expansion (bladder cancer, cisplatin-ineligible MIBC); organizational transformation to patient-centered model; Focus Area Approach in R&D (4 primary focuses); Sustainable Margin Transformation (SMT) cost optimization; first-in-class targeted protein degraders (ASP3082 for KRAS G12D); subcutaneous T-cell engagers (ASP2138 for gastric cancer); digital transformation (Chief Digital & Transformation Officer role)

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Astellas reframed its organizational structure in FY2025, moving from a functional-based model to a patient-centered model organized around end-to-end activities from early research through commercialization. The Chief Research & Development Officer (CRDO) oversees a broad portfolio including: CRDO Office, Biopharma & Ophthalmology Development, Clinical Operations & Quantitative Science, Early Development & Translational Science, Oncology Development, Regulatory Affairs & Pharmacovigilance, Oncology Research, Cell & Gene Therapy Research, Innovation Lab, and Primary Focus Leads for the four R&D focus areas. This structure fosters cross-functional collaboration with separate development streams for oncology and non-oncology (biopharma & ophthalmology). The company operates 15 global R&D sites with 2,500+ R&D employees.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory Affairs is organized under the CRDO alongside Pharmacovigilance as a combined "Regulatory Affairs & Pharmacovigilance" function. This integration is notable -- combining RA and PV under a single leadership structure facilitates tighter coordination between regulatory filing strategy and safety monitoring. Astellas manages global regulatory responsibilities for XTANDI (outside the U.S.), full global regulatory responsibility for PADCEV, IZERVAY, VEOZAH, VYLOY, and XOSPATA, plus pipeline programs. Key regulatory activities include PADCEV label expansion (EV-303/KEYNOTE-905 data for cisplatin-ineligible MIBC), XTANDI label defense as a legacy product, and first-in-class program filings for targeted protein degraders and T-cell engagers.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
Pharmacovigilance is organizationally paired with Regulatory Affairs under the CRDO, creating an integrated "RA & PV" function. This structure positions PV as closely linked to regulatory strategy rather than siloed in a separate safety organization. Given the diversity of Astellas's portfolio -- ADCs (PADCEV), small molecules (XTANDI, VEOZAH), complement inhibitors (IZERVAY), and emerging modalities (targeted protein degraders, T-cell engagers, cell & gene therapies) -- the PV function must be adaptable across multiple safety monitoring frameworks. The oncology portfolio, in particular, requires intensive safety governance for immune-mediated adverse events (PADCEV) and novel mechanism-related toxicities.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance is centralized under the CRDO, who oversees both clinical development and regulatory affairs/pharmacovigilance, creating a unified decision-making structure for B/R assessments. The Focus Area Approach provides a strategic framework for B/R decisions: resources are concentrated on four primary focuses where the company believes it can achieve the greatest impact, with B/R serving as a key gating criterion. The Sustainable Margin Transformation (SMT) adds a financial dimension to B/R governance, ensuring that R&D investments are directed to programs with the strongest B/R profiles and commercial potential.
