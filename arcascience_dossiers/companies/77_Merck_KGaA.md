# Merck KGaA

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** EUR 8.5B (Healthcare sector); EUR 21.2B (Group total across Healthcare, Life Sciences, Electronics)
- **Headquarters:** Darmstadt, Germany
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Oncology (Erbitux, Bavencio, Tepmetko, DDR inhibitors), neurology & immunology (MS/Mavenclad, myasthenia gravis, lupus), fertility (global market leader)
- **Pipeline Size:** 200+ active clinical trials involving Erbitux alone (30+ Phase III); active Phase II/III programs in DDR inhibitors (tuvusertib/M1774, M9466), neurology (cladribine in gMG), immunology (enpatoran in CLE/SLE); oncology lifecycle management across multiple assets
- **Strategic Priorities:** Innovation-driven and established franchise growth; oncology lifecycle management; expansion into new indications (neurology, immunology); cost discipline and R&D efficiency; digital transformation across the enterprise (operates as EMD Serono in US/Canada)

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Merck KGaA's Healthcare sector organizes clinical development by therapeutic franchise: Oncology, Neurology & Immunology, and Fertility. Each franchise has dedicated clinical development teams managing global programs. The oncology franchise is the largest, with an extensive lifecycle management strategy for Erbitux (200+ active trials) and newer assets like Bavencio, Tepmetko, and DDR inhibitors. The Neurology & Immunology franchise is expanding beyond MS (Mavenclad) into myasthenia gravis and lupus. The company operates a global clinical development network with significant capabilities in Europe, North America (via EMD Serono), and Asia.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs is a global function within the Healthcare sector, managing filings across EMA, FDA, and 65+ country markets. The team has demonstrated strong multi-jurisdictional capability with Tepmetko approved in ~40 markets and Bavencio in 70+ countries. Regulatory strategy is closely integrated with clinical development, as evidenced by the management of accelerated-to-full approval conversions (Tepmetko FDA full approval 2024) and lifecycle management filings.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
As a large multinational with marketed products across oncology, neurology, and fertility, Merck KGaA maintains a substantial global pharmacovigilance organization. Safety monitoring is managed centrally with regional support, covering marketed products and an extensive clinical trial portfolio. The PV organization must manage complex safety profiles across oncology combination regimens and immunomodulatory therapies.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
Benefit-risk governance is embedded within the franchise development structure, with key B/R decisions made at development committee level involving clinical, regulatory, safety, and commercial input. The discontinuation of the xevinapant Phase III program in 2024 illustrates active B/R governance in practice. Portfolio-level B/R decisions are elevated to Healthcare sector leadership, particularly for resource allocation across the three therapeutic franchises.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
Merck KGaA's Healthcare sector presents a compelling opportunity through its Oncology franchise, which manages 200+ active clinical trials for Erbitux alone plus growing DDR inhibitor and immunotherapy programs. The complexity of lifecycle management across multiple oncology assets and the expansion into neurology/immunology creates a clear need for integrated B/R assessment that spans therapeutic areas and geographies. ArcaScience should approach through the oncology development leadership, positioning the BRA platform as essential for: (1) optimizing combination regimen development decisions across DDR inhibitors and existing immunotherapy assets, (2) supporting multi-jurisdictional regulatory strategy for products like Tepmetko (40+ markets) and Bavencio (70+ countries), and (3) integrating real-world evidence from extensive marketed product experience into development-stage B/R assessments. The 2024 discontinuation of xevinapant after Phase III trial failure demonstrates the financial stakes of late-stage attrition — a direct pain point that ArcaScience's probabilistic modeling addresses. Entry through EMD Serono (US operations) may provide a faster path to pilot engagement than HQ-level procurement.
