## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Haleon's clinical development is fundamentally different from prescription pharma — focused on OTC product efficacy substantiation, clinical evidence for regulatory claims, and Rx-to-OTC switch programs rather than NDA-style clinical trials. The company operates 3 global R&D centers with ~GBP 300M annual budget. Clinical studies support product claims (e.g., "whitens teeth by two shades" for Sensodyne Clinical White, Panadol Dual Action combining paracetamol and ibuprofen). The Rx-to-OTC switch capability (4 switches in 8 years) requires specialized clinical development to demonstrate OTC safety and consumer self-selection capability.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs manages a complex global portfolio of OTC products across 170+ markets with varying regulatory frameworks (FDA OTC monograph/NDA, EU OTC regulations, APAC diverse national requirements). The Rx-to-OTC switch team has demonstrated exceptional regulatory capability with 4 successful switches. The Eroxon launch (first FDA-cleared OTC ED topical) represents novel regulatory pathway navigation. The company's 2026 reorganization into 6 Operating Units will distribute regulatory responsibility across regional structures.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV covers the safety monitoring for a massive OTC product portfolio consumed by hundreds of millions of consumers globally. OTC product safety monitoring differs from prescription pharma — focusing on consumer misuse, drug interactions, and population-level safety signals across diverse consumer demographics. The scale (GBP 11.2B in sales) and consumer accessibility of the products require robust post-marketing surveillance systems.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance in consumer health focuses on ensuring favorable benefit-risk profiles for products used by the general population without physician supervision. This creates unique B/R considerations: lower efficacy thresholds but higher safety requirements compared to prescription drugs. Rx-to-OTC switch decisions are fundamentally B/R assessments — determining whether products can be safely used in a consumer self-care setting. Portfolio B/R decisions inform brand extension, reformulation, and geographic launch strategies.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
Haleon represents an unconventional but potentially valuable opportunity if ArcaScience can adapt its BRA platform for the consumer health context. The strongest entry angle is through the Rx-to-OTC switch capability, where Haleon leads the industry (4 switches in 8 years) and where B/R assessment is the central analytical framework. ArcaScience should position the BRA platform specifically for: (1) Rx-to-OTC switch feasibility evaluation, integrating prescription drug safety data with consumer self-selection modeling to predict switch success probability, (2) consumer safety monitoring at scale, where the massive OTC product portfolio (hundreds of millions of consumers) requires continuous B/R monitoring adapted for self-care populations, and (3) Eroxon-type novel regulatory pathway navigation, where first-in-kind OTC products require innovative B/R assessment frameworks. The platform would need significant adaptation for OTC-specific B/R parameters (consumer self-selection safety, misuse potential, drug interaction modeling). A pilot focused on a candidate Rx-to-OTC switch would demonstrate the highest-value use case. However, this account requires product customization that may not align with ArcaScience's core platform — it should be pursued only if the Rx-to-OTC switch market segment is strategically interesting.
