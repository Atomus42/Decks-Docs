# Bristol-Myers Squibb

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2025):** $48.2 billion (essentially flat YoY as Growth Portfolio offset Legacy Portfolio declines); Growth Portfolio generated $26.4 billion (+17% YoY), comprising 55% of total revenue -- exceeding the 50%+ target at +18% growth through mid-year
- **Headquarters:** Princeton, New Jersey, USA
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Oncology (I/O, cell therapy, ADCs, protein degradation), Hematology (anticoagulation, CAR-T, CELMoD), Immunology (TYK2 inhibition, B/T cell targeting), Cardiovascular, Neuroscience
- **Pipeline Size:** Extensive; 80+ company-sponsored studies presented at ASCO 2025; 50+ oncology studies at ESMO 2025; over 30 priority clinical trial teams using AI-enabled Workbench platform; 10+ new medicines targeted for launch by 2030 across neuroscience, cardiovascular, immunology, and oncology
- **Strategic Priorities:** Navigating patent cliff (Revlimid, Eliquis LOEs; Legacy Portfolio down 15% to $21.8B in FY2025), Growth Portfolio acceleration (Opdivo Qvantig, Reblozyl +33%, Sotyktu +99%, Camzyos +200% in FY2025), "Predict First" AI approach, cell therapy leadership, protein degradation platform (CELMoD Phase 3 readouts in 2026: SUCCESSOR-1 and SUCCESSOR-2 for mezigdomide), AI Center of Excellence with NVIDIA, $40B five-year US investment commitment (May 2025, covering R&D, radiopharmaceutical manufacturing, and AI/ML), Evinova partnership for AI-native clinical trial optimization (February 2026), strategic AI partnerships (Recursion/Exscientia $1.3B+, VantAI $674M, insitro)

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
BMS's clinical development is led by Cristian Massacesi (EVP, Chief Medical Officer and Head of Development, since August 2025), who was recruited from AstraZeneca where he served as CMO of AstraZeneca and Alexion and as Oncology Chief Development Officer, leading a global organization of 3,000+ employees, advancing 150+ clinical studies, and securing multiple regulatory approvals. He previously held senior R&D positions at Pfizer and Novartis. R&D leadership also includes Robert Plenge (in the executive team, likely overseeing research). Clinical development operates across therapeutic areas (Oncology, Hematology, Immunology, Cardiovascular, Neuroscience) with cross-functional integration. Massacesi has publicly stated that "transforming clinical development is not just an opportunity; it is an urgent necessity," signaling strong receptivity to AI-enabled tools.

BMS has deeply integrated AI into clinical operations through the **Workbench** platform (developed with Accenture), a clinical trial accelerator that fuses AI-driven insights with real-time operational data. Workbench has been adopted by 30+ top priority clinical trial teams, with users surging from under 100 to nearly 900 in 3 months. More than 30 generative AI solutions have been deployed across R&D, Global Product Development & Supply (GPS), and Commercialization. In February 2026, BMS signed a strategic partnership with **Evinova** (AstraZeneca's AI health tech company) to deploy Evinova's Cost Optimizer module of Study Designer across BMS's global portfolio, applying AI to improve trial design, identify productivity opportunities, and accelerate development. This partnership -- notably with a company spun out of Massacesi's former employer -- demonstrates the new CMO's direct influence on AI adoption in clinical development.

BMS's cell therapy organization is headed by Lynelle Hoch (President, Cell Therapy Organization), reflecting the strategic importance of this modality. The 2026 development calendar is stacked with pivotal readouts: anticoagulant milvexian, IPF prospect admilparant, radiopharmaceutical RYZ101, CELMoD agents (SUCCESSOR-1 and SUCCESSOR-2 for mezigdomide), and 14 studies related to Cobenfy in neuroscience.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
The **Regulatory and Quality Compliance** team ensures all biopharmaceutical products meet regulatory standards throughout their lifecycle. Regulatory Affairs develops regulatory strategies supporting development, approval, and maintenance of drugs and biologics. The function operates across global offices including Princeton (US HQ), Otemachi (Japan -- housing Regulatory, Medical Affairs, PV, Clinical Strategies), and Boudry (Switzerland -- housing Regulatory, Drug Safety, Medical Affairs, Clinical R&D). BMS has demonstrated strong regulatory execution: Opdivo Qvantig (subcutaneous nivolumab + hyaluronidase) received FDA approval in December 2024 as the first and only subcutaneous PD-1 inhibitor, followed by European Commission approval in May 2025 across multiple solid tumor indications. Additional filings include FDA priority review for Opdivo+AVD in Hodgkin Lymphoma (PDUFA April 2026) and Sotyktu sNDA for psoriatic arthritis (PDUFA March 2026). The regulatory function manages submissions across multiple modalities: small molecules, biologics, cell therapies (Breyanzi, Abecma), subcutaneous reformulations (Opdivo Qvantig bridging B/R), and emerging platforms (ADCs, molecular glues, radiopharmaceuticals).

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
BMS's **Patient Safety** organization works globally to monitor the safety of all medicines. Every adverse event potentially linked to a BMS product is thoroughly evaluated in the context of all available data. At subsidiary level (e.g., RayzeBio), the Head of Pharmacovigilance -- Safety Science oversees all aspects of product safety across clinical development and post-marketing surveillance. PV responsibilities include developing global pharmacovigilance strategy for proactive risk management and compliance with FDA, EMA, and ICH regulations; overseeing end-to-end PV activities including case processing, safety surveillance, risk management, and signal detection; and establishing robust safety governance structures. PV works cross-functionally with clinical, regulatory, medical affairs, quality, and commercial teams. Regional PV operations include **Pharmacovigilance International Patient Safety** functions in key markets.

The PV function is currently navigating heightened scrutiny from the FDA's class-wide investigation into **T-cell malignancies following CAR-T therapy**. In January 2024, the FDA required boxed warnings on all BCMA- and CD19-directed CAR-T products (including BMS's Breyanzi and Abecma), with lifelong monitoring recommended for all treated patients. While BMS has stated that across 4,700+ patients treated commercially and clinically, it has "not observed any CAR-positive T-cell malignancy cases" and has not found a causal relationship, the 15-year mandated follow-up studies and ongoing FDA surveillance create sustained PV workload. Notably, in June 2025 the FDA removed REMS requirements for Breyanzi, Abecma, and Carvykti, signaling confidence in community management of CRS/neurotoxicity but maintaining the secondary malignancy boxed warning -- a B/R complexity that demands continuous, data-driven monitoring.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance at BMS operates through:
1. **Product Safety Teams** -- Asset-level safety monitoring and B/R assessment across development and post-marketing
2. **Safety Science leadership** -- Strategic PV and B/R governance per therapeutic area
3. **CMO/Head of Development** -- Portfolio-level B/R decisions; Cristian Massacesi's extensive multi-company CMO experience (AstraZeneca, Alexion, Pfizer, Novartis) brings diverse B/R governance perspectives; his AstraZeneca tenure coincided with Evinova's development, giving him firsthand experience with AI-enabled clinical tools
4. **Board Research & Development Committee** -- Oversight of pipeline strategy and major B/R decisions

The complexity of BMS's portfolio -- spanning I/O (Opdivo immune-related AEs plus Opdivo Qvantig subcutaneous bridging B/R), cell therapy (Breyanzi/Abecma CRS management plus FDA-mandated secondary malignancy monitoring and lifelong follow-up), protein degradation (CELMoD agents mezigdomide, golcadomide, iberdomide moving into Phase 3 with emerging safety profiles requiring novel B/R frameworks), anticoagulation (Eliquis bleeding risk management through LOE), and neuroscience (Cobenfy with 14 ongoing/planned studies) -- demands sophisticated, multi-modality B/R governance. The patent cliff intensifies this need: every Growth Portfolio asset's B/R profile must be optimized to maximize lifecycle value as legacy revenues decline.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
**Recommended approach: Enter through cell therapy B/R management, positioning around the unique safety monitoring requirements of CAR-T and next-generation cell therapies.**

BMS has a dedicated Cell Therapy Organization and is committed to cell therapy leadership. The safety profiles of Breyanzi and Abecma (CRS, neurotoxicity, prolonged cytopenias, B-cell aplasia, secondary malignancies) are among the most complex in all of pharma. Next-generation programs (allogeneic, armed CAR-T, gene editing, solid tumor expansion) will add entirely new safety dimensions. The narrative should center on:

1. **Cell therapy as the ultimate B/R use case -- now with regulatory urgency** -- No drug class requires more continuous, real-time B/R monitoring than cell therapies. The FDA's class-wide CAR-T secondary malignancy investigation (boxed warning required January 2024, lifelong monitoring mandated) has elevated B/R monitoring from a best practice to a regulatory imperative. While BMS reports no CAR-positive T-cell malignancy cases across 4,700+ patients, the 15-year follow-up studies and ongoing FDA surveillance mean BMS's PV function must maintain continuous, data-driven B/R assessment for Breyanzi and Abecma indefinitely. Dynamic, probabilistic B/R tools are no longer aspirational -- they are operationally necessary.
2. **Supporting the "Predict First" paradigm** -- BMS's stated AI philosophy is to "predict first." A probabilistic approval prediction and dynamic B/R platform is the embodiment of this approach applied to safety governance. This creates natural cultural alignment. The Workbench adoption curve (under 100 to 900 users in 3 months) proves BMS's workforce can adopt AI tools rapidly when value is demonstrated.
3. **New CMO as a champion** -- Cristian Massacesi arrived from AstraZeneca (where Evinova was built) and within 6 months signed the Evinova AI clinical trial partnership, stating that transforming clinical development is "an urgent necessity." His cross-industry experience with AI safety tools, combined with his explicit mandate to improve pipeline success rates during a critical patent cliff, makes him a natural champion for AI-enabled B/R assessment. Positioning ArcaScience through the Massacesi lens -- as the safety/B/R complement to the Evinova clinical operations tool -- creates a compelling narrative.
4. **Patent cliff portfolio management** -- As Revlimid and Eliquis face LOEs (Legacy Portfolio down 15% to $21.8B in FY2025), BMS needs to maximize the value of its Growth Portfolio ($26.4B, +17%). Dynamic B/R assessment accelerates regulatory approvals for label expansions (Opdivo Qvantig subcutaneous bridging, new Sotyktu indications including psoriatic arthritis PDUFA March 2026, Opdivo+AVD in Hodgkin Lymphoma PDUFA April 2026) and supports lifecycle B/R communication to payers and HCPs. Every Growth Portfolio asset's B/R profile must be optimized.
5. **Workbench + Evinova precedent demonstrates rapid external AI adoption** -- BMS has proven it can deploy AI tools at scale across its organization (Workbench: 900 users in 3 months) and is willing to partner externally for AI capabilities (Evinova, NVIDIA, VantAI). ArcaScience should position as the safety/B/R module complementing BMS's existing clinical operations and drug discovery AI stack.

A strong secondary entry is through the protein degradation platform, where first-in-class CELMoD agents (golcadomide showing 94% ORR in FL at EHA 2025, mezigdomide entering Phase 3 with SUCCESSOR-1 and SUCCESSOR-2 readouts expected 2026, BMS-986458 BCL6 degrader with first clinical data) have limited safety precedent and would benefit enormously from probabilistic B/R modeling as they advance into later-stage trials with emerging safety data.
