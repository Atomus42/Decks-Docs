# Biogen

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** $9.7B (2% decline YoY; Q4 showed 3% growth)
- **Headquarters:** Cambridge, Massachusetts, USA
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Neuroscience/neurodegeneration (Alzheimer's, MS, SMA, ALS), rare diseases (Friedreich ataxia, SMA), immunology (lupus, myasthenia gravis), nephrology (IgAN, AMR, PMN), mental health (postpartum depression)
- **Pipeline Size:** ~49 clinical-stage programs (15 preclinical, 14 Phase 1, 11 Phase 2, 6 Phase 3, 2 NDA/BLA); late-stage pipeline described as having "multi-billion dollar potential"; key collaborations with Eisai (LEQEMBI), Ionis (QALSODY/antisense), UCB (dapirolizumab pegol), Sage (ZURZUVAE)
- **Strategic Priorities:** "Delivering the New Biogen" -- next chapter of innovation and growth; neuroscience leadership with expanding immunology and rare disease portfolio; LEQEMBI commercialization and subcutaneous formulation development; SPINRAZA higher dose regimen; Friedreich ataxia franchise (SKYCLARYS); nephrology entry via HI-Bio acquisition (felzartamab); lupus pipeline (dapirolizumab pegol, litifilimab); "Fit for Growth" efficiency program; research transformation toward external innovation model; $2B manufacturing investment in Research Triangle Park

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Biogen's clinical development is organized across three strategic pillars: neurology, immunology, and rare disease. The R&D organization is led by an EVP/Head of Research and a separate clinical development leadership structure. The company has undergone significant portfolio rationalization, discontinuing programs in Alzheimer's (beyond LEQEMBI), Parkinson's disease, multiple system atrophy, and diabetic peripheral neuropathic pain to concentrate resources on high-value programs. The nephrology entry (HI-Bio acquisition, $1.15B) added felzartamab as a Breakthrough Therapy Designation asset. Key partnerships drive much of the late-stage pipeline: LEQEMBI with Eisai, dapirolizumab pegol with UCB, QALSODY/salanersen with Ionis, and ZURZUVAE with Sage/Supernus. Research transformation is underway to build a preclinical portfolio targeting dysregulated immune cells.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs manages a complex portfolio of partnership-driven and internally developed programs. Key regulatory activities include: LEQEMBI subcutaneous BLA (PDUFA date August 2025); LEQEMBI EU marketing authorization (complicated by CHMP safety information request); SPINRAZA higher dose sNDA/EMA validation; SKYCLARYS global expansion; felzartamab Breakthrough Therapy pathway; and dapirolizumab pegol regulatory planning after positive Phase 3. The LEQEMBI EU regulatory experience -- where the EC requested CHMP reconsideration of new safety data after initial positive opinion -- highlights the dynamic nature of B/R assessment in neurodegeneration and the critical importance of responsive B/R governance.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV at Biogen manages safety across a portfolio with some of the most complex safety monitoring requirements in the industry. LEQEMBI requires monitoring for amyloid-related imaging abnormalities (ARIA), a novel and serious safety concern that prompted the EU regulatory uncertainty. SPINRAZA safety monitoring in SMA patients (including infants) requires specialized pediatric PV capabilities. The MS franchise (TECFIDERA, TYSABRI) involves well-characterized but serious safety risks (PML with TYSABRI). ZURZUVAE for postpartum depression has unique safety considerations in breastfeeding populations. The nephrology pipeline (felzartamab, anti-CD38) introduces immunosuppression-related safety monitoring. Partnership structures (Eisai for LEQEMBI, UCB for dapirolizumab pegol) require inter-company PV coordination.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance is a defining organizational capability for Biogen given the therapeutic areas it operates in. Alzheimer's disease B/R (LEQEMBI) is among the most scrutinized in all of medicine -- the modest clinical benefits must be weighed against ARIA risks, and the CHMP's request for reconsideration of safety data demonstrates how dynamic B/R assessment is in this space. The "Fit for Growth" program and portfolio rationalization represent enterprise-level B/R decisions about where to invest limited resources. B/R governance must span the entire risk spectrum: from the very high safety bar for ZURZUVAE in postpartum depression (relatively mild condition, vulnerable population) to the more risk-tolerant framework for QALSODY in ALS (progressive fatal disease).

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
ArcaScience should lead with the LEQEMBI ARIA challenge. The EU regulatory experience -- where the CHMP requested reconsideration of new safety data after an initial positive opinion -- is the most vivid demonstration in recent pharmaceutical history of why static B/R assessment is inadequate. As LEQEMBI launches globally and real-world data on ARIA accumulates from diverse patient populations (different ApoE genotypes, different comorbidity profiles), the B/R profile will evolve continuously. ArcaScience's dynamic BRA platform is designed for exactly this scenario: continuously integrating new safety data (ARIA incidence, severity, outcomes) with efficacy evidence (cognitive decline trajectories) to maintain a real-time B/R assessment that can preempt regulatory challenges rather than react to them. The pitch: had Biogen/Eisai had ArcaScience's dynamic BRA during the EU submission, the CHMP safety data request could have been anticipated and addressed proactively. For the future, as LEQEMBI's subcutaneous formulation launches and the patient population expands, ArcaScience can serve as the real-time B/R intelligence layer that supports continuous regulatory dialogue with the EMA, FDA, and global regulators. The neuroscience-immunology convergence creates a second entry angle: Biogen's expanding immunology portfolio (lupus, nephrology) brings immune-mediated safety risks that interact with the existing neuroscience safety profile (immunosuppression and neurodegeneration). ArcaScience can model cross-portfolio safety patterns. Propose a pilot on LEQEMBI ARIA monitoring: create a dynamic BRA model that integrates clinical trial data, real-world evidence, and genetic risk factors (ApoE genotype) to continuously update the ARIA risk-benefit profile.
