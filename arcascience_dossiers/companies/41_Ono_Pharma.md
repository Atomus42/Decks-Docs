# Ono Pharmaceutical

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** ~$3.6B (¥374.6B for 9 months ended Dec 2024; annualized ~¥500B; includes royalties from Opdivo/BMS and Keytruda/Merck)
- **Headquarters:** Osaka, Japan (with integration of U.S. operations into Deciphera hub in Cambridge, MA)
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Oncology (immunotherapy, GIST, BRAF-mutant tumors), immunology (autoimmune diseases, allergy), neurology, specialty areas
- **Pipeline Size:** 17+ oncology pipeline programs; 11 global clinical-stage candidates; Opdivo royalties (via BMS), Keytruda royalties (via Merck); key acquisitions include Deciphera Pharmaceuticals ($2.4B) adding QINLOCK and ROMVIMZA
- **Strategic Priorities:** Transformation to Global Specialty Pharma by 2031; ¥200B annual R&D investment target by FY2031; Deciphera integration as global business hub (R&D + commercial in U.S./Europe); post-Opdivo patent expiration (2031) pipeline diversification; open innovation with academia and startups; biopharmaceutical development focus

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Ono is undergoing a major organizational transformation of its clinical development structure. As of July 2025, key functions of Ono Pharma USA (Cambridge, MA) — including U.S. R&D, commercial, and medical affairs, plus European R&D — are being integrated into Deciphera Pharmaceuticals, LLC. Deciphera becomes the business hub for R&D and commercial operations in the U.S. and Europe across multiple therapeutic areas. Japan-based clinical development continues from the Osaka headquarters, Tsukuba (Ibaraki), and Minase research centers. Clinical development spans oncology (Opdivo combinations, anti-CCR8, Axl/Mer inhibitors, EP4 antagonists), immunology (PD-1xCD3 bispecifics), and neurology.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs operates across PMDA (Japan), FDA (U.S.), and EMA (EU). With the Deciphera integration, U.S. and EU regulatory activities will be managed through the Deciphera hub. PMDA submissions remain led from Japan. The team manages regulatory strategies for both proprietary programs and partnered products (BMS for Opdivo combinations, Vertex for povetacicept).

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV manages safety for Opdivo (one of the most widely used immuno-oncology agents globally), QINLOCK, ROMVIMZA, and the broader portfolio. Opdivo safety monitoring generates enormous volumes of safety data globally, though BMS manages primary post-marketing PV for markets where they commercialize. Ono's PV team handles Japan-specific safety monitoring and contributes to global safety databases.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance is managed through the R&D leadership and the corporate governance structure (Audit & Supervisory Board system). The Board of Directors maintains a skill matrix for required competencies. The transformation from a Japan-centric to a global company creates the need for harmonized B/R governance across Japan, U.S., and EU operations.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
ArcaScience should target the Ono-Deciphera integration as the primary entry point. The creation of a global R&D and commercial hub presents a rare opportunity to embed B/R infrastructure during organizational formation. The pitch should emphasize: (1) as Ono transforms from a Japan-centric to a global specialty pharma, a unified BRA platform ensures consistent B/R assessment across PMDA, FDA, and EMA submissions — critical for the 11 global clinical-stage programs, (2) the Deciphera integration brings QINLOCK and ROMVIMZA with their own clinical data sets that need to be integrated with Ono's broader oncology portfolio for cross-program B/R insights, (3) the impending Opdivo patent expiration (2031) makes de-risking the post-Opdivo pipeline existentially important — probabilistic approval prediction helps Ono allocate the growing ¥200B R&D budget optimally. The 35% YoY R&D budget increase signals clear investment appetite. The Deciphera hub in Cambridge, MA may be the most accessible entry point, as U.S.-based biotech leadership tends to be receptive to AI-powered tools. Engage through ASCO, AACR, or through the Deciphera organization directly.
