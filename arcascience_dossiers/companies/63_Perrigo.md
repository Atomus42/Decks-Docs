# Perrigo

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** $4.37 billion (down 6.06% YoY); CSCA segment $3.9B (67%), International segment contributing remaining; adjusted operating income of $609M
- **Headquarters:** Dublin, Ireland (operational HQ in Allegan, Michigan, USA)
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Consumer self-care/OTC products — cough, cold, allergy, pain relief, digestive health, nutrition, skin care, women's health, scar care, blister care; largest store-brand OTC player in the US; top 10 OTC company by revenue in Europe
- **Pipeline Size:** OTC product development pipeline; 15 new store-brand products launched in 2024 targeting $800M in annual brand sales; key products include Opill (first OTC oral contraceptive), Nasonex 24HR, Compeed, ellaOne, Mederma; R&D investment of $215M (3.7% of sales); 94 clinical trials associated with the company
- **Strategic Priorities:** "One Perrigo" transformation to pure-play consumer self-care, Project Energize ($140-170M annualized savings by 2026), store-brand product innovation, Rx-to-OTC switch pipeline, portfolio streamlining, operational agility, debt reduction ($3.62B total debt), Stabilize/Streamline/Strengthen 2025-2027 plan

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Perrigo's clinical development focuses on OTC product development and Rx-to-OTC switches rather than traditional drug development. Clinical programs involve bioequivalence studies for store-brand equivalents of national brand OTC products, as well as clinical trials supporting Rx-to-OTC switches (e.g., Opill required extensive consumer behavior studies). The R&D organization develops new formulations and secures regulatory approvals for high-quality store-brand equivalents. Partnerships (e.g., Sol-Gel Technologies for dermatology generics) extend development capabilities.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs is critical for Perrigo's business model, managing OTC monograph compliance, ANDA filings for store-brand equivalents, and NDA/sNDA filings for Rx-to-OTC switches. The Opill approval (first OTC daily hormonal birth control) required extensive FDA engagement including advisory committee review. The regulatory team manages product registrations across the US (FDA), Europe (national agencies), and other markets. Consumer self-care regulatory pathways differ from traditional pharmaceutical pathways.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV for OTC products differs from prescription drug PV but remains substantial given the massive consumer reach (4,500+ OTC products). The OTC context involves monitoring adverse events from consumer self-medication, which presents unique challenges (underreporting, off-label consumer use, drug interactions). Opill's OTC status for hormonal contraception creates heightened PV requirements. The company also manufactures infant formula, adding FDA safety oversight complexity.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance at Perrigo centers on the Rx-to-OTC switch paradigm, where demonstrating that consumers can safely and effectively self-manage medications without healthcare provider oversight is the core B/R question. This is a fundamentally different B/R framework than prescription drugs, focusing on consumer comprehension, self-selection accuracy, and population-level safety in unsupervised use. The FDA advisory committee review process for Rx-to-OTC switches is an explicit B/R evaluation.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
Perrigo represents a non-traditional target for ArcaScience, but the Rx-to-OTC switch pipeline is a compelling niche use case. Switches like Opill require demonstrating that consumers can safely self-manage medications previously available only by prescription, which involves a unique B/R framework. If ArcaScience can adapt its platform to incorporate consumer behavior data, self-selection accuracy, and population-level OTC safety monitoring alongside traditional clinical evidence, it could position as a differentiated tool for Rx-to-OTC switch decisions. However, the company's financial challenges ($3.62B debt, Project Energize cost cuts) and fundamentally different B/R context make this a lower-priority target compared to prescription drug companies. If pursuing, engage through the Regulatory Affairs team focused on Rx-to-OTC switch opportunities, framing the BRA platform as a tool that integrates clinical efficacy data with consumer use data to strengthen switch applications.
