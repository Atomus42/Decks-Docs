## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Galderma's clinical development is organized around its three product categories — Injectable Aesthetics, Dermatological Skincare, and Therapeutic Dermatology — with R&D activities distributed across six sites globally. The therapeutic dermatology pipeline is the primary clinical development focus, led by the nemolizumab (Nemluvio) franchise. The ARCADIA Phase III program (1,728 patients with moderate-to-severe AD) demonstrated the registration-grade capabilities of the clinical development organization. New clinical trials for nemolizumab in systemic sclerosis and chronic pruritus of unknown origin were initiated in 2025. Relfydess (RelabotulinumtoxinA) completed Phase III and European decentralized procedures.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs manages submissions across FDA, EMA (decentralized procedure), and national agencies in 100+ countries. In 2024, the team achieved FDA approval of Nemluvio for both prurigo nodularis and atopic dermatitis, plus European positive CHMP opinion. Relfydess European decentralized procedure was completed with positive decision. National regulatory approvals are being finalized across 17+ markets. The regulatory team must coordinate across both therapeutic drug and aesthetic medical device regulatory pathways.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV manages safety across therapeutic biologics (Nemluvio), neuromodulators (Relfydess), injectable hyaluronic acid fillers (Restylane), and topical/OTC products (Cetaphil). The 2024 Nemluvio approvals significantly expanded PV responsibilities into biologic safety monitoring, including long-term immunogenicity surveillance. The aesthetic product portfolio has distinct safety considerations (injection-site reactions, filler complications) managed through medical affairs and PV jointly.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance operates under the Executive Committee chaired by the CEO. As a newly public company (IPO March 2024), governance structures are being formalized with institutional investor expectations. The pure-play dermatology focus means all B/R decisions sit within a single therapeutic domain, creating potential for a unified B/R approach across the portfolio.
