# Santen Pharmaceutical

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** JPY 300.0 billion (~$2.0 billion)
- **Headquarters:** Osaka, Japan (global operations in 60+ countries)
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Ophthalmology exclusively — glaucoma & ocular hypertension, myopia, allergic conjunctivitis, ptosis, retinal diseases, vernal keratoconjunctivitis, dry eye, corneal dystrophy, meibomian gland dysfunction
- **Pipeline Size:** 10+ clinical-stage programs across glaucoma, myopia, ptosis, retinal diseases, allergic conjunctivitis, and corneal conditions; prescription ophthalmic pharmaceuticals account for >80% of global sales
- **Strategic Priorities:** Ophthalmology specialization (pure-play since 2015 divestiture of anti-rheumatic business), myopia as new growth area (Ryjunea approved in EU), global manufacturing expansion (Santen Vision Park, Suzhou), regional growth strategies (Japan, EMEA, Asia), innovation in drug delivery and formulation technologies

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Santen's clinical development is exclusively focused on ophthalmology, giving it deep therapeutic expertise. Programs span multiple modalities including small molecules, prostaglandin derivatives, ROCK inhibitors, muscarinic antagonists, alpha-adrenergic agonists, immunosuppressants, and bispecific fusion proteins (licensed from RemeGen). Clinical development operates across Japan, EMEA, and Asia Pacific with trials conducted globally. The company leverages both internal R&D and in-licensing partnerships (RVL Pharmaceuticals for ptosis, RemeGen for retinal diseases, Alcon for ROCK inhibitors).

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs manages filings across Japan (PMDA), EU (EMA), and Asian markets. The company navigates different regulatory environments for the same molecules (e.g., separate filing strategies for glaucoma treatments in Japan, Europe, and Asia). The EU approval of Ryjunea as the first drug for myopia progression in Europe was a landmark regulatory achievement. PMDA filings for ptosis (December 2024) and parallel EU Phase 3 initiation demonstrate global regulatory coordination.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV is focused exclusively on ophthalmic products, creating deep expertise in ocular safety monitoring. The global portfolio serves approximately 50 million people, generating significant PV data. Santen's products include chronic therapies (glaucoma) requiring long-term safety monitoring and new therapeutic areas (myopia in children) requiring pediatric PV capabilities. Manufacturing operations (including the new Suzhou facility with 800M+ bottle annual capacity) connect to quality and safety oversight.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance benefits from Santen's pure-play ophthalmology focus, allowing consistent application of ophthalmic-specific B/R frameworks. The company's strategy of expanding into new ophthalmic therapeutic areas (myopia, ptosis, retinal diseases) requires B/R assessment for novel indications. In-licensing decisions (RemeGen bispecific for retinal diseases, RVL for ptosis) involve B/R evaluation. NHI price revisions in Japan (~JPY 6.0B impact in FY2024) create payer-level B/R discussions.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
ArcaScience should position its BRA platform for Santen's expansion into novel ophthalmic therapeutic areas where B/R assessment is most critical. The first-in-class myopia drug (Ryjunea/low-dose atropine) for pediatric use represents the highest-value entry point: pediatric B/R assessment requires integrating long-term safety data in a population with decades of potential exposure, and there is limited precedent for regulatory and payer evaluation. The retinal disease pipeline (RemeGen bispecific fusion protein targeting VEGF/FGF) represents another high-complexity B/R challenge in a competitive market (vs. anti-VEGF incumbents). Position ArcaScience as enabling Santen to maintain its deep ophthalmology expertise advantage while expanding into new indications where B/R complexity increases. The platform's ability to integrate clinical trial and real-world evidence is particularly valuable for a company that serves 50 million patients globally and can leverage that RWE for B/R insights. Engage through the corporate R&D leadership in Osaka or through EMEA development teams where the myopia and ptosis programs are most active.
