# Innovent Biologics

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** RMB 9.42B (~$1.3B; 51.8% YoY growth; first-ever Non-IFRS net profit and positive EBITDA)
- **Headquarters:** Suzhou, Jiangsu Province, China (building US lab in San Francisco)
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Oncology (IO + ADC strategy; PD-1, CLDN18.2, TROP2, B7H3, HER3, HER2 ADCs; bispecifics), autoimmune diseases (CD40L, OX40L, IL-4Ra/TSLP), cardiovascular/metabolic (mazdutide for obesity/T2D, tafolecimab/SINTBILO for hyperlipidemia), ophthalmology (IBI311 for thyroid eye disease)
- **Pipeline Size:** 36 new drug candidates; 22 in oncology; 15 approved products; 3 NDA under review, 4 in Phase III/pivotal, 15+ in early clinical stage; targets RMB 20B product revenue by 2027 and 5 global MRCT Phase 3 assets by 2030; 60,000L + 80,000L manufacturing capacity
- **Strategic Priorities:** Global expansion (IO + ADC "one-two punch" strategy); profitability achievement (first positive year); mazdutide obesity/diabetes franchise building; US development expansion (San Francisco lab); 30+ global healthcare partnerships (Eli Lilly, Sanofi, Incyte, LG Chem)

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Innovent has built a comprehensive clinical development organization supporting 36 pipeline candidates across oncology, autoimmune, cardiovascular/metabolic, and ophthalmology. The oncology pipeline is organized around two core platforms: immuno-oncology (IO) and antibody-drug conjugates (ADC), with the strategic "IO + ADC" combination approach. Clinical development leadership includes a Senior Vice President of Oncology Development. The company is building global clinical development capability with US expansion (San Francisco lab) and targets 5 global MRCT Phase 3 assets by 2030. Manufacturing infrastructure (60,000L antibody + 80,000L under construction) supports clinical supply needs.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs manages NMPA filings with growing international capability. Three NDAs were under review at year-end (2 for mazdutide, 1 for IBI311). The team has delivered 15 product approvals in China. International regulatory capability is expanding through partnerships (Eli Lilly, Sanofi, Incyte) and direct US operations. MRCT (multi-regional clinical trial) design capability supports simultaneous global regulatory submissions.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV manages safety monitoring for 15 approved products and a large active clinical trial portfolio. The expansion from oncology (immunotherapy safety profiles) into metabolic (mazdutide for obesity) and autoimmune areas requires diversified safety expertise. Partnership obligations with 30+ global companies add international safety reporting requirements.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance spans a rapidly growing portfolio that has reached profitability for the first time. The target of RMB 20B revenue by 2027 (from RMB 9.4B in 2024) requires aggressive but disciplined pipeline advancement decisions. The "IO + ADC" strategy involves complex combination B/R assessment for oncology programs. BD deal decisions (30+ partnerships) involve B/R evaluation of assets for in-licensing and out-licensing.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
Innovent's first profitable year, aggressive revenue targets (RMB 20B by 2027), and global MRCT ambitions (5 global Phase 3 assets by 2030) create a critical need for sophisticated B/R governance that scales with growth. ArcaScience should position the BRA platform as: (1) an "IO + ADC" combination optimizer that models B/R profiles for the strategic combination approach across 22 oncology candidates, (2) a global MRCT design tool that supports multi-regional clinical trial planning with integrated regulatory intelligence for NMPA, FDA, and EMA, and (3) a mazdutide franchise strategy tool that supports B/R-informed indication expansion into the massive obesity/T2D market. The newly achieved profitability and substantial cash position (RMB 10.2B) provide budget capacity. The San Francisco lab expansion offers a potential US-based entry point. A pilot focusing on the "IO + ADC" combination strategy — where B/R modeling of immunotherapy plus ADC combinations across different tumor types demonstrates immediate clinical decision value — would align perfectly with Innovent's differentiated strategic position.
