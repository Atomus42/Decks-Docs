# Pierre Fabre

## 1. Company Overview

- **Headquarters:** Castres, France (southwest France; new Campus Chartreuse headquarters operational in 2026)
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Oncology (targeted therapies — colorectal, breast, lung, skin cancer, hematologic malignancies; 39% of Medical Care revenue; EUR 520M oncology revenue), cell therapy (tabelecleucel/EBVALLO for EBV+ PTLD), dermatology/dermo-cosmetics (Eau Thermale Avene, Ducray; 15% of Medical Care), primary care, family healthcare
- **Pipeline Size:** 10+ R&D programs in targeted oncology; 9 clinical trials across 19 countries; 32 real-world evidence studies; key pipeline assets include PFL-002 (c-MET degrader, Phase I/II), PFL-241 (4th-gen EGFR inhibitor), STX-721 (EGFR exon 20), exarafenib (pan-RAF inhibitor), tabelecleucel (allogeneic T-cell immunotherapy for EBV+ PTLD, BLA under FDA review — received second CRL January 2026); R&D budget EUR 219M (60% allocated to oncology)
- **Strategic Priorities:** Oncology franchise expansion (targeted therapies); U.S. pharmaceutical market entry via Pierre Fabre Pharmaceuticals (CEO: Adriana Herrera); precision oncology and cell therapy (tabelecleucel BLA pathway); dermo-cosmetics growth (Avene brand); manufacturing in France (90%+ production); AI-driven drug discovery (expanded Iktos generative AI partnership, January 2026); enterprise-wide AI deployment (Microsoft Azure, internal GenAI platform, Oncoguide for pharmacists); employee shareholding model; Foundation-controlled governance
- **Key Leadership (2025-2026):** Eric Ducournau (Group CEO, awarded Legion of Honour July 2025); Marie-Andree Gamache (new CEO Medical Care, appointed March 2025, ex-Novartis UK/Ireland); Adriana Herrera (CEO, Pierre Fabre Pharmaceuticals U.S.)

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Pierre Fabre's clinical development is organized around the Medical Care division (oncology, dermatology, primary care, family health), now led by new CEO Marie-Andree Gamache (appointed March 2025, ex-Novartis UK/Ireland Country President). R&D is conducted primarily at 5 innovation centers, including the main oncology R&D center on the Toulouse Oncopole campus. The oncology R&D team is 100% focused on targeted therapies from discovery through clinical trials. Key partnerships drive the pipeline: Scorpion Therapeutics (EGFR inhibitors), RedRidge Bio (biparatopic antibody drug candidates), and Iktos (expanded AI-driven generative drug discovery collaboration announced January 2026, integrating Iktos' Makya and Spaya platforms into Pierre Fabre's oncology R&D). The U.S. subsidiary (Pierre Fabre Pharmaceuticals, CEO: Adriana Herrera) has assumed full global responsibility for tabelecleucel following the November 2025 BLA transfer from Atara Biotherapeutics, making Pierre Fabre directly accountable for all clinical development, regulatory, commercial, and manufacturing activities for this allogeneic T-cell immunotherapy.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs manages EMA, FDA, and emerging market regulatory submissions, with rapidly expanding FDA capabilities through the U.S. subsidiary. The tabelecleucel (EBVALLO) BLA pathway has been the most consequential FDA regulatory engagement: the initial BLA (submitted by Atara, May 2024) received a CRL in January 2025 over manufacturing inspection issues (no clinical or safety deficiencies); following resolution, the resubmitted BLA was accepted with Priority Review and accelerated approval status in July 2025; Pierre Fabre assumed full BLA ownership in November 2025; however, a second CRL was issued January 9, 2026, with the FDA stating it no longer considers the single-arm ALLELE study adequate for accelerated approval and requesting a new study — a significant and unexpected shift. Pierre Fabre has publicly characterized this as having "far-reaching consequences for the development of rare disease treatments." Updated Phase 3 ALLELE data were presented at ASH 2025 (December), including sub-analyses of pediatric patients and by prior therapy. The regulatory team also manages European approvals for BRAFTOVI + MEKTOVI in BRAF V600E NSCLC. EBVALLO remains marketed in Europe as the first allogeneic cell therapy.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV manages safety across oncology targeted therapies (BRAF/MEK inhibitors, cell therapy), dermatological products, and primary care products. The oncology PV capability handles targeted therapy-specific safety profiles (skin toxicities, cardiac effects from BRAF/MEK inhibition). Cell therapy (tabelecleucel/EBVALLO) introduces unique PV requirements — notably, the FDA's January 2026 CRL raised no safety concerns, validating the PV team's safety data package even as the efficacy evidence threshold shifted. With Pierre Fabre now holding full global PV responsibility for tabelecleucel (transferred from Atara November 2025), the PV function has expanded significantly to manage allogeneic T-cell immunotherapy safety across all markets. The company has deployed Oncoguide, an AI-powered platform used by 3,400+ French retail pharmacists to support cancer patients, integrating AI into patient safety monitoring. With 32 ongoing real-world evidence studies, PV has substantial RWE data integration responsibilities. The broad dermo-cosmetics portfolio has distinct cosmetovigilance requirements.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance operates under the Foundation-controlled governance structure. Pierre Fabre's majority shareholder (86%) is the Pierre Fabre Foundation (humanitarian foundation), with employees holding 9.5% via the Ruscus scheme. This unique ownership structure ensures long-term, patient-centric B/R decision-making without shareholder pressure. The Supervisory Board oversees B/R governance through the Audit Committee and other committees. The tabelecleucel FDA experience (two CRLs, shifting regulatory requirements) has created heightened urgency around robust B/R evidence frameworks, particularly for rare disease and cell therapy programs where single-arm studies face increasing regulatory scrutiny. The March 2025 appointment of Marie-Andree Gamache as Medical Care CEO (bringing Novartis-level B/R governance experience) and the expanded Executive Committee may signal a strengthening of B/R governance structures.
