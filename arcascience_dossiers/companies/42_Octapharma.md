# Octapharma

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** EUR 3.47B (~$3.8B; record; +6.1% YoY)
- **Headquarters:** Lachen, Switzerland
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Hematology (hemophilia A, B, von Willebrand disease), immunotherapy (immunodeficiency, autoimmune disease), critical care (albumin, clotting factors for ICU/emergency medicine)
- **Pipeline Size:** Multiple Phase 3 programs across hematology, immunology, and critical care; VWF concentrate prophylaxis (first ever FDA approval for VWD prophylaxis, Dec 2023); nanobody research from camelid-derived antibodies; recombinant protein development
- **Strategic Priorities:** 50% production capacity increase by 2028; AI-powered Advanced Analytics across production sites; expansion into new markets; data-driven company transformation; plasma donation center expansion (190+ centers globally); company-owned plasma supply (88% from own centers); yield improvement and CPL reduction

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Octapharma's clinical development is organized across its three therapeutic areas: Hematology, Immunotherapy, and Critical Care. The company operates seven R&D sites globally, with preclinical plasma research in Vienna (Austria) and clinical R&D sites in Vienna, Lachen (Switzerland), and Hoboken, New Jersey (USA). A molecular biochemistry group in Berlin develops technologies for examining protein structure and function. A global virus and prion validation group operates from Frankfurt, Germany. Clinical trials are conducted across Europe, Canada, U.S., Russia, Asia, and Japan. The company is pioneering nanobody research using camelid-derived antibodies (from camels and llamas).

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs manages FDA, EMA, and global submissions for plasma-derived biologics. The team navigates specialized blood product regulatory frameworks that differ from conventional pharmaceutical regulation. Recent major achievement: FDA extended approval for VWF concentrate to include prophylaxis across all forms of VWD — requiring the largest-ever prospective prophylaxis study in VWD (WIL-31). Global regulatory coordination across 120 countries where products are sold.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV manages safety across plasma-derived products with specialized considerations including viral safety, transmissible spongiform encephalopathy risk, immunogenicity (inhibitor development in hemophilia patients), and lot-to-lot consistency. The global virus and prion validation group in Frankfurt directly supports PV with safety testing. PV also monitors the safety of immunoglobulin products used in autoimmune conditions where safety profiles differ from immunodeficiency use.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance is led by the management board (family-owned structure with Wolfgang Marguerre as Chairman/CEO and family members on the management board). For plasma-derived products, B/R assessment integrates manufacturing quality data (viral safety, protein integrity) with clinical efficacy and safety data. The company's private, family-owned nature allows long-term B/R considerations without public market pressure.
