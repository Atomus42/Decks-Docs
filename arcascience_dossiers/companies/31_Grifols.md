## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Grifols' clinical development is led by the Chief Scientific Innovation Officer and organized across three business divisions: Biopharma (plasma-derived medicines), Diagnostic (transfusion medicine), and Bio Supplies. The company operates 12 R&D centers globally with 1,200+ researchers. Clinical programs span traditional plasma-derived products (fibrinogen, immunoglobulins, alpha-1 antitrypsin, albumin) and emerging modalities (GigaGen recombinant polyclonal antibodies). The Biotest integration has yielded major milestones: FESILTY (fibrinogen concentrate) received FDA approval in December 2025 for congenital fibrinogen deficiency and PRUFIBRY launched in Germany/Austria for both acquired and congenital fibrinogen deficiency. Yimmugo (IVIg) launched commercially in the U.S. in October 2025 following its June 2024 FDA approval, distributed by Kedrion under a seven-year agreement with a $1B U.S. revenue forecast. XEMBIFY (subcutaneous IG) grew 59.5% cc in FY2025, and overall IG revenues increased 14.7% cc, outperforming the market. Trimodulin remains in late-stage development for community-acquired pneumonia.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs manages submissions across FDA, EMA, PMDA, and multiple other agencies. The regulatory team handles both biologics (plasma-derived products) and diagnostics regulatory pathways. The team achieved a landmark year in 2025: FDA approval of FESILTY (Dec 2025) for congenital fibrinogen deficiency (U.S. rollout planned Q2 2026), German national approval of PRUFIBRY for both acquired and congenital fibrinogen deficiency, and FDA approval to manufacture Gel Cards and reagent Red Blood Cells at the San Diego diagnostics facility. Egypt's GEPD became the first fully integrated, EMA-certified end-to-end plasma ecosystem in Africa and the Middle East — a notable regulatory achievement for Grifols' global operations. The regulatory team is also managing FESILTY's anticipated broader label expansion (acquired fibrinogen deficiency, an estimated $800M U.S. market opportunity).

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV manages safety monitoring across a large portfolio of plasma-derived products with decades of post-marketing history. Safety considerations for plasma products include viral safety (despite extensive pathogen reduction processes), immunogenicity, and lot-to-lot consistency. The diagnostic division also has safety/quality responsibilities. Grifols has a dedicated global virus and prion validation group in Frankfurt, Germany, reflecting the critical importance of safety in plasma therapeutics. The 2025 launches of FESILTY, PRUFIBRY, and Yimmugo in the U.S. significantly expand the PV workload: three new products entering diverse patient populations simultaneously require robust post-marketing surveillance programs, adverse event reporting infrastructure, and real-world safety data collection. The Egypt GEPD fully integrated ecosystem adds a new geographic PV dimension in Africa and the Middle East.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance involves the Senior Executive Leadership Team (SELT), established in 2023. For plasma-derived products, B/R decisions incorporate manufacturing quality data (lot consistency, viral safety) alongside clinical efficacy data. The governance reform separating ownership from management (completed 2024) has professionalized B/R decision-making with independent oversight. However, the ongoing Brookfield take-private discussions (initially rejected at EUR 6.45B in Nov 2024; renewed preliminary talks in 2025 via family-linked investment vehicles Deria, Ponder Trade, and Radellor) create corporate governance uncertainty that could affect long-term B/R investment decisions. The three credit rating upgrades in late 2025/early 2026 (Moody's, S&P, Fitch) signal improving financial governance and reduced credit risk, potentially freeing capital for R&D and safety infrastructure investments.
