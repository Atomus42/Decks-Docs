# Fosun Pharma

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** ~RMB 42B (~$5.8B; H1 2024 revenue RMB 20.46B, up 5.31% YoY ex-COVID products; pharmaceutical manufacturing R&D 16.98% of segment revenue)
- **Headquarters:** Shanghai, China
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Solid tumors (lung, breast, gastric, colorectal cancer), hematological tumors, immuno-inflammatory diseases; cell therapy (Fosun Kairos/formerly Fosun Kite); biosimilars (trastuzumab/Han Qu You); medical devices; medical diagnosis; healthcare services
- **Pipeline Size:** 80+ major pipeline projects (innovative drugs and biosimilars by indication); 220 patent applications in 2024; key assets include serplulimab/Han Si Zhuang (anti-PD-1, approved in 30+ countries including EU), Han Qu You (trastuzumab, approved in 50+ countries), HLX22 (HER2 mAb for gastric cancer, Phase III), HLX78/lasofoxifene (breast cancer, Phase III), RT002/DaxibotulinumtoxinA; cell therapy platform via Fosun Kairos
- **Strategic Priorities:** Innovation-driven and global development strategy; AI adoption across R&D; antibody/ADC, cell therapy, and small molecule platform enhancement; globalization of innovative products (30+ countries for serplulimab); operating cash flow improvement (+31.13% YoY in 2024); radiopharmaceuticals, RNA therapeutics, gene editing exploration

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Fosun Pharma operates a diversified clinical development organization spanning independently developed drugs, co-developed products, licensed-in assets, and cell therapies. Development is organized around core therapeutic areas (solid tumors, hematological tumors, immuno-inflammatory diseases) with specialized platforms (antibody/ADC, cell therapy, small molecules). The Fosun Kairos subsidiary (100% owned after September 2024 acquisition of the remaining 50% stake in former Fosun Kite) serves as the dedicated cell therapy platform. Multiple international multi-center Phase III trials are active (HLX22, HLX78). The company employs a multi-model R&D approach: independent R&D, cooperative development, license-in, and deep incubation.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs manages a complex global filing portfolio spanning NMPA (China), EMA (EU), FDA (US), and 30+ additional country regulatory agencies. The EMA CHMP positive opinion for serplulimab in ES-SCLC demonstrates advanced European regulatory capability. Han Qu You has navigated regulatory approvals in 50+ countries. The team manages diverse regulatory pathways for innovative drugs, biosimilars, licensed products, and cell therapies.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV monitors safety for a diverse portfolio of marketed products across oncology (serplulimab in 30+ countries, trastuzumab in 50+ countries), cell therapy (CAR-T), and multiple other therapeutic areas. The global footprint requires multi-jurisdictional safety reporting. The cell therapy portfolio demands specialized PV capabilities for managing CAR-T safety profiles including cytokine release syndrome and neurotoxicity monitoring.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance spans a highly diversified portfolio across multiple modalities (small molecules, biologics, biosimilars, cell therapies, medical devices). Portfolio-level decisions involve balancing investment across independently developed vs. licensed-in vs. co-developed programs. The decision to acquire full ownership of Fosun Kite/Kairos ($50% stake) reflects a major B/R-driven strategic commitment to cell therapy.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
Fosun Pharma's unique combination of scale (80+ pipeline projects), diversity (small molecules to cell therapy), and global ambition (products in 50+ countries) creates a need for integrated B/R governance that spans modalities and geographies. ArcaScience should position the BRA platform as: (1) a multi-modality B/R unification tool that creates consistent assessment frameworks across small molecules, biologics, biosimilars, and cell therapies, (2) a global regulatory strategy optimizer that supports multi-jurisdictional filing decisions for products like serplulimab (30+ countries) and Han Qu You (50+ countries), and (3) a cell therapy B/R specialization layer that addresses the unique safety/efficacy considerations of CAR-T products through Fosun Kairos. The global regulatory complexity of managing EMA, FDA, and NMPA filings simultaneously, combined with the diverse modality portfolio, makes Fosun Pharma an account where integrated B/R assessment has exceptionally high value. A pilot could focus on serplulimab's global expansion (evaluating new indications and markets through B/R modeling) given its broad multi-country footprint.
