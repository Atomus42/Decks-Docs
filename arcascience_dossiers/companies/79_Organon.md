# Organon

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** $6.4B (2% as-reported growth, 3% ex-FX vs. FY2023)
- **Headquarters:** Jersey City, New Jersey, USA
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Women's health (contraception, fertility, maternal/peripartum conditions, endometriosis), biosimilars, established brands (cardiovascular, respiratory, dermatology via Vtama/Dermavant acquisition, migraine via Emgality licensing)
- **Pipeline Size:** Focused pipeline across women's health (ebopiprant for pre-term labor, non-hormonal contraception, Jada System for postpartum hemorrhage); Vtama (dermatology acquisition); Emgality/Rayvow (migraine licensing); OG-6219 discontinued in 2024
- **Strategic Priorities:** Building the world's leading women's health pipeline; innovative trial design; portfolio diversification via acquisitions (Dermavant/Vtama); reducing leverage below 4.0x; achieving sustained constant currency revenue growth despite LOE headwinds

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Organon's R&D organization is led by a Head of Research and Development who also serves as Chief Medical Officer. Clinical development is organized around four women's health pillars: contraception, fertility, maternal and peripartum conditions, and conditions predominantly unique to women. The company employs innovative trial design approaches, including decentralized elements (app-based data capture, reduced site visits) and patient-centric design informed by listening workshops with patients. Clinical operations must also support development activities for acquired assets (Vtama, Emgality).

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs is integrated within the R&D organization under the CMO. The team manages global filings for both women's health pipeline assets and established brands across multiple markets. Organon's regulatory capability spans innovative new drug filings, biosimilar regulatory pathways, and lifecycle management for established brands facing LOE challenges. The acquisition of Vtama and licensing of Emgality/Rayvow have expanded the regulatory portfolio beyond women's health.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
As a company with $6.4B in revenues from a broad portfolio of marketed products (Nexplanon, NuvaRing, established brands, biosimilars), Organon maintains a robust pharmacovigilance organization. The PV team inherited substantial infrastructure from the 2021 Merck & Co. spinoff and manages safety monitoring across a diverse product portfolio spanning multiple therapeutic areas and geographies.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
Benefit-risk governance is centralized within the R&D/CMO organization, with portfolio-level decisions involving commercial and finance leadership. The discontinuation of OG-6219 (endometriosis) based on Phase 2 data and subsequent pivot to prioritize other pipeline assets demonstrates active B/R-driven portfolio management. Women's health programs face unique B/R considerations given the historically underserved nature of these patient populations.

## 4. Buying Pathway Hypothesis
- **Interest Initiator:** CMO/Head of R&D — motivated by the need to optimize a focused but high-stakes pipeline after OG-6219 disappointment
- **Scientific Validator:** CMO and women's health clinical development leads
- **Budget Owner:** CMO or CFO — R&D budget constrained by leverage reduction targets, making ROI demonstration critical
- **Procurement Cycle:** 6-9 months; mid-size company with relatively flat organizational structure enables faster decision-making
- **Internal Friction Points:** Significant debt load (~4x leverage target) constrains discretionary spending; pipeline setback (OG-6219) may create conservative investment posture; broad portfolio of established brands may compete for PV/safety resources with pipeline programs; recently spun-off company still building independent operational capabilities

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
Organon represents a unique opportunity in the women's health space — one of the few companies with a pipeline nearly wholly dedicated to female-specific healthcare. The 2024 discontinuation of OG-6219 for endometriosis creates urgency for better B/R assessment tools to protect remaining pipeline investments. ArcaScience should position the BRA platform around: (1) de-risking the remaining women's health pipeline (ebopiprant, non-hormonal contraception) through probabilistic B/R modeling that integrates the limited historical clinical data available in underserved women's health indications, (2) supporting innovative trial design approaches (Organon's decentralized trial elements and patient-reported outcomes) by integrating diverse data types into the B/R framework, and (3) evaluating acquisition targets and licensing opportunities through B/R-informed due diligence. The pitch should emphasize cost-efficiency given Organon's leverage constraints — demonstrating that preventing even one late-stage failure (like OG-6219) would pay for the platform many times over. The CMO, who serves as both Head of R&D and top medical leader, is the single most important stakeholder.
