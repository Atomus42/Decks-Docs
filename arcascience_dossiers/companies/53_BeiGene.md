# BeiGene (BeOne Medicines)

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** ~$3.6 billion (full year 2024 product revenue; 2025 guidance of $4.9-5.3B); BeiGene is transitioning its name to BeOne Medicines
- **Headquarters:** Beijing, China and Cambridge, Massachusetts, USA (dual headquarters)
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Oncology (hematology, solid tumors), with focus on BTK inhibitors, PD-1 checkpoint inhibitors, BCL2 inhibitors, antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs), bispecific antibodies, targeted protein degraders (CDACs)
- **Pipeline Size:** 13 new molecular entities (NMEs) entered the clinic in 2024; ~3,700 R&D colleagues; clinical trials in 45+ countries across 6 continents; 70+ trials for TEVIMBRA alone
- **Strategic Priorities:** Global self-commercialization model, next-generation oncology pipeline expansion, BRUKINSA franchise maximization, sonrotoclax (BCL2 inhibitor) as flagship pipeline asset, US biologics manufacturing (Hopewell, NJ), AI-driven drug discovery, "Fast to Proof of Concept" development approach, achieving sustained GAAP profitability

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
BeiGene operates one of the largest oncology clinical development organizations in the industry with ~3,700 R&D colleagues. Clinical development follows a "Fast to Proof of Concept" approach that rapidly generates early clinical data to gate investment decisions, maximizing resources by channeling data-gated investments into the most promising candidates while de-prioritizing others. The company has demonstrated strengths in translational small molecule and biologics discovery with three core platform technologies: multi-specific antibodies, CDACs (chimeric degradation activation compounds), and ADCs. Clinical operations span six continents with trials in 45+ countries.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
BeiGene's regulatory function operates globally, managing interactions with the FDA, EMA, NMPA (China), PMDA (Japan), and regulatory authorities across 45+ markets. BRUKINSA is approved in 65+ markets, and TEVIMBRA in 45 markets. The regulatory team manages complex global submissions including breakthrough therapy designations, priority reviews, and accelerated approvals. The EMA PRIME designation for BGB-16673 (BTK CDAC) signals active regulatory engagement for novel mechanisms.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
Given the scale of BeiGene's global oncology operations (14,000+ patients enrolled in TEVIMBRA trials alone, 1.3M+ patients treated globally), the PV/drug safety function is substantial. Post-marketing pharmacovigilance is critical for BRUKINSA and TEVIMBRA across 65+ and 45+ markets respectively. The transition from clinical-stage to commercial-stage company with multiple marketed products has required significant PV infrastructure build-out, including real-world evidence integration and signal detection across diverse global populations.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
Benefit-risk governance at BeiGene is embedded in its data-gated development approach, where rapid proof-of-concept data drives go/no-go decisions. For a company with 13 NMEs entering the clinic annually, structured B/R evaluation is essential to portfolio prioritization. The company's oncology focus means B/R decisions involve complex assessments of efficacy in serious/life-threatening conditions against safety profiles. Regulatory interactions with multiple health authorities create demand for standardized B/R frameworks.
