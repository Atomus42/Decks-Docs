# Shionogi

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** ~$2.9B (¥438.3B; record revenue for third consecutive year; operating profit ¥156.6B record)
- **Headquarters:** Osaka, Japan (relocating to Grand Green Osaka/Umekita Phase 2 in 2025)
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Infectious disease (antibiotics, antivirals — COVID-19, influenza, HIV, AMR), pain/CNS (depression, Fragile X syndrome, ADHD, Alzheimer's, chronic pain, Parkinson's tremor), metabolic disorders, rare diseases, oncology, stroke
- **Pipeline Size:** 20+ pipeline programs spanning infectious disease, pain/CNS, metabolic disorders, rare diseases, oncology, and stroke; key assets include cefiderocol (Fetroja/Fetcroja), ensitrelvir (Xocova), zuranolone (MDD, NDA filed in Japan), zatolmilast (Fragile X/Alzheimer's), resiniferatoxin (pain), SDT-001 (digital therapeutic for ADHD)
- **Strategic Priorities:** STS2030 (Shionogi Transformation Strategy) medium-term plan; HIV franchise royalty growth (26.6% increase via ViiV Healthcare/GSK); global infectious disease leadership and AMR advocacy; CNS pipeline expansion (zuranolone, zatolmilast); vaccine business growth (¥100B target by 2030); Qpex Biopharma acquisition (xeruborbactam AMR pipeline); JT Pharmaceutical Division acquisition; digital therapeutics (SDT-001 approved for ADHD in Japan)

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Shionogi reorganized its R&D in April 2024, creating a Drug Discovery Research Division (healthcare solution creation stage) and a Pharmaceutical Technology Research Division (CMC research). The Vaccine Business Division was newly established under the Supply Supervisory Unit. Overseas group companies now report to the Healthcare Business Supervisory Unit, centralizing global healthcare business operations. Clinical development spans two primary therapeutic areas: infectious diseases and pain/CNS disorders, with expanding programs in metabolic disorders, rare diseases, oncology, and stroke. The company consists of 43 consolidated subsidiaries, 4 affiliates, and 2 joint ventures. Key partnerships include Sage Therapeutics (zuranolone for Japan/Taiwan/South Korea) and ViiV Healthcare/GSK (HIV royalties).

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs manages PMDA (Japan primary), FDA (U.S.), EMA (EU), NMPA (China), and other global agencies. The team navigates diverse regulatory pathways from traditional NDA (zuranolone MDD filing in Japan, Sep 2024) to digital therapeutic approvals (SDT-001 for ADHD approved in Japan, Feb 2025) to antibiotic regulatory pathways (with GAIN Act/LPAD pathway considerations for AMR products). Cefiderocol regulatory activities span global markets including new submissions in China and Australia.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV manages safety across anti-infective products (cefiderocol with the largest-ever real-world evidence study — PROVE, ~1,000 patients), antiviral products (ensitrelvir/Xocova), CNS products (zuranolone), and the HIV franchise (via ViiV). The infectious disease portfolio requires PV capabilities for antimicrobial resistance monitoring (tracking resistant pathogen emergence). The digital therapeutic (SDT-001) introduces a novel PV paradigm for software-based treatments. Shionogi's commitment to AMR access (GARDP/CHAI partnerships) means PV must function across low-income country settings.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance operates under the R&D Supervisory Unit with the CEO (Chairman, President, Director, and CEO) providing ultimate authority. The April 2024 reorganization centralized healthcare business operations, which should streamline B/R decision-making across Japan and overseas. The Intellectual Property Department was moved directly under R&D Supervisory Unit, reflecting the importance of IP in B/R and lifecycle decisions. The company operates as a single business segment (prescription drugs and related businesses).
