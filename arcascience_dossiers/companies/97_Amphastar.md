# Amphastar Pharmaceuticals

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** $732M (operating cash flow $213.4M; strong margin expansion to 27.4% adjusted net income margin)
- **Headquarters:** Rancho Cucamonga, California, USA
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Specialty injectables (epinephrine, glucagon, phytonadione, lidocaine), intranasal products (BAQSIMI glucagon nasal powder), inhalation products (Primatene MIST), insulin API manufacturing, biosimilar insulins (in development)
- **Pipeline Size:** 20+ product candidates including 4 ANDAs filed targeting $2B+ market, 4 biosimilar products targeting $7B+ market, 2 generic products targeting $1.3B+ market; proprietary peptide pipeline (AMP-105 oncology, AMP-107 ophthalmology, AMP-109, AMP-110); vertically integrated API facilities in US, France, China; 163 R&D employees
- **Strategic Priorities:** Pivot from generic injectables to proprietary specialty products (BAQSIMI, Primatene MIST); biosimilar insulin development (AMP-004, AMP-005); proprietary peptide platform expansion; vertical integration as competitive moat; US manufacturing domestic supply chain advantage; BAQSIMI reaching $250M peak potential

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Amphastar's clinical development spans three distinct categories: (1) generic product development requiring bioequivalence studies for ANDA filings, (2) biosimilar insulin development requiring comparative clinical trials, and (3) proprietary peptide development (AMP-105, AMP-107) requiring full IND-to-NDA clinical programs. The 163-person R&D team manages this diverse portfolio across injectable, intranasal, and inhalation dosage forms. The BAQSIMI integration (acquired from Lilly) involved clinical supply chain transition. The proprietary peptide pipeline represents a strategic evolution toward novel drug development.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs manages FDA filings across multiple pathways: 505(b)(2) for specialty products, ANDA for generics, biosimilar pathway (351(k)) for insulin products, and potentially NDA for proprietary peptides. The team has demonstrated capability across complex injectable product filings where manufacturing specifications are integral to regulatory submissions. FDA Drug Shortage Award recognition validates the regulatory team's responsiveness and strategic capability.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV covers marketed products including critical care injectables (epinephrine), consumer-facing products (Primatene MIST OTC), and specialty products (BAQSIMI). The diversity of settings (hospital/urgent care for injectables, retail pharmacy for inhalation, consumer for OTC) creates varied post-marketing safety monitoring requirements.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance is evolving as the company transitions from a primarily generic injectable manufacturer to a proprietary specialty pharma company. Generic product B/R assessment references established profiles, while the emerging proprietary peptide pipeline (AMP-105 for oncology, AMP-107 for ophthalmology) requires full innovative drug B/R evaluation. This transition represents a fundamental shift in organizational B/R capability needs.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
Amphastar's strategic pivot from generic injectables to proprietary specialty products creates a transitional moment where the organization needs to build clinical development capabilities beyond bioequivalence studies. ArcaScience should focus on the emerging proprietary peptide pipeline as the entry wedge: (1) AMP-105 (first-in-class oncology peptide) requires rigorous B/R assessment as it enters clinical development with a novel mechanism targeting cell growth and metastasis inhibition, (2) AMP-107 (ophthalmic anti-VEGF eye drops) represents a potential market disruption ($9.4B market) where strong B/R evidence will be critical for FDA and market acceptance as a non-invasive alternative to injections, and (3) biosimilar insulin programs (AMP-004, AMP-005) benefit from structured B/R frameworks for regulatory submissions. The pitch should emphasize that as Amphastar evolves beyond generic injectables into novel therapeutics, the BRA platform provides the clinical development sophistication needed for this transition. The strong cash flow position supports investment capacity. A pilot with AMP-105 or AMP-107 would align with the company's most exciting growth opportunities.
