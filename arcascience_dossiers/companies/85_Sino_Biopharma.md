# Sino Biopharmaceutical

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** RMB 28.87B (~$3.97B; 10.2% YoY growth; gross profit margin 81.5%)
- **Headquarters:** Hong Kong, China (operational headquarters)
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Oncology (37.2% of revenue, RMB 10.73B), hepatitis/liver disease, respiratory, surgery/analgesia, cardio-cerebrovascular
- **Pipeline Size:** 70 innovative drugs under research (39 oncology, 7 liver disease, 13 respiratory, 6 surgery/pain, 5 other); 65 generic drugs; 6 innovative products approved in 2024 alone; key assets include benmelstobart (anti-PD-L1), D-1553 (KRAS G12C), TQ-B3101 (ROS1/ALK/c-Met); R&D investment RMB 5.49B (~19% of revenue)
- **Strategic Priorities:** Transformation from generic-oriented to innovation-driven pharma; innovative drug sales targeting 45% of turnover in 2025 and 50% in 2026; 30+ innovative products approved by 2027; 3-5 new innovative product launches per year; strategic acquisitions (LaNova Medicines for $951M); dual antibody and ADC platform development; AI-driven drug discovery

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Sino Biopharmaceutical operates a large-scale clinical development organization supporting 70 innovative drug programs across four major therapeutic areas. Oncology is the dominant area with 39 candidates spanning anti-PD-L1 antibodies, KRAS inhibitors, multi-target kinase inhibitors, and ADCs. Clinical development includes programs at all stages from Phase I through marketing application, with 5 at NDA stage and 4 in Phase III as of late 2023. The LaNova Medicines acquisition adds additional clinical-stage assets including PD-1xVEGF bispecifics and anti-SIRPa antibodies. Specialized R&D platforms cover dual antibodies and ADCs.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs primarily interfaces with China's NMPA, managing a high volume of filings — 16 marketing approvals and 66 clinical trial approvals obtained in 2024. The team has secured 3 breakthrough therapy designations, demonstrating ability to leverage expedited regulatory pathways. As the company targets international expansion, regulatory capability for ex-China markets (FDA, EMA) is likely growing.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV manages safety monitoring for a rapidly expanding portfolio of innovative drugs plus a large generic drug base. With 6 innovative products approved in 2024 and the portfolio expected to grow to 30+ by 2027, PV requirements are scaling rapidly. Safety monitoring for oncology immunotherapies (benmelstobart) and targeted therapies requires specialized capability.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance spans innovative and generic drug portfolios, with the innovation transformation requiring increasingly sophisticated assessment capabilities. Portfolio-level decisions about which of the 70 innovative candidates to advance are high-stakes given the R&D investment (~19% of revenue). The LaNova acquisition ($951M) demonstrates willingness to make large B/R-informed portfolio investments.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
Sino Biopharmaceutical's innovation transformation (from generic to 50% innovative revenue by 2026) and massive pipeline (70 innovative drugs) create an urgent need for portfolio-level B/R optimization. ArcaScience should position the BRA platform as: (1) an oncology portfolio optimizer, helping prioritize among 39 oncology candidates competing for development resources, (2) a tool for M&A due diligence, supporting B/R assessment of acquisition targets like LaNova, and (3) a bridge for international expansion, enabling B/R profiling that translates NMPA clinical evidence into FDA/EMA regulatory strategy. The platform's probabilistic approval prediction is particularly valuable given that Sino Biopharma aims to launch 3-5 innovative products per year. A pilot with the oncology development team, focusing on the most advanced candidates (benmelstobart, D-1553), would demonstrate immediate value. China market entry may require a local partnership or joint venture structure.
