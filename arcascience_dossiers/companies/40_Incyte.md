# Incyte

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** $4.2B (+15% YoY; driven by Jakafi $2.8B and Opzelura $508M)
- **Headquarters:** Wilmington, Delaware, USA (European HQ in Morges, Switzerland)
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Oncology/hematology (MPN, GVHD, lymphoma, solid tumors), inflammation/autoimmunity/dermatology (atopic dermatitis, hidradenitis suppurativa, vitiligo, prurigo nodularis)
- **Pipeline Size:** 15+ clinical programs; 7 marketed products; 18+ key milestones expected in 2025; programs span MPNs, GVHD, solid tumors, and dermatology/inflammation; goal of 10+ impactful product launches by 2030
- **Strategic Priorities:** Jakafi franchise defense and extension; Opzelura expansion into new dermatology indications; 10+ product launches by 2030; Escient acquisition ($750M for MRGPR programs); pipeline diversification into solid tumor oncology (KRAS G12D, CDK2i); JAK2V617F disease-modifying therapy development; retifanlimab and tafasitamab indication expansion

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Incyte's clinical development is led by the EVP and Chief Medical Officer, who oversees clinical development, clinical operations, pharmacovigilance, statistics, data management, and regulatory affairs — all under one integrated leadership structure. The development organization spans hematology/MPN (Jakafi lifecycle, mCALR, JAK2V617Fi, BETi, ALK2i), solid tumor oncology (CDK2i, KRASG12Di, TGFbetaR2xPD-1), inflammation/autoimmunity/dermatology (Opzelura expansion, povorcitinib, EP262/EP547 from Escient), and GVHD (Niktimvo). The company has operations in North America, Europe (Morges, Switzerland), and Japan.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs sits under the CMO's integrated organization. The team manages FDA, EMA, and PMDA submissions. Recent achievements include FDA approval of Niktimvo (2024), retifanlimab Phase 3 success (SCAC and NSCLC), and sNDA submission for ruxolitinib cream in pediatric AD. The regulatory team handles both oncology (accelerated approval pathways, breakthrough designations) and dermatology (standard NDA, sNDA) regulatory strategies.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV is integrated under the CMO alongside clinical development and regulatory affairs. PV manages safety across JAK inhibitor products (Jakafi, Opzelura, Olumiant — with specific JAK-class labeling requirements), anti-CSF-1R antibody (Niktimvo), FLT3 inhibitor (Pemigatinib), and emerging programs. The JAK inhibitor safety class-wide scrutiny from regulators (FDA class-wide boxed warnings) makes PV particularly important and high-profile at Incyte.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance operates within the CMO's integrated clinical/regulatory/PV organization, creating natural alignment between clinical data generation, safety monitoring, and regulatory communication. The integrated structure under a single executive is advantageous for cohesive B/R decision-making. Given the JAK inhibitor regulatory scrutiny, B/R governance for ruxolitinib products receives board-level attention.
