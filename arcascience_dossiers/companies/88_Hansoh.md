# Hansoh Pharmaceutical

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** ~RMB 13B (~$1.8B estimated; H1 2025 TTM revenue ~$1.83B; innovative drugs reached 82.7% of revenue by H1 2025, indicating strong innovation-driven growth)
- **Headquarters:** Lianyungang, Jiangsu Province, China
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Oncology (aumolertinib/Ameile, flumatinib, ADCs including HS-20093/B7-H3 ADC), CNS (historical strength), metabolic diseases (PEG-loxenatide, GLP-1 programs), autoimmune diseases, anti-infectives
- **Pipeline Size:** 7 marketed innovative drugs; 70+ clinical trials covering 40+ innovative drug candidates; key assets include HS-20093 (B7-H3 ADC, 3 NMPA breakthrough designations), HS-10529 (KRAS G12D), HS-10535 (Merck metabolic deal, $112M upfront); 11 in-licensing projects; R&D covers peptides, small molecules, biologics, ADCs, siRNAs
- **Strategic Priorities:** Innovation-driven growth (innovative drugs >82% of revenue); oncology ADC platform leadership; global BD partnerships (GSK $101.5M+ for HS-20089/B7-H4 ADC, Merck $112M+ for metabolic asset); expanding into autoimmune and metabolic diseases; global R&D footprint (4 centers: Shanghai, Lianyungang, Changzhou, Maryland USA)

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Hansoh has built a substantial clinical development organization managing 70+ clinical trials across 40+ innovative candidates. Development is organized by therapeutic area with oncology as the primary franchise. The ADC platform is a strategic priority with multiple candidates (HS-20093 targeting B7-H3, HS-20089 targeting B7-H4 licensed to GSK). The company operates 4 R&D centers including a Maryland, USA facility, indicating global development capability. The modality range (peptides, small molecules, biologics, ADCs, siRNAs) requires diverse clinical development expertise.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs primarily manages NMPA interactions, having secured multiple breakthrough therapy designations for HS-20093 (3 for ES-SCLC, nsNSCLC, and osteosarcoma). The GSK and Merck partnerships require international regulatory coordination capability. Production quality systems have been certified by FDA, EMA, and PMDA, indicating readiness for international regulatory filings. 10,000+ employees support the fully integrated organization.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV manages safety monitoring for 7 marketed innovative drugs across oncology, CNS, and metabolic areas. The expanding ADC pipeline (B7-H3, B7-H4, KRAS G12D) requires specialized safety monitoring for novel mechanisms. Partnership obligations to GSK and Merck add international safety reporting requirements.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance spans the innovative drug portfolio with decisions made by therapeutic area leaders and R&D management. The strategic shift from older generics to 82.7% innovative drug revenue reflects B/R-driven portfolio management. BD deal decisions (GSK, Merck) involve B/R assessment of pipeline assets for out-licensing and in-licensing.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
Hansoh's emergence as a major Chinese innovator with transformative BD deals (GSK, Merck) creates a compelling need for structured B/R governance that meets international partner standards. ArcaScience should position the BRA platform as: (1) a partnership-enablement tool that provides the B/R assessment rigor expected by GSK, Merck, and future international partners, (2) an ADC portfolio optimizer for the B7-H3 and other ADC programs where indication selection and competitive positioning depend on dynamic B/R profiling, and (3) a BD deal support tool that enables rigorous B/R assessment for both in-licensing (11 projects) and out-licensing decisions. The GSK deal for HS-20089 (B7-H4 ADC) and the Merck deal for HS-10535 provide concrete use cases where structured B/R frameworks would add value to ongoing partnership management. Entry through the Maryland, USA R&D center may be easier than through Chinese headquarters.
