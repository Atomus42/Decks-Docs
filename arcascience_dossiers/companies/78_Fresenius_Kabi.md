# Fresenius Kabi

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** EUR 8.4B (Fresenius Kabi); Group revenue EUR 21.5B (Fresenius SE)
- **Headquarters:** Bad Homburg, Germany
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Biosimilars/biopharma (immunology, oncology), IV generics and fluids, clinical nutrition (parenteral and enteral), MedTech (infusion systems, blood collection)
- **Pipeline Size:** 10+ biosimilar molecules in pipeline; 6 FDA-approved biosimilars (Stimufend, Idacio, Tyenne, Otulfi, Conexxence, Bomyntra); licensed vedolizumab biosimilar candidate (PB016); mAbxience CDMO integration expanding pipeline capacity
- **Strategic Priorities:** Biosimilar portfolio expansion (fastest-growing segment at 63% EBIT growth); Biopharma as key growth vector alongside MedTech and Nutrition; vertical integration via mAbxience acquisition; CDMO capabilities; global manufacturing network optimization

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Fresenius Kabi's clinical development is concentrated in the Biopharma Business Unit, led by a dedicated President. The unit employs 1,650+ professionals covering the full value chain from R&D through manufacturing to commercialization. Clinical development focuses primarily on biosimilar programs requiring analytical similarity, PK bioequivalence, and confirmatory efficacy trials. The European Research & Development Center houses core biosimilar development capabilities. Clinical operations span immunology (adalimumab, tocilizumab, ustekinumab, denosumab biosimilars) and expanding into new biologics through licensing (vedolizumab biosimilar from Polpharma).

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs manages biosimilar filings globally, with demonstrated expertise in both FDA and EMA pathways. The team secured 2 FDA approvals in 2024 alone (Tyenne, Otulfi) and has successfully navigated the complex biosimilar regulatory landscape across multiple jurisdictions. Regulatory strategy is closely integrated with the Biopharma Business Unit, supporting global launch sequences and multi-market filing strategies.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
Pharmacovigilance at Fresenius Kabi covers a broad portfolio of marketed products across biosimilars, IV drugs, nutrition products, and medical devices. The safety organization must manage post-marketing surveillance for multiple biosimilars referencing different originator products, each with its own safety profile and regulatory reporting requirements. PV is organized at the corporate level with specialized support within the Biopharma unit.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
Benefit-risk governance for biosimilar development is primarily focused on demonstrating totality of evidence (analytical, PK, clinical) to establish biosimilarity. B/R decisions are made at the Biopharma Business Unit level, with escalation to Fresenius Kabi leadership for portfolio investment decisions. The expansion from generic IV drugs into complex biologics has elevated the importance of structured B/R assessment within the organization.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
Fresenius Kabi's Biopharma unit is the company's fastest-growing segment (63% EBIT growth in 2024) with 10+ biosimilar molecules in the pipeline and aggressive launch targets. The competitive biosimilar market rewards speed-to-market and regulatory execution. ArcaScience should position the BRA platform specifically for biosimilar development, emphasizing: (1) optimization of the totality-of-evidence assessment that underpins biosimilar regulatory submissions, (2) multi-jurisdictional regulatory strategy support for simultaneous FDA/EMA/global filings, and (3) competitive intelligence integration to inform portfolio prioritization (which biosimilar targets to invest in). The platform's ability to integrate clinical comparability data with real-world safety evidence from originator products and already-launched biosimilars creates a unique value proposition. A pilot focused on one of the later-stage pipeline molecules (e.g., the vedolizumab biosimilar from Polpharma) would demonstrate value within the context of a specific regulatory filing strategy.
