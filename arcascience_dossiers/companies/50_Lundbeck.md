# Lundbeck

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** DKK 22.0B (~$3.2B; record revenue; +14% CER; +11% DKK)
- **Headquarters:** Copenhagen (Valby), Denmark
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Exclusively brain diseases — depression (Trintellix, Rexulti), schizophrenia (Abilify Maintena, Rexulti), Alzheimer's disease (Rexulti for agitation), migraine, Parkinson's disease, rare epilepsies (Dravet syndrome, LGS), multiple system atrophy (MSA)
- **Pipeline Size:** 4 Phase III programs expected by 2026; key assets include bexicaserin (rare epilepsies, Breakthrough Therapy Designation), amlenetug (MSA, Phase III MASCOT trial), LP659 (S1P modulator for neuroinflammation), Rexulti (PTSD expansion, sNDA); strategic brands represent 75% of revenue (DKK 16.5B)
- **Strategic Priorities:** "Focused Innovator Strategy" (winning in neuro-rare and specialist-treated diseases); Longboard Pharmaceuticals acquisition ($2.6B for bexicaserin, Dec 2024); neuro-rare disease franchise building; mid-term growth security through strategic brand lifecycle; R&D reinvestment (~20% of revenue); Lundbeck Foundation majority ownership (~70%)

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Lundbeck's clinical development is exclusively focused on brain diseases — making it the only fully integrated pharmaceutical company solely devoted to psychiatric and neurological disorders. The R&D organization is led by the EVP Head of R&D and spans research centers in Denmark and the U.S. The $2.6B Longboard acquisition (completed December 2024) significantly expanded the clinical development portfolio, adding bexicaserin (Phase III DEEp SEA study in Dravet syndrome, ~160 participants) and LP659 (S1P modulator). The MASCOT Phase III trial for amlenetug in MSA was initiated November 2024. The Otsuka partnership co-develops brexpiprazole (Rexulti) with clinical programs in PTSD and Alzheimer's agitation. Lundbeck has production facilities in Denmark, France, and Italy.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs manages EMA, FDA, and global submissions with specialized CNS expertise. The team navigates complex psychiatric and neurological regulatory pathways including breakthrough therapy designations (bexicaserin) and rare disease/orphan drug frameworks. The PTSD sNDA for brexpiprazole/sertraline combination represents novel psychiatric combination regulatory strategy. Regulatory teams coordinate with Otsuka on co-developed product submissions.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV manages safety exclusively in the CNS domain, requiring deep neuropsychiatric safety expertise. Key safety considerations include: antipsychotic metabolic and extrapyramidal effects (Rexulti), anticonvulsant safety monitoring (bexicaserin in pediatric populations), neurodegenerative disease safety (amlenetug for MSA), and long-term psychiatric medication safety (Trintellix, Cipralex legacy). The pure CNS focus means PV staff have specialized expertise in neuropsychiatric adverse events, suicidality monitoring, and cognitive safety assessment.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance operates under the two-tier board structure (Board of Directors and Executive Management). The Lundbeck Foundation (~70% shareholder) provides long-term strategic oversight. The Board of Directors (11 members) approves corporate strategy and evaluates Executive Management. B/R decisions benefit from deep CNS domain expertise — every decision-maker in the company operates within the brain disease context.
