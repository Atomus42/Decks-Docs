## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Chugai operates a lifecycle management system for project-level integrated management spanning research, regulatory affairs, drug safety, manufacturing, and other functions. Clinical development leverages the Roche alliance for global coordination — Chugai leads development in Japan while Roche handles EU and U.S. development for many shared assets. For in-house products, Chugai leads global clinical development independently; however, the portfolio has shifted significantly in 2025-2026: AMY109 (endometriosis) was discontinued by Roche, and BRY10 (chronic diseases, first MALEXA AI-generated molecule) was dropped after Phase 1 data review in January 2026. NXT007 (next-gen hemophilia A bispecific) is now the flagship in-house program, with Phase I/II NXTAGE trial showing hemostatic normalization and a favorable safety profile, and three Phase III studies (including a head-to-head vs. Hemlibra) planned for 2026. Additional programs advancing include GYM329 (neurological disorders/obesity), DONQ52 (celiac disease), and RAY121 (autoimmune diseases), all of which entered new clinical trials. Sparsentan (acquired via Renalys Pharma subsidiary) showed positive Phase III results in IgA nephropathy and is planned for Japan regulatory filing in 2026. The company cooperates with numerous medical institutions and clinical research centers, operates the Chugai Venture Fund in the U.S. (investments in Leal Therapeutics, HYKU Biosciences), and opened a US Partnering Office in January 2026.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs is integrated into the lifecycle management system. Chugai manages Japan-focused regulatory submissions (PMDA) independently, while leveraging Roche's regulatory infrastructure for FDA and EMA submissions on co-developed products. FY2026 is expected to see the highest number of regulatory application plans in company history, reflecting pipeline maturation. Notable regulatory activity: Tecentriq filed for additional indication (adjuvant MRD-positive bladder cancer), sparsentan planned for Japan filing in 2026, and Lunsumio + Polivy targeted for early market penetration. For purely in-house assets seeking global registration (especially NXT007's three planned Phase III studies), Chugai continues building independent regulatory capabilities supported by the new US Partnering Office.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
Drug safety is part of the project-level integrated management system. PV teams handle both in-house marketed products and Roche-licensed products distributed in Japan. Chugai's DHBL (Deep Human Biology Learning) approach emphasizes understanding disease biology from clinical data, which naturally feeds safety signal detection and benefit-risk assessment. The BRY10 discontinuation (January 2026) — reportedly based on "a comprehensive review of the data obtained to date" though Chugai did not disclose specific safety-related reasons — underscores the rigor of the PV/data review process, even for AI-discovered molecules. Roche's parallel decision to axe AMY109 and four Phase 1 solid tumor assets similarly reflects heightened scrutiny on early-stage B/R profiles. The expanding NXT007 program (no serious adverse events or thromboembolic events across all dose levels in Phase I/II) demonstrates strong safety governance as the program advances to Phase III.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R decisions are made at the project lifecycle management level, involving cross-functional teams that include clinical development, regulatory affairs, drug safety, and manufacturing. For Roche-partnered products, B/R governance involves dual-company decision-making. The Chief Scientific Officer oversees the Human Biology Creation Hub, which plays a role across drug discovery, development, and clinical research.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
ArcaScience should position its BRA platform as complementary to Chugai's MALEXA AI platform, not competitive with it. The narrative should focus on how ArcaScience handles the downstream benefit-risk assessment challenge — translating MALEXA's drug discovery outputs into continuous, regulatory-grade B/R profiles throughout clinical development. The ideal entry point is through Chugai's in-house pipeline (NXT007, AMY109, BRY10) rather than Roche-partnered assets, as these are fully under Chugai's control. BRY10, as the first MALEXA-generated molecule to enter clinical trials, is a compelling proof-of-concept opportunity. ArcaScience should also highlight its ability to support multi-regional regulatory strategy (PMDA + FDA + EMA), which is critical as Chugai expands global registration of in-house products. The growing R&D budget (up 56% over four years) signals willingness to invest in innovative tools. Engage through scientific conferences in Japan (DIA Japan, JPMA events) or through Chugai's open innovation channels.
