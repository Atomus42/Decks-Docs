# Eisai

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** ~$5.3B (¥789.4B; record revenue driven by LENVIMA, LEQEMBI, and Dayvigo)
- **Headquarters:** Tokyo, Japan
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Neurology (Alzheimer's disease, sleep disorders), oncology (liver cancer, thyroid cancer, breast cancer), global health
- **Pipeline Size:** 15+ clinical programs across neurology, oncology, global health, and GI; major assets include LEQEMBI (lecanemab, approved in 44 countries), E2814 (anti-tau, Phase II), LENVIMA/KEYTRUDA LEAP program (multiple Phase 3 trials), BB-1701 (HER2 ADC), FZEC (ADC)
- **Strategic Priorities:** LEQEMBI global commercialization and lifecycle expansion (subcutaneous dosing, maintenance treatment); DHBL (Deep Human Biology Learning) R&D reorganization; Alzheimer's disease continuum (preclinical through clinical AD); LENVIMA lifecycle management through LEAP combination program; AI/ML integration in drug discovery; organizational transformation for profitability by FY2026

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Eisai reorganized its R&D into the DHBL (Deep Human Biology Learning) structure in October 2022, representing a fundamental shift to drug discovery based on human biology rather than traditional target-based approaches. Under the Chief Scientific Officer, the "Human Biology Creation Hub" spans drug discovery, development, and clinical research. Clinical development is organized by therapeutic area: Neurology Discovery, Oncology Discovery, and Global Health teams, supported by an Integrated Chemistry team utilizing AI/ML and structural biology. The Biogen partnership co-manages LEQEMBI development and commercialization globally. The LENVIMA/KEYTRUDA LEAP program is co-managed with Merck. The G2D2 Discovery Center in Cambridge, MA, anchors U.S.-based innovation.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs manages submissions to PMDA, FDA, EMA, and 44+ countries where LEQEMBI is now approved. The regulatory team navigates complex pathways including accelerated approval, priority review, and supplemental BLA submissions (e.g., LEQEMBI subcutaneous dosing under priority review with FDA action date May 2026). The multi-company regulatory coordination (Eisai leads, Biogen co-commercializes) requires sophisticated regulatory management.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV manages safety across two major blockbusters with distinct safety profiles — LEQEMBI (amyloid-related imaging abnormalities/ARIA monitoring) and LENVIMA (tyrosine kinase inhibitor cardiovascular and hepatic safety). The Alzheimer's safety landscape is particularly complex, involving biomarker-based monitoring (ARIA detection via MRI) and careful patient selection. Eisai has stated it leads safety monitoring for LEQEMBI with final decision-making authority.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance operates within the DHBL framework under the CSO, with the Human Biology Creation Hub playing a cross-cutting role. For LEQEMBI, B/R decisions involve both Eisai and Biogen, with Eisai holding final decision-making authority. For LENVIMA, B/R governance involves the Merck strategic collaboration. The ongoing organizational transformation toward profitability by FY2026 means B/R decisions increasingly factor in commercial viability alongside clinical merit.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
ArcaScience should target the LEQEMBI (lecanemab) program as the primary entry point. LEQEMBI presents one of the most compelling use cases for dynamic B/R assessment in the industry: (1) ARIA (amyloid-related imaging abnormalities) requires continuous, patient-level B/R monitoring as the drug expands into broader Alzheimer's populations, (2) the transition from intravenous to subcutaneous dosing (sBLA under priority review) requires updating B/R profiles with new formulation-specific safety data, (3) LEQEMBI is now approved in 44 countries, creating a massive real-world evidence stream that must be integrated with clinical trial data for ongoing B/R assessment, and (4) the AHEAD 3-45 study in preclinical AD extends the B/R framework to an entirely new patient population (clinically normal individuals with brain amyloid). The messaging should emphasize how ArcaScience's platform can help Eisai demonstrate continuous B/R to regulators globally as LEQEMBI expands — turning the ARIA safety challenge into a differentiated competitive advantage through transparent, data-driven B/R communication. Secondary entry through the LEAP oncology program provides a multi-therapeutic-area value proposition. Engage through CTAD (Clinical Trials on Alzheimer's Disease) or DIA events.
