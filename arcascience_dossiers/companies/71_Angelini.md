# Angelini Pharma

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** EUR 1,207.3 million (~$1.3 billion; +3.6% YoY); 50.6% from international markets; EBITDA of EUR 302.4M (+14% YoY); EBITDA margin 25%
- **Headquarters:** Rome, Italy (part of Angelini Industries, a privately held Italian conglomerate; Angelini Industries reached EUR 1.6B group revenue)
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Brain health / CNS (mental health — depression, schizophrenia; epilepsy — including rare genetic epilepsy), consumer health (OTC brands worldwide), pediatrics
- **Pipeline Size:** Cenobamate/Ontozry (innovative anti-epileptic, 100,000+ patients treated, partnered with SK Biopharmaceuticals); CV-01 (Cureverse partnership, first-in-class Keap1/Nrf2 pathway for epilepsy/neurodegeneration); Radiprodil (GRIN Therapeutics collaboration, $50M upfront + up to $520M milestones for ex-North America rights, Phase 3 pivotal trial initiated Q3 2025 for rare genetic epilepsy, breakthrough/orphan designations); SVG105 (Sovargen option agreement, first-in-disease ASO therapy for drug-resistant epilepsy from FCD type II, preclinical); JCR Pharmaceuticals (blood-brain barrier penetrating therapies, up to $505.5M); Angelini Ventures (EUR 300M biotech/digital health fund, EIB co-investment partnership launched)
- **Strategic Priorities:** Brain health leadership consolidation via aggressive M&A and licensing (>$685M committed in 2025 partnerships), CNS portfolio growth (27.2% growth in 2023), global expansion (products in 70+ countries, direct operations in 20+), consumer health portfolio restructuring (Amuchina/Infasil brands transferred to Fater to sharpen pharma focus), Angelini Ventures biotech investments (Adcytherix ADC co-investment with EIB), partnership-driven pipeline strategy, digital transformation (Chief Information & Digital Officer appointed), diversification into biologics and antisense oligonucleotides alongside small molecules
- **Leadership Change (Jan 2026):** Sergio Marullo di Condojanni appointed CEO of Angelini Pharma while retaining CEO role at Angelini Industries; priorities include transformative M&A, global scaling, and strengthening long-term competitiveness

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Angelini Pharma's clinical development focuses on Brain Health (mental health and epilepsy) and Consumer Health. The company has made its "largest ever investment" in CNS, acquiring or licensing therapeutic solutions for three major CNS disorders: epilepsy, schizophrenia, and depression. Clinical development relies heavily on partnerships: SK Biopharmaceuticals (cenobamate), Cureverse (CV-01 for epilepsy/neurodegeneration), Grin Therapeutics (radiprodil for rare genetic epilepsy — Phase 3 pivotal trial initiated Q3 2025 with $115M near-term commitment from Angelini), JCR Pharmaceuticals (blood-brain barrier penetrating therapies), and Sovargen (SVG105 ASO therapy for drug-resistant epilepsy, preclinical co-development). The pipeline has diversified significantly in 2025, now spanning innovative small molecules, biologics, and antisense oligonucleotides. Positive cenobamate (Ontozry) real-world evidence data were presented in September 2025 for adults with uncontrolled focal onset seizures. The company has strong internal R&D capabilities with focus on new pediatric treatments and operates directly in 20+ countries.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs manages filings across 70+ countries through both direct operations and strategic alliance partners. Cenobamate (Ontozry) regulatory management involves multiple European market approvals and ongoing label extensions, supported by positive real-world evidence data shared in September 2025. Radiprodil (GRIN Therapeutics) has received FDA breakthrough therapy designation, orphan drug designation, and rare pediatric disease designation, as well as European regulatory designations — creating a complex multi-jurisdiction regulatory pathway for a Phase 3 rare epilepsy program. SVG105 (Sovargen) as a first-in-disease ASO therapy for FCD type II drug-resistant epilepsy will require novel regulatory pathway navigation as it advances from preclinical to clinical stages. CV-01 (Keap1/Nrf2 modulator) as a first-in-class molecule requires navigating novel regulatory pathways. The JCR partnership for blood-brain barrier penetrating therapies involves regulatory coordination across territories. Consumer health regulatory management spans OTC compliance across multiple markets, though the portfolio is being streamlined (Amuchina/Infasil transfer to Fater) to sharpen regulatory focus on prescription medicines.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV supports both prescription CNS products and consumer health OTC portfolio across 70+ countries. Cenobamate PV involves monitoring for anti-seizure medication safety including DRESS syndrome risk (which required specific REMS in the US by partner SK Life Science); the growing real-world evidence base (data presented September 2025) increases the volume and complexity of PV signal monitoring. Radiprodil Phase 3 (rare genetic epilepsy in pediatric populations with tuberous sclerosis complex and focal cortical dysplasia) introduces PV requirements for a rare pediatric population with complex comorbidities. SVG105 (ASO therapy) will introduce new modality-specific safety monitoring requirements as it enters clinical development. CNS drug PV is inherently complex given psychiatric comorbidities, polypharmacy, and subjective adverse event reporting. The diversification of modalities (small molecules, biologics via JCR, and ASOs via Sovargen) significantly increases PV complexity across the portfolio. Consumer health PV covers a high-volume OTC portfolio with different reporting obligations than prescription products, though the transfer of brands to Fater may reduce this burden over time.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance is particularly important for Angelini's CNS portfolio given the complexity of neuropsychiatric benefit-risk assessment and the rapid expansion of the pipeline in 2025. Cenobamate's positioning as an innovative anti-epileptic requires demonstrating B/R advantages over established AEDs, now supported by growing real-world evidence. Radiprodil (GRIN Therapeutics) entering Phase 3 for rare genetic epilepsy with breakthrough designation creates heightened B/R governance requirements for a rare pediatric population where B/R thresholds differ from adult epilepsy. SVG105 (Sovargen) as a first-in-disease ASO therapy for drug-resistant epilepsy requires de novo B/R framework development for a novel modality in a novel indication. The CV-01 first-in-class molecule (Keap1/Nrf2 pathway) requires de novo B/R framework development. The private ownership structure under new CEO Sergio Marullo di Condojanni (appointed January 2026) may allow more long-term B/R investment decisions without public market quarterly pressure, and his stated focus on "transformative M&A" suggests continued pipeline expansion requiring scalable B/R governance.
