# argenx

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** ~$2.2 billion (global product net sales); VYVGART (efgartigimod) surpassed 10,000 patients across 3 indications in 30+ countries; significant growth from $1.2B in 2023
- **Headquarters:** Amsterdam, Netherlands (listed on Euronext and NASDAQ: ARGX)
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Immunology/autoimmune diseases — myasthenia gravis (MG), chronic inflammatory demyelinating polyneuropathy (CIDP), immune thrombocytopenia (ITP), and expansion to 15 total autoimmune indications; also complement pathway diseases, neuromuscular diseases (ALS, CMS, SMA)
- **Pipeline Size:** 3 clinical-stage platforms: efgartigimod/VYVGART (FcRn blocker, 15 indications), empasiprubart (C2 complement inhibitor, 4 indications/2 in Phase 3), ARGX-119 (MuSK agonist, 3 neuromuscular indications); 4 new IND-stage candidates (ARGX-213, ARGX-121, ARGX-109, ARGX-220); 20 clinical studies planned in 2025 (10 registrational, 10 proof-of-concept)
- **Strategic Priorities:** Vision 2030 (50,000 patients, 10 labeled indications, 5 Phase 3 candidates), VYVGART franchise expansion across 15 autoimmune diseases, empasiprubart Phase 3 advancement, ARGX-119 proof-of-concept in neuromuscular diseases, profitability achievement in 2025, Immunology Innovation Program (IIP) for discovery, SIMPLE ANTIBODY/NHANCE/ABDEG technology platforms

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
argenx runs one of the most ambitious clinical development programs in the autoimmune space, planning 20 clinical studies in 2025 alone (10 registrational and 10 proof-of-concept). The development organization is structured around three clinical platforms: efgartigimod (the FcRn blocker franchise), empasiprubart (complement C2 inhibitor), and ARGX-119 (MuSK agonist). The Immunology Innovation Program (IIP) serves as the discovery engine, combining antibody engineering technologies with disease biology expertise. Clinical operations span global sites with key presence in Europe and North America.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs has successfully secured approvals for VYVGART in 30+ countries across 3 indications (MG, CIDP, ITP). The team manages multiple indication expansions for efgartigimod across 15 autoimmune diseases, requiring parallel regulatory strategies for each indication. The regulatory function coordinates global submissions across EMA, FDA, PMDA, and other authorities. For empasiprubart and ARGX-119, the team is establishing regulatory pathways for novel mechanisms. Vision 2030 targets 10 labeled indications, requiring sustained regulatory execution.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV supports VYVGART across 30+ countries with 10,000+ patients treated. The FcRn blocker mechanism has a distinctive safety profile (increased susceptibility to infections due to IgG reduction) requiring careful monitoring across diverse autoimmune populations. Expansion to 15 autoimmune indications means PV must monitor for indication-specific safety signals in populations with different baseline risks. Empasiprubart (complement pathway inhibition) and ARGX-119 (MuSK agonist) bring different safety profiles requiring novel PV approaches.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance is central to argenx's strategy of expanding a single mechanism (FcRn blockade) across 15 autoimmune diseases. Each indication expansion requires a distinct B/R evaluation: the risk-benefit of IgG reduction differs in MG (clear benefit) vs. other autoimmune conditions where the unmet need and competitive landscape vary. Portfolio-level B/R decisions determine which of the 15 indications advance to registrational studies. Empasiprubart is described as a "pipeline-in-a-product" candidate, requiring B/R assessment across 4 diseases. The 10 registrational + 10 POC study plan for 2025 demands systematic B/R evaluation at scale.
