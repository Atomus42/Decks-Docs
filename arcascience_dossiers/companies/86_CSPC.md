# CSPC Pharmaceutical Group

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** ~RMB 30B (~$4.4B; revenue declined 7.8% YoY due to centralized volume-based procurement (VBP) pricing pressure; oncology products dropped ~28%). Q2 2025 revenue fell further, down 21.9% YoY to RMB 7.0B; gross margin declined for the fifth consecutive year to 65.6% (from 72.3% in 2020). Analysts project a return to earnings growth by 2026-2027 as VBP drag subsides and new products ramp.
- **Headquarters:** Shijiazhuang, Hebei Province, China
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Oncology (chemotherapeutics, ADCs, bispecific antibodies), cardiovascular, CNS/psychiatry/neurology, autoimmunity, anti-infectives, digestion/metabolism; obesity/metabolic (emerging via AstraZeneca partnership); bulk products (Vitamin C, antibiotics)
- **Pipeline Size:** 300+ projects in pipeline (40+ innovative small molecules, 50+ innovative macromolecules, 20+ new preparation drugs); 200+ innovative drugs in development; ranked 24th globally for R&D pipeline size in 2024; 16 marketing approvals and 66 clinical trial approvals in 2024; key assets include SYS6002/CRB-701 (Nectin-4 ADC), SYH2082 (long-acting GLP-1R/GIPR agonist, AstraZeneca $18.5B deal), SYH2086 (oral GLP-1 RA, Madrigal $2B deal), SYS6160 (EGFR ADC, FDA Fast Track), ulsinumab (IL-12/IL-23 inhibitor, NMPA review)
- **Strategic Priorities:** Innovation transformation to offset VBP-driven generic revenue erosion; AI-driven drug discovery platform (validated by AstraZeneca's multi-billion-dollar partnerships); massive out-licensing deal flow (AstraZeneca $18.5B obesity deal Jan 2026, AstraZeneca $5.3B chronic disease deal June 2025, Madrigal $2B oral GLP-1 deal July 2025, Radiance Biopharma ROR1 ADC); leadership recruitment (Liu Yongjun as global R&D president from Innovent); 50+ new products/indications for marketing approval within 5 years; proprietary LiquidGel once-monthly dosing technology platform

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
CSPC has built a massive clinical development organization supporting 300+ pipeline projects. The new executive president and global R&D head (recruited from Innovent in 2024) brings multinational pharmaceutical experience to transform the development organization. Clinical development spans innovative small molecules, macromolecules (ADCs, mAbs, bispecifics), mRNA vaccines, and new preparation technologies. The Corbus Pharmaceuticals collaboration for CRB-701 involves joint clinical development with Corbus responsible for US development and CSPC for China. Six Phase III trials were active with FDA Fast Track Designation secured for SYS6160. **[2025-2026 Update]** CSPC's AI-driven peptide drug discovery platform has been validated at the highest level through AstraZeneca's two consecutive mega-deals: a $5.3B chronic disease collaboration (June 2025) focusing on AI-enabled oral small molecule discovery for immunological diseases, and an $18.5B obesity/T2D collaboration (January 2026) leveraging CSPC's LiquidGel once-monthly dosing technology across eight programs including clinical-ready asset SYH2082 (GLP-1R/GIPR agonist entering Phase I). The Madrigal Pharmaceuticals deal (July 2025, $120M upfront, $2B milestones) for SYH2086 (oral GLP-1 RA) further validates CSPC's small molecule metabolic pipeline. SYS6160 (EGFR ADC for NSCLC) is slated for NDA filing in 2026. Ulsinumab (IL-12/IL-23 inhibitor for plaque psoriasis) is under NMPA review with approval expected early 2026. New products Mingfule (blood clots) and irinotecan liposome (oncology) are ramping commercially.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs manages high-volume NMPA filings (16 marketing approvals + 66 CTA approvals in 2024) and is expanding international regulatory capability through partnerships. The AstraZeneca deal and Corbus collaboration indicate growing FDA filing capability. The team navigates China's complex regulatory landscape including VBP pricing negotiations, breakthrough therapy designations, and NRDL (National Reimbursement Drug List) submissions. **[2025-2026 Update]** Regulatory complexity has escalated significantly with multiple simultaneous NDA tracks: ulsinumab (NMPA review), SYS6160 EGFR ADC (NDA filing planned 2026, FDA Fast Track), pertuzumab biosimilar (NDA filing 2025). The three AstraZeneca partnerships and the Madrigal deal require coordinated regulatory strategies across NMPA, FDA, and other jurisdictions. The Radiance Biopharma out-licensing of SYS6005 (ROR1 ADC) adds another international regulatory coordination requirement. CSPC is positioned among the top five Chinese pharma companies for licensing deal origination, increasing the demand for global regulatory infrastructure.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV monitors a large portfolio of marketed products spanning oncology chemotherapeutics (Jinyouli, Duomeisu), cardiovascular drugs (Xuanning), bulk products (Vitamin C), and an expanding innovative drug portfolio. The transition from chemotherapeutic drugs to newer modalities (ADCs, immunotherapies) requires evolving safety monitoring capabilities. **[2025-2026 Update]** PV demands are intensifying as CSPC transitions from a primarily China-domestic portfolio to a global out-licensing model. The AstraZeneca partnerships alone span up to 12 programs across obesity, T2D, immunological diseases, and cardiovascular indications, each requiring safety data packages meeting global regulatory standards. The Madrigal deal for SYH2086 (oral GLP-1 RA) adds metabolic safety monitoring for a novel modality. New product launches (Mingfule, irinotecan liposome, ulsinumab pending) expand the post-marketing safety surveillance scope. The emerging GLP-1/metabolic pipeline introduces new safety signal categories (GI events, pancreatitis risk, metabolic effects) distinct from CSPC's traditional oncology PV expertise.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance is critical given the simultaneous pressures of VBP-driven revenue erosion on existing products and massive investment in innovation. Portfolio-level B/R decisions determine resource allocation across 300+ projects. The recruitment of Liu Yongjun (ex-Innovent, ex-AstraZeneca/Sanofi) signals intent to professionalize B/R governance with global pharmaceutical standards.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
CSPC's simultaneous revenue crisis (VBP-driven oncology decline of 28%) and innovation transformation (300+ pipeline projects, AstraZeneca partnership) create a paradoxical but powerful opportunity. The newly recruited Global R&D President brings multinational pharma experience and a mandate to transform. ArcaScience should position the BRA platform as: (1) a portfolio triage tool that helps prioritize the most promising of 300+ projects given constrained resources, (2) support for international partnerships (AstraZeneca, Corbus) where structured B/R frameworks are expected by multinational partners, and (3) a clinical attrition reduction tool that protects the innovation investment from late-stage failures. The new R&D leadership's background at Innovent and multinational pharma (AstraZeneca, Sanofi) suggests familiarity with and receptivity to sophisticated B/R tools. Approaching through the new leadership's transformation mandate, rather than legacy procurement channels, offers the best entry pathway.
