## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Daiichi Sankyo's clinical development is anchored by its oncology ADC platform, with seven ADCs in clinical development spanning two distinct technology platforms. The R&D organization underwent transformation under outgoing Global Head of R&D Ken Takeshita, who was credited with turning R&D "into a globally integrated engine of innovation." The new Global Head of R&D, John Tsai (ex-Novartis CMO, effective April 2025), brings extensive global clinical development experience -- he previously led the development of 160 projects and 500 clinical trials resulting in 15 global approvals at Novartis. CEO Okuzawa praised Tsai's appointment as bringing "unique expertise to our continued pursuit of cutting-edge science and technology" to execute the next five-year business plan. Clinical development is organized with distinct streams: oncology ADC programs (the core, with five registrational readouts positive in 2025 and five launches planned for 2026), non-oncology programs (cardiovascular, managed for lifecycle), and partnership-managed programs (AstraZeneca co-develops Enhertu and Datroway; Merck co-develops HER3-DXd, I-DXd, R-DXd). The U.S. subsidiary (Daiichi Sankyo Inc.) has a dedicated EVP of Drug Development for the Americas, reflecting the importance of the U.S. oncology market. R&D investment rose 5.2% in Q1 FY2025 to JPY 105.9B, reflecting strategic commitment to the DXd ADC portfolio. Global Head of R&D and Global Head of Specialty Medicine Clinical Development are key functional roles.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs is organized with global leadership (Head of Global Regulatory Affairs) and regional functions (VP Head of EU and International Regulatory Affairs at Daiichi Sankyo Europe). The regulatory function is under extraordinary pressure as the company transitions from a 2025 of successful readouts to a 2026 of five simultaneous commercial launches, alongside ongoing partnership-managed filings. Quality Assurance & Regulatory Affairs (QARA) is identified as a combined organizational unit in the global management structure. Key 2025 regulatory milestones achieved: Datroway FDA approval for HR+/HER2- breast cancer (Jan 2025); Datroway FDA accelerated approval for EGFR-mutated NSCLC (Jun 2025); Enhertu approved in combination with pertuzumab for first-line HER2+ metastatic breast cancer (Dec 2025, DESTINY-Breast09); I-DXd Breakthrough Therapy Designation for ES-SCLC (Aug 2025). Key 2026 milestones upcoming: Datroway TNBC sBLA Priority Review (PDUFA Q2 2026, TROPION-Breast02); Enhertu neoadjuvant therapy decision (DESTINY-Breast11, FY2026 H1); DESTINY-Lung04 data (HER2-mutated NSCLC first-line, FY2026 H1); TROPION-Lung07/08 data (Datroway first-line NSCLC, FY2026 H2). The I-DXd partial clinical hold due to higher-than-expected Grade 5 ILD events in IDeate-Lung02 -- subsequently lifted after implementation of stricter enrollment criteria and enhanced safety monitoring -- underscores the regulatory complexity of the ADC platform and the critical importance of proactive safety management in regulatory interactions. The 2024 setbacks (CRL for HER3-DXd, Dato-DXd NSCLC withdrawal) remain relevant precedent for the regulatory function.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
Clinical Safety & Pharmacovigilance (CSPV) is a dedicated organizational unit within the global management structure, responsible for safety monitoring across the ADC portfolio and legacy cardiovascular products. ADC safety monitoring is now recognized as a class-wide concern: a 2025 pharmacovigilance study in The Oncologist found that 10 of 15 ADCs were associated with ILD events, with trastuzumab deruxtecan (Enhertu) accounting for 68.4% of all ADC-related ILD reports and carrying the highest reporting odds ratio (ROR 49.04). ILD incidence ranges from 10.7-26.0% for Enhertu and 3.6-25.0% for Datroway, and more than 26% of ADC-related ILD cases result in death. The I-DXd program experienced an FDA partial clinical hold in 2025 due to higher-than-anticipated Grade 5 ILD events in the IDeate-Lung02 trial (ES-SCLC), prompting implementation of stricter enrollment eligibility criteria, more frequent unblinded safety data review, and additional investigator training. Jefferies analysts noted that "ILD is a potentially fatal side effect known to be associated with all of Daiichi's DXd ADCs." Each ADC platform (DXd and modified PBD) has distinct safety profiles, and the seven-ADC portfolio creates the industry's most complex cross-program safety signal analysis requirements -- a safety signal in any one DXd-platform drug has immediate implications for the entire portfolio. Partnership structures (AstraZeneca, Merck) add inter-company PV coordination complexity, particularly when safety events like the I-DXd clinical hold require rapid cross-partnership communication.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance is managed through R&D leadership and portfolio committees, with CEO Okuzawa and new Global Head of R&D Tsai shaping the strategic direction. The ADC platform creates a unique B/R challenge that intensified in 2025: each ADC shares technology platform elements (same linker-payload) but targets different antigens in different tumor types, meaning safety signals from one program have immediate implications for others. The I-DXd partial clinical hold in 2025 -- driven by fatal ILD events in the ES-SCLC trial -- forced platform-wide safety reassessment and demonstrated exactly why platform-level B/R assessment (evaluating the DXd technology platform as a whole, not just individual products) is a critical governance need. The strategic decision to discontinue DS-9606 (CLDN6 ADC) in January 2026 reflects active portfolio-level B/R governance. With five ADC launches planned for 2026, B/R governance must now simultaneously manage commercial-stage products (Enhertu, Datroway) alongside late-stage development programs (I-DXd, R-DXd, HER3-DXd) -- each with distinct ILD risk profiles. The AstraZeneca and Merck partnerships add complexity, as B/R decisions involve joint governance committees. The company's stated strategic priority of reducing partnership dependency ("bar for doing another partnership gets higher") creates additional governance dynamics as Daiichi Sankyo builds independent B/R governance capability.
