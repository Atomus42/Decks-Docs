# Sobi (Swedish Orphan Biovitrum)

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** ~SEK 26 billion (~$2.5 billion; ~19% growth at CER); adjusted EBITA margin in mid-30s percent
- **Headquarters:** Stockholm, Sweden
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Hematology (hemophilia A and B, thrombocytopenia, PNH), immunology (HLH, Still's disease, CAPS, FMF), specialty care (gout, blood cancers — DLBCL, CMML, myelofibrosis)
- **Pipeline Size:** Active pipeline in hematology, immunology, and specialty care; key programs include pegcetacoplan (C3G/IC-MPGN), pegadricase (gout, FDA review), pozdeutinurad (gout, Phase 3 via Arthrosi acquisition for $950M), Gamifant expansion (HLH/MAS)
- **Strategic Priorities:** Revenue ambition of SEK 55B by 2030, mid-to-late-stage pipeline focus, gout franchise build-out (pegadricase + pozdeutinurad), hemophilia portfolio evolution (ALTUVOCT/efanesoctocog alfa), complement pathway expansion (pegcetacoplan), strategic acquisitions (Arthrosi for $950M), Sanofi partnership for hemophilia products

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Sobi's clinical development is organized around its three therapeutic pillars: Hematology, Immunology, and Specialty Care. The company focuses on mid-to-late-stage development opportunities, leveraging external innovation through in-licensing and acquisitions rather than early-stage discovery. Key partnerships include Sanofi (hemophilia products including ALTUVOCT), Apellis (pegcetacoplan for C3G), and the acquired Arthrosi pipeline (pozdeutinurad for gout). The company has approximately 1,800 employees with presence in ~30 countries and delivers medicines in many more.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs operates across Europe (Sobi's primary territory for many products under the Sanofi collaboration), the US, and other markets. Key regulatory activities include EMA and FDA submissions for pegcetacoplan (C3G/IC-MPGN, planned 2025 filings), FDA review of pegadricase for gout, and ongoing label expansions for existing products. The Sanofi collaboration means regulatory responsibilities are divided by territory (Sobi in Europe/MENA/Russia, Sanofi in North America and rest of world).

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV covers a diverse rare disease portfolio across hematology and immunology. Hemophilia products (ALTUVOCT, Elocta, Alprolix) require long-term safety monitoring for factor replacement therapies. Gamifant (emapalumab) for primary HLH requires vigilant safety monitoring in a critically ill, often pediatric population. The gout franchise (pegadricase, pozdeutinurad) brings different PV requirements for a chronic inflammatory condition. Multi-partner PV coordination (Sanofi, Apellis) adds complexity.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance at Sobi is shaped by the rare disease context where small patient populations and high unmet need require careful balance. The company's strategy of focusing on mid-to-late-stage opportunities means B/R assessment of in-licensing and acquisition targets is a core competency. The $950M Arthrosi acquisition demonstrates significant investment based on B/R evaluation. The Sanofi partnership involves shared B/R governance for hemophilia products across territories.

## 4. Buying Pathway Hypothesis
- **Interest Initiator:** Chief BD Officer or Head of Specialty Care — B/R evaluation needs for acquisition targets (like Arthrosi) or new therapeutic area entry (gout)
- **Scientific Validator:** CMO/Head of R&D — evaluates scientific rigor for rare disease and specialty care applications
- **Budget Owner:** CEO or CFO — adjusted EBITA margin in mid-30s and SEK 55B revenue ambition by 2030 indicate investment capacity
- **Procurement Cycle:** 6-9 months; Swedish corporate culture values evidence-based decision-making; mid-cap company with relatively lean procurement
- **Internal Friction Points:** Partner-dependent portfolio (Sanofi, Apellis) may limit Sobi's ability to independently adopt B/R tools; acquisition-heavy model means organizational bandwidth is consumed by integration; relatively small internal R&D compared to revenue may limit R&D tool investment appetite; territorial restrictions from partnerships may limit data access

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
ArcaScience should target Sobi's acquisition-driven growth strategy and its entry into the gout therapeutic area. The $950M Arthrosi acquisition and the gout franchise build-out (pegadricase + pozdeutinurad) represent Sobi's biggest strategic bet, and rigorous B/R assessment is critical for a new disease area. Position the BRA platform as a due diligence and continuous monitoring tool that supports Sobi's business model of identifying and advancing mid-to-late-stage assets. The company's SEK 55B revenue ambition by 2030 requires significant pipeline and franchise execution where B/R excellence is a competitive advantage. Engage through the CMO's office or BD leadership in Stockholm, emphasizing the platform's ability to support Sobi's external innovation model with systematic B/R evaluation of potential acquisitions and in-licensing opportunities.
