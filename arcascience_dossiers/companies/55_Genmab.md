# Genmab

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** ~$3.1 billion (DKK 21,526 million; 31% growth YoY); primarily royalty-driven (DARZALEX royalties ~$2.2B)
- **Headquarters:** Copenhagen, Denmark (with presence across North America, Europe, Asia Pacific)
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Oncology (hematology, solid tumors), antibody therapeutics including bispecific T-cell engagers, antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs), immune checkpoint modulators, effector function-enhanced antibodies
- **Pipeline Size:** 12+ proprietary clinical-stage programs; 6+ approved medicines (DARZALEX, EPKINLY/TEPKINLY, Tivdak, Kesimpta, RYBREVANT, TECVAYLI); extensive partnered pipeline using DuoBody, HexaBody, and ADC platforms
- **Strategic Priorities:** Proprietary pipeline advancement (Rina-S, acasunlimab in Phase 3), ADC platform expansion (ProfoundBio acquisition for $1.8B), global self-commercialization capability build-out, KYSO ("Knock Your Socks Off") antibody medicine vision for 2030, EPKINLY launch execution

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Genmab's clinical development is organized to support both proprietary and partnered programs. Proprietary programs (where Genmab is responsible for at least 50% of development) include 12+ clinical-stage antibody therapeutics. The company has expanded its development capabilities through the ProfoundBio acquisition, adding next-generation ADC programs. Key partnerships with AbbVie (epcoritamab/EPKINLY, 50:50), Pfizer (tisotumab vedotin/Tivdak, 50:50), and BioNTech (DuoBody-CD40x4-1BB, 50:50) create a complex co-development governance structure. Clinical operations span global sites with key R&D hubs in Copenhagen and the US.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Genmab manages regulatory interactions across multiple markets for its commercialized products and pipeline. EPKINLY/TEPKINLY regulatory strategy involves multiple indication expansions (FL, DLBCL). Tivdak received full FDA approval for cervical cancer. The regulatory team manages orphan drug designations, breakthrough therapy designations, and accelerated approvals across US, EU, and Japan. Partnership structures mean regulatory responsibilities are shared with co-development partners, requiring coordination across organizations.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV responsibilities are shared between Genmab and its partners. For EPKINLY (self-commercialized in US and Japan, partnered with AbbVie), Genmab has direct PV responsibilities in self-commercialized markets. DARZALEX PV is primarily Janssen/J&J's responsibility. The growing number of commercialized products and the expansion of EPKINLY into new indications increase PV complexity. ADC safety monitoring (Tivdak, Rina-S) is particularly important given the known toxicity profiles of ADCs.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
Benefit-risk governance at Genmab operates at both the proprietary pipeline level and within partnership governance structures. The company's antibody technology platforms (DuoBody, HexaBody, ADC) produce candidates with novel mechanisms where B/R assessment requires integrating preclinical, translational, and clinical data. The decision to acquire ProfoundBio for $1.8B signals confidence in B/R profiles of acquired ADC assets. Two programs advancing to Phase 3 (Rina-S, acasunlimab) in 2024 represent major B/R governance milestones.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
ArcaScience should target Genmab's expanding proprietary pipeline, particularly the ADC platform built through the $1.8B ProfoundBio acquisition. ADCs present unique B/R challenges (narrow therapeutic windows, payload toxicity, target selection) where continuous B/R monitoring is essential. Rina-S and acasunlimab advancing to Phase 3 represent high-stakes decision points where probabilistic approval prediction could provide significant strategic value. The partnership model creates an additional value proposition: standardized B/R frameworks that can be shared across alliance governance structures. Engage through the Copenhagen R&D headquarters or the CMO's office, emphasizing the platform's ability to integrate data across antibody technology platforms (DuoBody, HexaBody, ADC) for portfolio-level B/R insights.
