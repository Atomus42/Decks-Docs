# AbbVie

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** $56.3 billion total net revenues; Growth Platform net revenues of $47.3 billion (+18.6%)
- **Headquarters:** North Chicago, Illinois, USA
- **CEO:** Robert A. Michael (CEO since July 2024; also Chairman of the Board effective July 2025, succeeding founder-CEO Richard Gonzalez who departed the board entirely)
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Immunology (Skyrizi, Rinvoq, Humira legacy), Oncology (ADCs including EMRELIS, bispecifics, molecular glue degraders), Neuroscience (Parkinson's, Alzheimer's, psychiatry/psychedelics, migraine), Aesthetics (Botox), Eye Care, emerging in vivo cell therapy (Capstan)
- **Pipeline Size:** 50+ clinical-stage programs; R&D investment of $10.8 billion in 2024; 20+ investigational oncology medicines in clinical trials
- **Strategic Priorities:** Post-Humira diversification through Skyrizi/Rinvoq growth, building industry-leading neuroscience franchise, oncology portfolio expansion (ADCs, bispecifics, molecular glue degraders via acquisitions), AI integration through ARCH platform, enterprise AI governance, next-generation immunology via in vivo CAR-T

### Key 2024-2026 Developments
- **CEO Transition (2024-2025):** Robert A. Michael became CEO on July 1, 2024, replacing Richard Gonzalez (who became Executive Chairman). In February 2025, the board elected Michael as Chairman effective July 1, 2025, completing the full leadership handover. Michael previously served as COO and navigated the Humira LOE. This is AbbVie's first-ever CEO change since its 2013 spinoff.
- **Emraclidine Phase II Failure & $3.5B Writedown:** In November 2024, the EMPOWER Phase 2 trials of emraclidine (M4-selective PAM from $8.7B Cerevel acquisition) failed to meet primary endpoints in schizophrenia, erasing ~$40B in market value and triggering a $3.5B non-cash impairment charge (January 2025). AbbVie is exploring higher doses via a multiple ascending dose study (data expected early 2026), but the schizophrenia monotherapy program is effectively paused. The remaining Cerevel programs (tavapadon for Parkinson's, emraclidine in Alzheimer's/Parkinson's psychosis) are valued at ~$3.6B.
- **EMRELIS (Teliso-V) FDA Approval (May 2025):** First AbbVie-developed solid tumor ADC; telisotuzumab vedotin received accelerated approval for c-Met-high non-squamous NSCLC. ORR of 35% in the LUMINOSITY Phase 2 trial. Confirmatory Phase 3 (TeliMET-NSCLC-01) underway. Analysts project potential $1B+ peak sales.
- **Capstan Therapeutics Acquisition ($2.1B, completed August 2025):** Acquired CPTX2309, a potential first-in-class in vivo CAR-T therapy using targeted lipid nanoparticles (tLNPs) for B cell-mediated autoimmune diseases. The CellSeeker platform enables CAR-T generation without ex vivo manufacturing or lymphodepletion, representing a transformational new modality for AbbVie's immunology franchise.
- **Gilgamesh Pharmaceuticals Acquisition ($1.2B, completed October 2025):** Acquired bretisilocin (GM-2505), a short-acting 5-HT2A agonist (psychedelic) in Phase 2 for major depressive disorder with a 94% remission rate in Phase 2. Expands AbbVie's psychiatry pipeline into novel psychedelic-assisted therapy.
- **Aliada Therapeutics Acquisition ($1.4B, completed December 2024):** Acquired ALIA-1758, an anti-pyroglutamate amyloid beta antibody using transferrin receptor-mediated blood-brain barrier crossing (MODEL platform) for Alzheimer's disease. Currently in Phase 1.
- **Rinvoq Expansion (2025-2026):** Positive Phase 3 results in both vitiligo and alopecia areata. FDA/EMA applications submitted for vitiligo (February 2026). Alopecia areata filing underway. Already approved in GCA. Also in Phase 3 for hidradenitis suppurativa, Takayasu arteritis, and systemic lupus erythematosus.
- **Calico Partnership Ended (November 2025):** AbbVie terminated its 11-year, $1.75B aging research collaboration with Alphabet-backed Calico Life Sciences following the Phase II/III failure of fosigotifator in ALS. ~100 associated employees laid off. Signals AbbVie's strategic shift away from small-molecule aging research toward injectable biologics and genetic medicines.

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
AbbVie's R&D is led by Dr. Roopal Thakkar (EVP, Research & Development and Chief Scientific Officer, since 2024), reporting to new CEO Robert Michael. Dr. Thakkar's background spans development, regulatory affairs, and medical affairs -- having previously served as SVP of Development and Regulatory Affairs/CMO, and VP of Global Regulatory Affairs. R&D is organized by therapeutic area (Immunology, Oncology, Neuroscience, Aesthetics/Eye Care) with functional sub-units including Clinical Pharmacology, Data & Statistical Sciences (DSS), Development Sciences, Discovery Research, Global Medical Affairs, Health Economics Outcomes Research (HEOR), and Therapeutic Development. The ARCH (AbbVie R&D Convergence Hub) platform serves as the expanding analytics backbone, harmonizing data across therapeutic areas with 1.7 billion knowledge graphs and embedded generative AI; ARCH adoption is broadening as the portfolio diversifies into new modalities. Recent acquisitions have significantly expanded clinical development scope and complexity: Cerevel Therapeutics ($8.7B, neuroscience -- though the emraclidine schizophrenia program has stalled), Aliada Therapeutics ($1.4B, Alzheimer's brain shuttle), Capstan Therapeutics ($2.1B, in vivo CAR-T for autoimmune disease), Gilgamesh Pharmaceuticals ($1.2B, psychedelic-based psychiatry), and ImmunoGen (oncology ADCs). The termination of the Calico partnership (November 2025) removed aging/neurodegeneration small-molecule programs from the pipeline, while the Capstan and Gilgamesh acquisitions add entirely novel modality classes (in vivo cell therapy, psychedelic-assisted treatment) that require new clinical development infrastructure and expertise.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory Affairs at AbbVie sits within the broader R&D organization and is closely integrated with Development. Dr. Thakkar's career progression through Global Regulatory Affairs before becoming CSO reflects the strategic importance of regulatory at AbbVie. The function includes **Regulatory Operations, Policy & Intelligence** as a distinct sub-unit, indicating a dedicated team for regulatory strategy and horizon scanning. AbbVie's regulatory function also includes **Quality Assurance** oversight spanning pharmacovigilance, regulatory affairs, and medical affairs to ensure optimal QA oversight and continuous improvement. The regulatory team manages a high volume of submissions across immunology (Rinvoq FDA/EMA submissions for vitiligo filed February 2026; alopecia areata filing underway; already approved in GCA; Phase 3 ongoing for HS, Takayasu arteritis, SLE) and is simultaneously building regulatory capabilities in oncology (EMRELIS post-marketing confirmatory trial management; tavapadon NDA for Parkinson's) and neuroscience. The 2024-2025 acquisition wave adds unprecedented regulatory complexity: ADC post-marketing commitments (EMRELIS), psychedelic compound development (bretisilocin -- a novel regulatory pathway), in vivo CAR-T gene therapy classification questions (Capstan's CPTX2309), and blood-brain barrier crossing biologics (Aliada's ALIA-1758). Each of these modalities brings distinct regulatory frameworks that the team must build competency in simultaneously.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
AbbVie maintains a dedicated **Pharmacovigilance and Patient Safety (PPS)** function within R&D, which includes specialized teams for **Benefit-Risk Management**, the **QPPV (Qualified Person for Pharmacovigilance) Office**, and **PV Operations**. Product Safety Teams operate at the asset level, with daily safety monitoring conducted by Senior Drug Safety Associates working collaboratively with the Head of PV/Affiliate Safety Representative, PV Operations Manager, PV Compliance Manager, and PV Benefit Risk Manager. At the local/regional level, Therapy Area PV Specialists ensure compliance with local regulations. The **Office of Risk Management** is a distinct function within PPS that advises Product Safety Teams on risk management strategies, medication error prevention, and benefit-risk assessments across product development, new marketing applications, and marketed products. This Office collaborates with therapeutic area leads on early drug development approaches to anticipate and characterize risks, and recommends industry-leading approaches to quantify, analyze, and mitigate risks, including the use of digital tools, artificial intelligence, and educational frameworks.

### Evolving Safety Monitoring Demands (2025-2026)
PPS faces a rapidly expanding scope of modality-specific safety monitoring requirements:

- **EMRELIS (ADC) Post-Marketing Safety:** The May 2025 accelerated approval of EMRELIS brings ADC-specific safety monitoring obligations. The prescribing information carries warnings for ILD/pneumonitis (10% incidence, including 3 fatal cases in trials; median onset 48 days), peripheral neuropathy (51% incidence, Grade 3 in 11%), ocular surface disorders, and infusion-related reactions. Post-marketing pharmacovigilance must support the confirmatory Phase 3 trial (TeliMET-NSCLC-01) required to maintain accelerated approval status. This is AbbVie's first marketed ADC and requires building internal ADC-specific safety expertise.
- **Rinvoq JAK Inhibitor Safety (Ongoing, High Regulatory Scrutiny):** Rinvoq carries FDA boxed warnings for serious infections, mortality, malignancy, MACE, and thrombosis -- class-wide requirements for all JAK inhibitors. As Rinvoq expands into vitiligo (filed), alopecia areata (filing), and other new indications, PPS must manage B/R assessments across an ever-widening patient population while maintaining compliance with JAK class-wide REMS-like post-marketing requirements. Each new indication changes the denominator in the B/R calculus, particularly for cosmetic/quality-of-life indications where risk tolerance is lower.
- **Emraclidine B/R Lesson:** The Phase II failure is a significant B/R case study -- continuous, dynamic B/R monitoring during Phase 2 could have identified the efficacy gap between doses and placebo earlier, potentially informing dose optimization before the full program readout. The $3.5B writedown underscores the financial consequences of binary Phase 2 go/no-go decisions without intermediate B/R assessment tools. As AbbVie explores higher doses in a new ascending dose study, the need for real-time B/R signal integration is acute.
- **Novel Modality Safety Frameworks Needed:** The acquisition pipeline introduces modalities with limited PV precedent: in vivo CAR-T (Capstan's CPTX2309 -- cytokine release syndrome risk without ex vivo control, transient CAR expression kinetics), psychedelic-assisted therapy (Gilgamesh's bretisilocin -- acute psychoactive event monitoring, psychological adverse events, in-clinic supervision requirements), and brain shuttle antibodies (Aliada's ALIA-1758 -- CNS-specific safety monitoring for biologics crossing the BBB). Each demands new B/R frameworks that do not yet exist as industry standards.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
AbbVie has a particularly well-defined B/R governance structure:
- **Product Safety Teams** -- Asset-level safety monitoring and B/R assessment
- **PPS Benefit-Risk Management** -- Dedicated team promoting excellence in structured B/R assessment and patient-centered risk management throughout the product lifecycle
- **Office of Risk Management** -- Strategic advisory function for risk quantification, analysis, and mitigation using digital tools and AI
- **R&D Leadership** -- CSO and therapeutic area heads provide portfolio-level B/R governance

AbbVie explicitly promotes digital tools and AI for B/R assessment, making this one of the most receptive organizational structures in the industry for ArcaScience's platform.

## 4. Buying Pathway Hypothesis
- **Interest Initiator:** Office of Risk Management or Benefit-Risk Management team within PPS -- these functions have an explicit mandate to adopt digital tools and AI for B/R assessment, making them the most natural first contact
- **Scientific Validator:** Head of PPS and therapeutic area development leads (particularly oncology and neuroscience); they would evaluate whether the platform's methodology aligns with AbbVie's structured B/R assessment standards and supports the complex safety profiles of new modalities
- **Budget Owner:** CSO (Dr. Thakkar) for R&D-wide deployment; Head of PPS for function-specific deployment. Given Dr. Thakkar's regulatory affairs background, she would be personally receptive to platforms that strengthen regulatory submissions.
- **Procurement Cycle:** 9-12 months. AbbVie's explicit organizational commitment to digital tools and AI in B/R assessment reduces the typical education cycle. The Office of Risk Management's mandate creates a pre-existing business case framework. However, integration with the ARCH platform will be a technical requirement that adds evaluation time. The CEO transition (Michael fully in charge as Chairman/CEO from July 2025) creates a window for new vendor evaluations as the incoming leader establishes his own strategic priorities and technology stack.
- **Internal Friction Points:** The ARCH platform is a significant internal investment -- ArcaScience must demonstrate interoperability and complementarity with AbbVie's existing data infrastructure. AbbVie has executed an unprecedented wave of acquisitions in 2024-2025 (Cerevel, Aliada, ImmunoGen, Capstan, Gilgamesh, plus deals with ADARx, Gubra, Xilio), which may consume integration bandwidth but also create urgent need for scalable B/R tools to handle the modality explosion. Custom LLMs for SDTM conversion suggest AbbVie prefers building over buying for certain data capabilities. The Board-level review of AI risks and opportunities means any AI platform purchase will face executive scrutiny. The $3.5B emraclidine writedown and Calico partnership termination may increase internal pressure to demonstrate better risk management in development decisions, creating a favorable environment for tools that strengthen B/R assessment.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
**Recommended approach: Enter through the Office of Risk Management, positioning as the "industry-leading digital tool for B/R quantification" that AbbVie's own mandate describes -- now with urgency driven by the emraclidine writedown and the modality explosion from 2024-2025 acquisitions.**

AbbVie is arguably the single most aligned target for ArcaScience in the top 10 pharma companies. The company has a dedicated Office of Risk Management with an explicit mandate to adopt "digital tools, artificial intelligence, and educational frameworks" for benefit-risk quantification. This is not an interpretation -- it is a publicly documented organizational mandate. The 2024-2025 developments have made this even more urgent. The narrative should center on:

1. **Fulfilling the Office of Risk Management's stated mandate** -- ArcaScience's dynamic BRA platform is precisely the "industry-leading approach to quantify, analyze, and mitigate risks" that this function is tasked with recommending. This creates a pre-built internal business case.
2. **The emraclidine lesson: preventing the next $3.5B writedown** -- The emraclidine Phase II failure is the most powerful proof point for dynamic B/R assessment. A platform that continuously integrates efficacy signals, dose-response data, and safety profiles during Phase 2 could have identified the efficacy gap between doses and placebo earlier, enabling adaptive dose-escalation decisions before the binary pass/fail readout. Position ArcaScience as the tool that transforms binary go/no-go decisions into continuous B/R-informed development. This resonates especially as AbbVie is now running a higher-dose MAD study for emraclidine -- exactly the situation where real-time B/R assessment adds maximal value.
3. **EMRELIS ADC post-marketing safety as an immediate deployment opportunity** -- EMRELIS is AbbVie's first marketed ADC, carrying serious ILD/pneumonitis warnings (10% incidence, fatal cases) and peripheral neuropathy (51%). AbbVie needs to build ADC-specific safety monitoring capability from scratch while managing an accelerated approval confirmatory trial. Dynamic B/R assessment can provide real-time benefit-risk integration across the confirmatory Phase 3 and expanding post-marketing data, directly supporting the accelerated-to-full approval pathway.
4. **Supporting the post-Humira diversification B/R challenge** -- AbbVie is simultaneously managing mature immunology assets (Humira declining, Skyrizi/Rinvoq expanding into new indications with the JAK boxed warning calculus) and building entirely new therapeutic franchises across an unprecedented range of novel modalities. The modality count has exploded: ADCs (EMRELIS), in vivo CAR-T (Capstan), psychedelic compounds (Gilgamesh), brain shuttle antibodies (Aliada), bispecifics, molecular glue degraders, and more. Each modality class requires distinct B/R frameworks, and no single organization can build all of them from scratch simultaneously.
5. **Rinvoq JAK safety monitoring as an ongoing anchor use case** -- Rinvoq's expansion into vitiligo, alopecia areata, and other new indications continuously reshapes the B/R profile. The boxed warnings (infections, mortality, malignancy, MACE, thrombosis) create a particularly sensitive B/R environment for indications where risk tolerance is lower (cosmetic/QoL). Dynamic B/R assessment can provide the quantitative framework that regulators increasingly demand for JAK inhibitor benefit-risk justification in each new indication.
6. **Integrating with the ARCH platform** -- Position ArcaScience as the B/R-specific analytical engine within AbbVie's broader ARCH data ecosystem, feeding probabilistic B/R outputs into the 1.7 billion knowledge graphs.
7. **CEO transition as a strategic window** -- Michael's consolidation of leadership (Chairman/CEO from July 2025) creates a natural inflection point for new vendor evaluations and technology investments. The post-emraclidine environment increases C-suite receptivity to tools that improve development decision-making.

The strongest initial meeting should target the VP of PPS or Director of the Office of Risk Management, using AbbVie's own language about "digital tools and AI for risk quantification." The emraclidine writedown provides a concrete, recent, high-stakes reference point for why dynamic B/R assessment matters. Conference touchpoints at DIA Global and ICPE would be ideal. A secondary entry path through the oncology development team (EMRELIS ADC safety monitoring) offers an immediate, deployment-ready use case that can serve as a proof-of-value pilot.
