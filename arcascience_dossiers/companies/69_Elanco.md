# Elanco Animal Health

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** $4.439 billion (flat YoY reported, +3% organic CER); Pet Health $439M (Q4, +6%), Farm Animal $570M (Q4, -7% reported); adjusted EBITDA of $910M (20.5% margin)
- **Headquarters:** Greenfield, Indiana, USA
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Pet health (parasiticides, dermatology/allergic disease, pain, parvovirus), farm animal health (cattle, poultry, swine, sheep — production enhancement, disease prevention, nutritional health); key products include Zenrelia, Credelio Quattro, Befrena, Experior, CPMA
- **Pipeline Size:** 100+ active R&D programs balanced across species; 5-6 major differentiated product approvals expected between 2026-2031; monoclonal antibody (mAb) platform for companion animal; 2025 innovation revenue target $640-720M
- **Strategic Priorities:** Monoclonal antibody platform build-out (Zenrelia, Befrena, mAb manufacturing expansion), Credelio Quattro global launch, US-centric restructuring (Elanco Ascend), manufacturing expansion ($130M Kansas facility investment, $400M total US manufacturing/R&D investment), innovation revenue acceleration, margin expansion, farm animal protein future

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Elanco's R&D organization is led by the EVP of Research, Development and Regulatory Affairs. The company is restructuring to consolidate discovery, development, and regulatory preparation under a unified Innovation Laboratories at its Indiana headquarters. Clinical development for animal health products involves target animal safety studies, effectiveness studies, and CMC (Chemistry, Manufacturing, and Controls) technical sections. The monoclonal antibody platform is a key R&D priority with $130M manufacturing investment. R&D expenses were approximately $93M per quarter, increasing 15% driven by pipeline investments.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Animal health regulatory affairs interfaces with the FDA's Center for Veterinary Medicine (CVM) and USDA for approval of animal drugs and biologics. The regulatory process involves up to 5 major technical sections (Target Animal Safety, Effectiveness, CMC, Human Food Safety for farm animals, Environmental Impact for farm animals). The company has achieved USDA conditional approval for CPMA (canine parvovirus monoclonal antibody). Elanco Ascend restructuring cites "accelerated USDA regulatory times" as a factor favoring US-centric development. Zenrelia's US label includes a boxed warning based on safety trial outcomes, demonstrating the importance of B/R assessment in animal health.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
Animal health PV differs from human pharma PV but involves post-marketing surveillance for adverse events in animals and, for farm animal products, human food safety considerations. Zenrelia's boxed warning (based on 3x dose safety trial in unvaccinated dogs) exemplifies the importance of safety monitoring. The monoclonal antibody platform's expansion to multiple companion animal species creates new PV requirements. Farm animal products require monitoring for residue levels and food safety implications. Products are sold in 90+ countries, requiring multi-jurisdictional safety reporting.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance in animal health operates under a different framework than human pharma, with different regulatory standards and endpoints. The Zenrelia boxed warning demonstrates that B/R assessment remains critical even in animal health. The company's strategy of "advancing only those with the highest probability of success" across 100+ R&D programs requires portfolio-level B/R prioritization. Farm animal products involve additional B/R dimensions including human food safety, environmental impact, and antimicrobial resistance considerations.
