# Almirall

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** EUR 985.7M (~$1.1B; 10.6% YoY growth, surpassed guidance)
- **Headquarters:** Barcelona, Spain
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Medical dermatology (atopic dermatitis, psoriasis, actinic keratosis, hidradenitis suppurativa, epidermolysis bullosa); biologics (Ebglyss/lebrikizumab, Ilumetri/tildrakizumab); small molecules (Wynzora, Klisyri)
- **Pipeline Size:** Deep dermatology pipeline including Phase I-II assets (ALM27134 anti-IL-1RAP, ALM223 IL-2 mutant fusion protein, anti-IL-21 mAb licensed from Novo Nordisk, ZKN-013 readthrough inducer); late-stage lifecycle management (efinaconazole EU, sarecycline China, Klisyri large field); 4 Proof of Concept studies planned within 15 months
- **Strategic Priorities:** Leadership in medical dermatology; biologics portfolio expansion (peak sales >EUR 800M by 2030); R&D investment at 12.6% of sales; AI-based drug discovery (Microsoft collaboration); biomarker development (CRG partnership); European market expansion for Ebglyss

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Almirall's R&D pipeline is exclusively focused on medical dermatology, providing a highly concentrated clinical development organization. Development is organized across late-stage lifecycle management programs (efinaconazole, sarecycline, Klisyri label expansion) and early-stage innovative programs (autoimmune dermatological diseases). The company manages both internally developed assets and licensed programs (anti-IL-21 from Novo Nordisk, ZKN-013 from Eloxx, ALM223 co-developed with Simcere). Clinical operations support multi-country European trials and global registrational programs.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs manages filings across European markets (EMA decentralized procedure for efinaconazole), China (NMPA for sarecycline), and the US (FDA for Klisyri large field). The team has demonstrated expertise in dermatology-specific regulatory pathways and manages the rolling market-by-market launch of Ebglyss across Europe (11 countries launched by early 2025). Regulatory strategy supports both innovative biologics and small molecule dermatology products.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
Pharmacovigilance manages safety monitoring for a growing portfolio of marketed dermatology products, including biologics (Ebglyss, Ilumetri) with immunological mechanisms requiring careful safety surveillance. The PV team supports the large-scale ADTrust observational study (1,200 patients, 2-year prospective study for Ebglyss) and ongoing post-marketing surveillance across multiple European markets.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance is concentrated within the dermatology-focused R&D organization, with portfolio decisions involving clinical, regulatory, and commercial leadership. The strategic decision to pursue 4 Proof of Concept studies across different dermatological diseases within 15 months requires disciplined B/R assessment to allocate resources effectively. Benefit-risk considerations in dermatology include both efficacy endpoints and quality-of-life measures.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
Almirall's exclusive focus on medical dermatology and its ambitious pipeline expansion (4 PoC studies in 15 months, biologics peak sales target >EUR 800M) create a clear need for disciplined B/R assessment. ArcaScience should position the BRA platform as a dermatology-specialized decision tool that: (1) optimizes resource allocation across the 4 planned PoC studies by providing probabilistic B/R modeling for different autoimmune dermatological diseases, (2) supports the Ebglyss European rollout with integrated real-world evidence from the ADTrust study feeding back into the B/R profile, and (3) enhances the existing Microsoft AI collaboration by providing a domain-specific B/R layer. Almirall's existing comfort with AI-based approaches (Microsoft partnership, biomarker programs with CRG) lowers the adoption barrier. A compelling entry point would be the ALM27134 (anti-IL-1RAP for hidradenitis suppurativa) program, where Phase I-to-Phase II transition represents a critical B/R decision point. The focused therapeutic area means a single successful pilot would have immediate relevance across the entire pipeline.
