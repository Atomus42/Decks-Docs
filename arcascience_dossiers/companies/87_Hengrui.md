# Hengrui Medicine

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** ~RMB 27.8B (~$3.8B; H1 2024 revenue RMB 13.6B, up 21.78% YoY; innovative drug income RMB 6.6B, up 33% YoY)
- **Headquarters:** Lianyungang, Jiangsu Province, China
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Oncology (ADCs via HRMAP platform, immunotherapy, targeted therapy), autoimmune diseases, anesthesia/pain management, cardiovascular, metabolic diseases, respiratory, ophthalmology
- **Pipeline Size:** 400+ ongoing clinical trials (20+ international studies); 23 new molecular entities commercialized in China; 10+ differentiated ADC candidates in clinical development; 14 global R&D centers; key assets include trastuzumab rezetecan (HER2 ADC), SHR-1701 (anti-PD-L1/TGF-beta bispecific, NDA accepted), HRS-9821 (PDE3/4 inhibitor for COPD, licensed to GSK); $5B+ invested in R&D since 2024
- **Strategic Priorities:** Global expansion through licensing partnerships (GSK $12B potential milestone deal for 12 programs); becoming a top global innovator in oncology ADCs; pipeline diversification beyond oncology into cardiovascular, metabolic, autoimmune; China+Global dual-market regulatory strategy; comprehensive ADC platform (HRMAP)

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Hengrui operates one of the largest clinical development organizations among Chinese pharma companies, with 400+ ongoing clinical trials across 14 global R&D centers. Clinical development is organized by therapeutic area with oncology as the dominant franchise. The company's HRMAP (Rapid Modular ADC Platform) provides an integrated end-to-end capability from molecular design through clinical translation. International clinical trials (20+ studies) and the GSK partnership signal a transition from China-only to global development programs. The R&D pipeline increased by 19% in 2024.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs manages a high-volume NMPA filing operation with growing FDA/EMA capabilities through international partnerships. The NMPA accepted the NDA for SHR-1701 in 2024. Multiple breakthrough therapy designations have been secured (8 for trastuzumab rezetecan alone). The GSK deal for up to 12 programs requires international regulatory expertise. Regulatory strategy increasingly spans both NMPA and global regulatory pathways.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV manages safety monitoring for 23 commercialized new molecular entities and 400+ ongoing clinical trials. The oncology portfolio requires specialized safety monitoring for immunotherapies, ADCs, and targeted agents. Post-marketing surveillance in China (GVP compliance) and expanding international safety reporting (for GSK-partnered programs) demand a sophisticated PV organization.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance operates across a massive pipeline, with portfolio-level decisions informed by therapeutic area leaders and R&D management. The GSK partnership ($500M upfront, $12B potential milestones) demonstrates that international partners evaluate Hengrui's pipeline through rigorous B/R frameworks. The company's 10+ ADC candidates compete for clinical development resources, requiring systematic B/R-driven prioritization.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
Hengrui's GSK partnership ($12B potential milestones for 12 programs) fundamentally changes its B/R governance requirements — multinational partners expect structured, quantitative B/R frameworks that align with global regulatory standards. ArcaScience should position the BRA platform as: (1) a partnership-readiness tool that provides the B/R assessment framework expected by GSK and future international partners, (2) an ADC portfolio optimizer that helps prioritize among 10+ HRMAP ADC candidates based on probabilistic B/R profiling, and (3) a bridge between NMPA clinical evidence and FDA/EMA regulatory strategy for programs transitioning to global development. The GSK partnership is the critical entry wedge — demonstrating how ArcaScience supports the international development programs that GSK will evaluate for option decisions. A pilot focused on the most advanced GSK-partnered program (HRS-9821 PDE3/4 inhibitor for COPD) would have immediate strategic relevance.
