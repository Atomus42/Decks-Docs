# Sarepta Therapeutics

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2024):** $1.90 billion total revenue (up 56% YoY); ELEVIDYS gene therapy at $820.8M; PMO RNA franchise at $967.2M; full-year guidance exceeded by $100M+
- **Headquarters:** Cambridge, Massachusetts, USA
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Rare genetic neuromuscular diseases — Duchenne muscular dystrophy (DMD, gene therapy and RNA), limb-girdle muscular dystrophies (LGMDs), spinocerebellar ataxias, facioscapulohumeral dystrophy (FSHD), myotonic dystrophy (DM1); expanding into CNS (Huntington's disease, ALS) via siRNA
- **Pipeline Size:** 40+ programs across gene therapy, RNA (PMO), siRNA, and gene editing platforms; ELEVIDYS (gene therapy, approved), 3 marketed PMOs (EXONDYS 51, VYONDYS 53, AMONDYS 45); SRP-9003 (LGMD2E gene therapy, Phase 3 complete); Arrowhead siRNA collaboration (7 clinical/preclinical programs); strategic pivot to siRNA following 2025 safety events
- **Strategic Priorities:** siRNA-focused pivot following gene therapy safety events, ELEVIDYS franchise optimization (back on market for ambulatory patients), LGMD gene therapy pipeline, Arrowhead siRNA partnership execution (DM1, FSHD, SCA programs), cost restructuring ($400M savings by 2026, 36% workforce reduction), gene editing platform advancement, precision genetic medicine leadership

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Sarepta's clinical development has undergone a major strategic shift following 2025 safety events (deaths linked to ELEVIDYS and SRP-9004). The company is transitioning from gene therapy as the primary platform to siRNA-focused development. The Arrowhead Pharmaceuticals collaboration provides 4 clinical-stage and 3 preclinical siRNA programs. Current clinical operations span the ELEVIDYS post-marketing program (back on market for ambulatory DMD patients), SRP-9003 LGMD2E BLA submission, and multiple siRNA programs (SRP-1004 for SCA2, ARO-ATXN1 for SCA1, ARO-ATXN3 for SCA3, ARO-HTT for Huntington's). The 36% workforce reduction in July 2025 significantly impacts clinical development capacity.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs has navigated some of the most complex regulatory pathways in biopharma: ELEVIDYS received accelerated approval (June 2023), broad label expansion (June 2024), then FDA-directed shipping pause (July 2025) and resumption for ambulatory patients. The team manages post-marketing commitments for ELEVIDYS, accelerated approval obligations, and REMS-like safety monitoring. SRP-9003 BLA submission requires regulatory coordination for a gene therapy in rare LGMD. The siRNA pipeline introduces new regulatory requirements (Arrowhead's TRiM CNS delivery platform for subcutaneous administration). Orphan drug designations and pediatric disease regulatory pathways are core competencies.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV has become the most critical function at Sarepta following the ELEVIDYS and SRP-9004 safety events. The deaths linked to severe acute liver failure after gene therapy administration have resulted in FDA clinical trial pauses, shipping restrictions, and heightened post-marketing surveillance. PV now monitors ELEVIDYS patients for liver toxicity, immune responses, and long-term gene therapy effects. The PMO franchise (3 marketed products for DMD) requires ongoing chronic therapy PV. The siRNA pipeline will bring different safety considerations including off-target gene silencing and CNS delivery safety.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance is at the center of Sarepta's current strategic crisis. The gene therapy safety events have fundamentally altered the company's B/R calculus, leading to the strategic pivot from gene therapy to siRNA. The FDA's decision to allow ELEVIDYS back on market for ambulatory patients represents a B/R determination that the benefit for this specific population outweighs the identified liver toxicity risk. SRP-9003 BLA submission requires demonstrating favorable B/R in LGMD despite platform-level safety concerns. The Arrowhead siRNA collaboration involves B/R evaluation of new modalities and delivery platforms. Board and executive-level B/R governance has been elevated to the highest strategic priority.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
Sarepta represents a unique and urgent opportunity for ArcaScience. The gene therapy safety events (deaths from ELEVIDYS and SRP-9004) have created an existential need for continuous, real-time benefit-risk assessment. Position the BRA platform as critical infrastructure for three immediate needs: (1) ELEVIDYS post-marketing B/R monitoring for ambulatory patients (the FDA-permitted population), enabling real-time safety signal detection and B/R profile characterization that supports continued market access; (2) SRP-9003 BLA B/R package for LGMD, where the gene therapy platform safety concerns require the most rigorous B/R documentation possible; and (3) siRNA platform B/R framework establishment for the new strategic direction (Arrowhead collaboration programs). The BRA platform's ability to integrate clinical trial and real-world evidence is directly aligned with Sarepta's need to continuously demonstrate that gene therapy benefits outweigh risks for specific patient populations. However, recognize the financial constraints (36% workforce reduction, $400M cost target) and position as a cost-effective tool that addresses the company's most critical strategic need. Engage directly with the CMO or Chief Safety Officer, who have outsized organizational influence in the current environment. This is a high-urgency but financially constrained opportunity.
