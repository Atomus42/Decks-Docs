# Jazz Pharmaceuticals

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2025):** $4.3B (record; +10% YoY in Q4; 21st consecutive year of top-line growth; Xywav $1.66B, Epidiolex $1.06B, Rylaze $403M, Zepzelca $307M)
- **Headquarters:** Dublin, Ireland (significant U.S. operations in Palo Alto, CA and Philadelphia, PA)
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Sleep disorders (narcolepsy, idiopathic hypersomnia), epilepsy (Dravet syndrome, LGS, TSC), oncology (SCLC, AML, ALL, BTC, GEA, breast cancer, DMG)
- **Pipeline Size:** 10+ clinical programs; key assets include Ziihera (zanidatamab, approved 2024 BTC, sBLA for GEA expected Q1 2026), Modeyso (dordaviprone, FDA accelerated approval Aug 2025 for DMG), Zepzelca + atezolizumab (FDA approved 2025 for 1L ES-SCLC maintenance), suvecaltamide (Phase 3, Parkinson's tremor), JZP441 (orexin-2 agonist), SAN2355 (Kv7.2/Kv7.3 activator, licensed from Saniona Aug 2025), JZP351 (MDS/AML), JZP898 (INDUKINE)
- **Strategic Priorities:** Oncology pipeline expansion (Ziihera peak sales potential >$2B; GEA launch expected H2 2026); Modeyso commercial ramp ($48M since Aug 2025 launch); Xywav and Epidiolex franchise protection; portfolio diversification across three $1B+ therapeutic areas; 2026 revenue guidance $4.25-4.50B; Ziihera global launch across multiple indications (BTC approved in U.S., EU conditional approval Jun 2025, Canada Jan 2026; GEA and breast cancer in development)
- **Recent M&A:** Chimerix acquisition completed Mar 2025 ($935M; added Modeyso/dordaviprone for DMG); Saniona SAN2355 global licensing agreement (Aug 2025; Kv7 activator for epilepsy)
- **Leadership Change:** Renee Gala appointed President and CEO effective Aug 2025 (succeeded long-time CEO); Thomas Riga appointed Chief Business Officer effective Jan 2026 (joined via Chimerix acquisition)

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Jazz Pharmaceuticals' clinical development is led by EVP Global Head of R&D and CMO Robert Iannone, M.D. The organization spans three therapeutic areas: neuroscience (sleep + epilepsy) and oncology. Clinical programs are global, with trial sites across North America, Europe, and Asia. The oncology pipeline expanded significantly in 2025 through the Chimerix acquisition (adding Modeyso/dordaviprone for DMG) and continued zanidatamab lifecycle development. The Phase 3 HERIZON-GEA-01 trial produced practice-changing results, supporting zanidatamab as the HER2-targeted agent of choice in 1L GEA regardless of PD-L1 status; an sBLA filing is expected in Q1 2026, with potential GEA launch in H2 2026. The EmpowHER breast cancer trial is ongoing (results expected late 2027/early 2028). The neuroscience pipeline includes novel mechanism-of-action programs (suvecaltamide T-type calcium channel blocker, JZP441 orexin-2 receptor agonist) and was further expanded by the SAN2355 licensing from Saniona (Kv7.2/Kv7.3 activator for epilepsy, preclinical). Jazz has manufacturing facilities in Ireland, England, and Italy. Cooperative group collaborations (Children's Oncology Group, European MDS Cooperative Group, MD Anderson Cancer Center) support hematology programs, including Phase 1/2 Vyxeos investigations in higher-risk MDS and relapsed/refractory AML.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Regulatory affairs manages FDA, EMA, and global submissions. The team delivered three major regulatory milestones in 2024-2025: FDA accelerated approval of Ziihera for BTC (Nov 2024), FDA accelerated approval of Modeyso for H3 K27M-mutant DMG (Aug 2025), and FDA approval of Zepzelca + atezolizumab for 1L ES-SCLC maintenance (2025). Global regulatory coordination is advancing with Ziihera receiving EC conditional marketing authorization (Jun 2025) and Health Canada conditional approval (Jan 2026). The zanidatamab sBLA for GEA is expected by end of Q1 2026. The regulatory team navigates both accelerated/expedited pathways (Ziihera, Modeyso) and traditional pathways (suvecaltamide Phase 3). Irish-domiciled structure requires coordination between European and U.S. regulatory operations.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
PV manages safety across three distinct therapeutic portfolios: oxybate-based sleep medicines (Xywav/Xyrem with REMS programs and abuse-potential monitoring), cannabidiol-based epilepsy treatment (Epidiolex with controlled substance considerations), and an expanding oncology portfolio (cytotoxics, antibodies, ADCs, and now the targeted small molecule Modeyso). The Modeyso launch in 2025 for ultra-rare DMG adds new PV complexity with limited long-term safety data in a small patient population. The oxybate REMS program remains one of the most complex in the pharmaceutical industry, requiring comprehensive safety monitoring and distribution controls. Jazz PV leadership is active in industry pharmacovigilance forums, with executives presenting at both Drug Safety Europe and Pharmacovigilance USA conferences in 2025. The company is investing in AI/ML/GenAI capabilities for clinical trial execution and digital healthcare, with dedicated AI data science roles focused on optimizing clinical operations and PV compliance readiness.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
B/R governance operates under EVP Global Head of R&D and CMO Robert Iannone, with cross-functional input from regulatory, safety, and commercial teams. The three-pillar portfolio structure (sleep, epilepsy, oncology) means B/R decisions span very different therapeutic contexts. The rapid expansion of the oncology portfolio in 2025 — with Modeyso under accelerated approval (requiring confirmatory trial), Ziihera expanding from BTC to GEA and breast cancer, and Zepzelca approved in a new 1L maintenance setting — significantly increases the complexity and volume of B/R governance decisions. The CEO transition to Renee Gala (Aug 2025) and new CBO Thomas Riga (Jan 2026) create an evolving governance environment with new leadership dynamics.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
ArcaScience should target Jazz through the Ziihera (zanidatamab) franchise expansion and the broader oncology portfolio acceleration under new CEO Renee Gala. Ziihera has peak sales potential exceeding $2B across BTC, GEA, and breast cancer — making it the single most important pipeline asset. The sBLA for GEA is imminent (Q1 2026 filing, H2 2026 potential launch), creating an immediate use case. The pitch should focus on: (1) supporting Ziihera's multi-indication development with dynamic B/R assessment that integrates practice-changing HERIZON-GEA-01 Phase 3 data with BTC real-world evidence from 2024-2025 launch into a unified B/R profile across all zanidatamab indications, (2) enabling probabilistic approval prediction for the GEA sBLA and future breast cancer filing (EmpowHER data expected 2027-2028), and (3) managing B/R for Modeyso under accelerated approval — as an ultra-rare DMG therapy, the confirmatory trial requirement makes dynamic B/R updating critical. Jazz's active investment in AI/ML/GenAI capabilities for clinical operations suggests organizational readiness for an AI-powered B/R platform. Position ArcaScience as the tool that helps Jazz manage B/R across its unusually diverse portfolio during a period of rapid portfolio expansion under new leadership. Engage at ASCO (oncology), AES (epilepsy), or directly through the new CBO Thomas Riga, who is focused on accelerating corporate development across rare disease.
