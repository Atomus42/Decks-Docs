# Johnson & Johnson

## 1. Company Overview
- **Revenue (FY2025):** $94.2 billion total company (6.0% growth YoY); Innovative Medicine segment ~$57B+; MedTech ~$32B. FY2024 was $88.8 billion total.
- **Headquarters:** New Brunswick, New Jersey, USA
- **Corporate Structure:** Following the 2023 Kenvue consumer health spinoff (now being acquired by Kimberly-Clark for ~$48.7B), J&J operates as a focused two-segment company: **Innovative Medicine** (pharmaceuticals) and **MedTech** (medical devices/surgery). A further spinoff of the DePuy Synthes orthopedics business (~$9B revenue) is planned for late 2026, which will further concentrate the company on high-growth pharma and surgical innovation.
- **Therapeutic Focus Areas:** Oncology, Immunology, Neuroscience, Cardiovascular & Metabolism, Pulmonary Hypertension, Infectious Diseases, Autoimmune/Rare Disease
- **Pipeline Size:** ~107 programs across Innovative Medicine and MedTech; 18 positive registrational studies, 17 new Phase III initiations, and 49 regulatory filings in major markets in 2024
- **Key 2025 FDA Approvals:**
  - **DARZALEX FASPRO** (daratumumab) -- First-ever approved treatment for high-risk smoldering multiple myeloma (HR-SMM), approved Nov 2025 based on the AQUILA Phase 3 trial (51% reduction in progression/death vs. active monitoring). Creates an unprecedented B/R paradigm: treating a pre-malignant, asymptomatic condition with immunotherapy.
  - **AKEEGA** (niraparib + abiraterone acetate) -- Expanded indication approved Dec 2025 for BRCA2-mutated metastatic castration-sensitive prostate cancer (mCSPC), the first precision therapy for this population. 54% reduction in radiographic progression.
  - **RYBREVANT FASPRO** (subcutaneous amivantamab + hyaluronidase) -- Approved Dec 2025 for all IV amivantamab indications in EGFR-mutated NSCLC. Reduces administration time from ~5 hours to ~5 minutes; reduces infusion-related reactions from 66% to 13% and VTE rates from 18% to 11%.
  - **IMAAVY** (nipocalimab) -- FcRn-blocking antibody approved Apr 2025 for generalized myasthenia gravis (gMG), the broadest gMG population for an FcRn blocker. Acquired via the $6.5B Momenta Pharmaceuticals deal; projected peak sales >$5B. sNDA submitted for warm autoimmune hemolytic anemia (wAIHA).
  - **SPRAVATO** (esketamine) -- sNDA approved Jan 2025 as first and only monotherapy for treatment-resistant depression (TRD), removing prior requirement for oral antidepressant co-administration.
  - **CAPLYTA** (lumateperone) -- sNDA accepted for adjunctive treatment of major depressive disorder (MDD), expanding the neuroscience portfolio acquired via Intra-Cellular Therapies.
- **Major Acquisitions:** Intra-Cellular Therapies acquisition ($14.6B) closed Apr 2025, adding CAPLYTA (schizophrenia, bipolar depression) and ITI-1284 (GAD, Alzheimer's psychosis) to solidify J&J's ambition to become the #1 neuroscience company worldwide. Halda Therapeutics also acquired in 2025.
- **AI/Innovation Initiatives:** Polyphonic AI Fund for Surgery launched Jun 2025 (MedTech segment) in partnership with NVIDIA and AWS, funding startups in surgical AI, data governance, and perioperative decision-making. Initial QuickFire Challenge awardees include Mayo Clinic and QAS.AI. Separately, J&J CIO Jim Swanson confirmed a strategic pivot from testing 900+ generative AI use cases to prioritizing high-impact deployment in drug discovery, supply chain, and pharmacovigilance.
- **Strategic Priorities:** AI-powered clinical development ("Team of Teams" framework), real-world evidence generation, regulatory acceleration via digital tools, massive inorganic growth (~$50B invested since Jan 2024), neuroscience expansion, bispecific antibody portfolio leadership, and earlier-line treatment paradigms (SMM, mCSPC)

## 2. Clinical & Regulatory Organizational Mapping

### Clinical Development Structure
Johnson & Johnson's Innovative Medicine division operates under EVP/Worldwide Chairman Jennifer L. Taubert. R&D investment was $13.53 billion in 2024 (23.7% of Innovative Medicine sales). Clinical development is organized by therapeutic area (Oncology, Immunology, Neuroscience, Infectious Diseases, Cardiovascular & Metabolism, Pulmonary Hypertension) within a global matrix structure. The company has adopted an innovative "Team of Teams" approach unveiled at DPHARM 2024, which uses Slack Enterprise Grid as a multi-channel collaboration platform to democratize knowledge, streamline workflows, and enable asynchronous and real-time cross-functional collaboration across drug development programs. Nicole Turner (Senior Director, Global Development, Data Science & Digital Health) leads AI integration for clinical trials, including targeted recruitment and personalized care initiatives.

### Regulatory Affairs Structure
Global Regulatory Affairs (GRA) is a centralized function within the Innovative Medicine division. It is organized with both therapeutic area alignment and functional sub-groups including Product Labeling, Policy & Intelligence, and Chemistry, Manufacturing & Controls (CMC). Regional GRA offices operate under a global leadership team: HyangWon (Harriet) Min serves as VP of Regulatory Affairs for Asia Pacific, sitting on the Global Regulatory Affairs Senior Leadership Team. The GRA Scholar Program rotates fellows through multiple disease-therapeutic areas and regulatory sub-functions over two years. Regulatory strategy is also supported by the Asia Pacific Strategy Organization (APSO) led by VP Matthias Skillecorn, which integrates Regional Therapeutic Area Leaders, Market Access, and Medical Affairs.

### Pharmacovigilance / Drug Safety Structure
Patient safety is governed by the Office of the Chief Medical Officer (OCMO), established in 2013 as a functionally independent global safety organization under Penny Heaton, M.D. (Global Head, OCMO). Stephen Lin serves as Chief Safety Officer, VP, and Head of Global Medical Safety for Innovative Medicine. The OCMO is structurally independent from commercial interests and applies a single medical safety standard across all J&J sectors. At the local level, pharmacovigilance specialists are embedded within Innovative Medicine OCMO to ensure local PV compliance. J&J has deployed machine learning-enabled, rapid-cycle analytics for safety signal evaluation, reducing safety evidence generation from months to days. The company is also piloting generative AI for pharmacovigilance efficiency improvements and using AI-enabled technologies for safe dose selection and signal detection.

**2025 Safety Landscape Developments:**
- **Bispecific antibody safety monitoring** is a rapidly growing area. J&J's TECVAYLI (teclistamab, BCMAxCD3) and TALVEY (talquetamab, GPRC5DxCD3) both carry boxed warnings for CRS and ICANS. Step-up dosing protocols, prophylactic tocilizumab strategies (OPTec study showed zero CRS in initial patients), and real-world safety data generation (Blood Cancer Journal 2025: 54% CRS incidence with talquetamab, 7.3% grade 2+) create ongoing demand for dynamic safety monitoring tools. The dual bispecific combination (teclistamab + talquetamab, RedirecTT-1 study) adds further monitoring complexity.
- **CAR-T REMS elimination:** In June 2025, the FDA eliminated REMS requirements for all six approved CAR-T therapies including Carvykti, removing hospital certification requirements and shortening post-treatment monitoring from 4 weeks to 2 weeks. However, 15-year post-marketing observational safety studies for secondary malignancies remain mandatory. A new boxed warning for immune effector cell-associated enterocolitis (IEC-EC) was added to Carvykti's label in November 2025. Carvykti reached $808M in H1 2025 sales, surpassing Yescarta as the top-selling CAR-T globally.
- **Spravato REMS complexity** remains significant: the drug requires administration only in certified healthcare settings under direct observation with 2-hour post-dose monitoring, no home use permitted, and enrollment of patients, pharmacies, and treatment settings in the REMS program. The January 2025 monotherapy approval expands the eligible TRD population but does not relieve REMS burden, creating ongoing compliance and monitoring infrastructure needs.
- **SMM treatment safety paradigm:** The DARZALEX FASPRO approval for smoldering myeloma creates an entirely new B/R framework -- treating asymptomatic patients with immunotherapy, where tolerability and long-term safety carry outsized weight relative to efficacy in the benefit-risk equation.

### Benefit-Risk Governance
J&J employs a multi-tiered safety governance model:
1. **Safety Management Teams** -- Day-to-day safety reviews and product-level B/R decisions
2. **Sector Medical Safety Councils** -- Chaired by sector Chief Medical Officers; handle complex safety assessments
3. **J&J Medical Safety Council** -- Chaired by the J&J Chief Medical Officer; sets enterprise-wide safety standards and policies
4. **Enterprise Compliance Risk Committee (ECRC)** -- Cross-functional senior leaders providing governance over risk management across all segments

J&J Innovative Medicine has been a pioneer in structured benefit-risk assessments, including incorporation of patient preference information. Ellen Janssen (Director, Global Epidemiology and Benefit-Risk Assessment, Janssen R&D) leads structured B/R assessments throughout the product lifecycle. On the MedTech side, a quantitative benefit-risk assessment framework was recently deployed for pipeline and marketed device risk profiling.

**Emerging B/R challenges (2025-2026):** The expanding bispecific and cell therapy portfolio (teclistamab, talquetamab, Carvykti, and combinations) creates unprecedented multi-product B/R monitoring demands where CRS/ICANS management, step-up dosing safety, and infection risk must be continuously weighed against deep response rates in heavily pretreated patients. The DARZALEX FASPRO SMM approval introduces a fundamentally new B/R calculus: the AQUILA study's ODAC vote was 6-2, reflecting genuine regulatory debate about treating pre-malignant patients with active immunotherapy. J&J's B/R governance framework must now accommodate both "treat the critically ill" and "prevent progression in the asymptomatic" paradigms simultaneously.

## 4. Buying Pathway Hypothesis
- **Interest Initiator:** Director-level within OCMO's Benefit-Risk Assessment or Global Medical Safety team, or the Data Science & Digital Health function -- these groups already use ML-based safety analytics and would recognize the BRA platform's value
- **Scientific Validator:** Chief Safety Officer and/or Director of Global Epidemiology and Benefit-Risk Assessment; they would evaluate scientific rigor, methodology alignment with ICH frameworks, and integration with existing rapid-cycle analytics
- **Budget Owner:** Global Head, OCMO (for enterprise deployment) or VP-level GRA/Therapeutic Area Lead (for therapeutic area-specific deployment)
- **Procurement Cycle:** 9-15 months. J&J's decentralized structure means individual operating companies may pilot independently, but enterprise deployment requires OCMO and IT alignment. The "Team of Teams" culture favors cross-functional evaluation committees.
- **Internal Friction Points:** J&J has invested heavily in proprietary ML-based safety analytics -- ArcaScience must demonstrate complementarity rather than redundancy. IT security review will be rigorous given the Slack Enterprise Grid and cloud infrastructure already in place. Preferred vendor lists and J&J's external innovation partnerships (e.g., the $50B inorganic investment strategy) may require navigating business development channels. J&J's decentralized structure means each operating company has its own decision-making authority, which can slow enterprise-wide adoption but also create multiple entry points. The Polyphonic AI Fund for Surgery (MedTech segment) and CIO Jim Swanson's stated pivot from "a thousand flowers to a prioritized focus on gen AI" signal an increasingly rigorous evaluation bar for external AI vendors -- ArcaScience must demonstrate clear differentiation from J&J's internal AI capabilities. The post-Kenvue two-segment structure may simplify procurement paths within Innovative Medicine but the upcoming DePuy Synthes spinoff means organizational attention may be divided through late 2026.

## 5. Strategic Entry Angle
**Recommended approach: Enter through bispecific antibody safety monitoring in oncology, with a secondary play on the unprecedented SMM benefit-risk paradigm.**

J&J's OCMO has already demonstrated commitment to ML-based rapid-cycle safety analytics and structured benefit-risk assessment. The highest-value entry angle has sharpened significantly in 2025: **bispecific antibody safety monitoring** is now the strongest near-term opportunity. J&J operates the industry's most complex bispecific portfolio (TECVAYLI/teclistamab, TALVEY/talquetamab, RYBREVANT FASPRO/amivantamab, plus dual bispecific combinations), each requiring CRS/ICANS monitoring, step-up dosing safety management, and continuous real-world evidence generation. With the June 2025 elimination of CAR-T REMS, the safety monitoring burden has partially shifted from REMS-mandated infrastructure to pharmacovigilance-led ongoing surveillance -- exactly where a dynamic BRA platform adds value. The narrative should center on how ArcaScience's platform can:

1. **Power bispecific safety monitoring at scale** by providing continuous, probabilistic B/R profiling across J&J's multi-product bispecific portfolio -- integrating step-up dosing CRS rates, prophylactic tocilizumab outcomes, real-world infection data, and combination safety signals (teclistamab + talquetamab) into a single dynamic framework
2. **Support the unprecedented SMM benefit-risk paradigm** created by the DARZALEX FASPRO approval for smoldering myeloma -- the first-ever treatment for a pre-malignant, asymptomatic condition. The 6-2 ODAC vote reflects genuine B/R ambiguity; J&J needs a dynamic platform to continuously reassess the risk-benefit as long-term data accumulates on treating asymptomatic patients with immunotherapy
3. **Complement existing ML safety analytics** by adding continuous, probabilistic B/R profiling that integrates clinical trial data with real-world evidence -- accelerating the months-to-days signal evaluation J&J has already achieved
4. **Support regulatory acceleration** for the 49+ annual filings J&J makes across major markets, particularly in oncology where accelerated pathways (breakthrough therapy, conditional approval) require robust B/R documentation
5. **Enable the "Team of Teams" vision** by providing a shared, real-time B/R dashboard that democratizes safety intelligence across the cross-functional collaboration model J&J is building

A secondary entry angle is the **neuroscience portfolio expansion** (the $14.6B Intra-Cellular Therapies acquisition closed Apr 2025) where CAPLYTA (schizophrenia, bipolar depression, MDD) and ITI-1284 (GAD, Alzheimer's psychosis) have complex CNS safety profiles requiring ongoing B/R monitoring, alongside SPRAVATO's persistent REMS complexity.

A third, more strategic entry angle is through the **Polyphonic AI Fund ecosystem**. While the Fund is MedTech-focused (surgical AI), it signals J&J's institutional appetite for AI partnerships and startup collaboration. ArcaScience could position itself as a natural Innovative Medicine counterpart to the surgical AI fund concept -- a startup-scale AI partner for the drug safety side of the house, leveraging the same partnership model J&J has validated with NVIDIA/AWS on the MedTech side.

The strongest conference touchpoints would be at DPHARM, DIA Global, ISPE, and ASH (where J&J's bispecific and myeloma data are prominently featured), as well as J&J Innovation's QuickFire Challenge events.
