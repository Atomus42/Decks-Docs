Goals & means:
Reveal the Risk and Benefit of a monoclonal antibody
32 million open access datasets + 5200 pages of Sanofi internal data

Output & take away:
One predictive benefit-risk value tree & cross-comparison between 1st and 2nd gen + A benefit–risk competition analysis 27 key inflammatory adverse events revealed
64 key biomarkers for clinical confirmation 

Impact:
Literature review time reduced from 18 months to 2 weeks

Unlocking Success through Collaboration
Goals & means:
Out of 100 selected drugs available on the market, find the best candidates for being repurposed to glioblastoma. 
The first key task is to prove if there is a crossing of the BBB (Blood Brain Barrier), induced by these drugs. 

Output & take away:
1 dataset showcasing the safety datapoints directly connected to the identification of the crossing of the BBB  (eye deficiency, headache, earing issue, etc.)

Impact:
3 drugs were found to be compatible with glioblastoma, 2 are now pushed in phase 2 of clinical trials
