# T2 — Meeting Framing Templates & Agendas

> Use these templates to frame, run, and document every recurring meeting in the OKR cadence. Every meeting must have a clear purpose, time-boxed agenda, and written action items.

## Meeting Hygiene Rules

1. **No agenda, no meeting.** Cancel if the agenda is empty.
2. **Start on time, end on time.** No exceptions.
3. **Action items written before leaving.** Every item has an owner and a date.
4. **No spectators.** If you're not contributing, you shouldn't be there.
5. **Scorecard updated before, not during.** Meetings are for decisions, not data entry.
6. **Weekly cadence is non-negotiable.** These meetings happen every week. No rescheduling.
