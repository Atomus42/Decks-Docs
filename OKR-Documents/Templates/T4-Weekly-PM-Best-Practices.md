## Section 4: Blocker Escalation Protocol

```
BLOCKER ESCALATION PROTOCOL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Level 1 — SELF-RESOLVE (0-24h)
  Owner tries to unblock themselves.
  Document the blocker in the daily standup.

Level 2 — PEER RESOLVE (24-48h)
  Raise in daily BRA OKR Scoreboard.
  Assign a peer to help unblock.

Level 3 — ESCALATE TO LEADERSHIP (48h+)
  Call an ad-hoc escalation meeting (T2 Agenda 6).
  CEO or relevant owner makes a decision.

Level 4 — CUT LIST (if unresolvable)
  Reference A7 Cut List:
  1. Cut Axis 3 (stretch)
  2. Cut KR1.5d stretch (accept 15 min ship time)
  3. Cut conference attendance
  4. Cut internal process improvements
  NEVER cut: Pipeline reviews, demo prep, proposal speed, Fixing sessions
```
