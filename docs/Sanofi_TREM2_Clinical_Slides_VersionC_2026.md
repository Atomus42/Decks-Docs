## Slide 1: A Landscape in Motion — The Benefit-Risk Context for TREM2 Agonism Has Changed Since INVOKE-2

**Headline:** VG-3927 Enters a Radically Different Landscape Than the One INVOKE-2 Was Designed For — and the Benefit-Risk Question Has Changed Accordingly

**Content:**

**The landscape when INVOKE-2 was designed (~2021-2022):**

The question was straightforward: *Does TREM2 agonism work in Alzheimer's disease?* The genetic rationale was strong (R47H OR 2.92; Jonsson et al., *NEJM*, 2013; Guerreiro et al., *NEJM*, 2013). DAM biology was established (Keren-Shaul et al., *Cell*, 2017). No anti-amyloid antibody had yet been approved. Standard of care was symptomatic only — cholinesterase inhibitors and memantine. The competitive landscape for TREM2 agonism was essentially empty. A successful Phase 2 would have been the first disease-modifying evidence for microglial-targeted therapy in AD.

**The landscape VG-3927 enters now (March 2026):**

Every dimension of that original context has shifted.

**TREM2 agonist competitive landscape:**

| Program | Company | Modality | Status (March 2026) | Key Data |
|---------|---------|----------|-------------------|----|
| **AL002 (latozinemab)** | Alector/AbbVie | IV mAb, stalk-binding | **FAILED** Phase 2 (Nov 2024) | No benefit on CDR-SB or any secondary endpoint; ARIA-E 29%, ARIA-H 27% without amyloid clearance; APOE4 homozygotes most severely affected (*Front Aging Neurosci*, 2025) |
| **VHB937** | Novartis | IV mAb, IgSF-binding | **Phase 2 recruiting** | Mechanistically distinct from AL002: stabilizes surface TREM2, reduces SPP1/sCSF1R/CCL3; well tolerated in Phase 1 |
| **VG-3927** | Sanofi (ex-Vigil) | Oral small molecule | **Phase 2 planned** | First-in-class oral TREM2 agonist; no Fc domain; CSF/plasma ratio 0.91; ~50% CSF sTREM2 reduction at 25 mg; favorable Phase 1 safety (n=115) |
| **DNL919 (TAK-920)** | Denali/Takeda | ATV-engineered Ab | **Discontinued** (Aug 2023) | Narrow therapeutic window; TfR-mediated anemia |

**Anti-amyloid antibodies — the new treatment landscape:**

| Program | Company | Status | Key Efficacy | Key Safety |
|---------|---------|--------|-------------|-----------|
| **Lecanemab** | Eisai/Biogen | **Approved** (Jan 2023) | CLARITY AD: 27% slowing on CDR-SB (van Dyck et al., *NEJM*, 2023) | ARIA-E 12.6% overall |
| **Donanemab** | Eli Lilly | **Approved** (2024) | TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2: 35% slowing on CDR-SB (Sims et al., *JAMA*, 2023) | ARIA-E ~24% overall |

These are now part of the treatment landscape against which VG-3927 must be evaluated. They did not exist as approved therapies when INVOKE-2 was designed.

**New TREM2 biology since INVOKE-2 design:**

- TREM2 expression level independently determines whether agonism produces neuroprotective vs. neuroinflammatory microglial phenotypes (*Nature Communications*, 2026) — introducing patient-level heterogeneity not accounted for in INVOKE-2's design
- Comprehensive re-evaluation of TREM2/sTREM2 biology and the ambiguity of sTREM2 as a pharmacodynamic biomarker (*Mol Neurodegeneration*, 2025)
- Stage-dependent effects: TREM2 agonism may be protective in amyloid-dominant disease but harmful when tau pathology predominates (Shi et al., *The Neuroscientist*, 2025; Ulland & Colonna, *Nat Rev Neurol*, 2018)

**Updated regulatory frameworks:**

- CIOMS Working Group XII (2025): new international standard for structured benefit-risk assessment methodology
- EMA 2026 AI guideline for medicines regulation
- FDA AI credibility framework for regulatory submissions

**The benefit-risk question has changed:**

When INVOKE-2 was designed, the question was: *"Does TREM2 agonism work in AD?"*

The question for VG-3927 is fundamentally different and more complex: *"Can an oral TREM2 agonist — with a mechanism that avoids AL002's failure modes — add benefit beyond or alongside anti-amyloid therapy, with an acceptable safety profile, in appropriately selected patients, within an evolving competitive and scientific landscape?"*

This is not a question that can be answered with a static, one-time benefit-risk assessment. It requires continuous evidence integration as each element of the landscape evolves — competitive data, scientific publications, regulatory guidance, and VG-3927's own emerging clinical data.

## Slide 3: The VG-3927 Benefit-Risk Value Tree — A Living Framework for Clinical Decision-Making

**Headline:** A Structured, TREM2-Specific Benefit-Risk Value Tree — Every Node Backed by Evidence from the Profiling Base, Every Weight Configurable, Every Branch Dynamically Updated as New Data Emerge

**Content:**

**What the platform builds and maintains for VG-3927:**

ArcaScience's platform constructs a benefit-risk value tree — a structured, hierarchical framework that organizes all benefit and risk dimensions, links each to its underlying evidence, assigns configurable weights, and produces an integrated benefit-risk evaluation. This is not a static diagram. It is a living analytical structure that updates as new evidence enters the system. Below is the TREM2-specific value tree that the platform would build for VG-3927, based on the current evidence landscape:

---

**BENEFITS BRANCH:**

**1. Cognitive efficacy (primary clinical benefit)**
- Endpoint: CDR-SB (the regulatory standard for AD efficacy, used in CLARITY AD and TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2)
- Current evidence: No efficacy data exist for any TREM2 agonist; INVOKE-2 (AL002) showed zero benefit on CDR-SB (*Front Aging Neurosci*, 2025). VG-3927 preclinical data show ~40% plaque reduction in 5xFAD mice (VG-3927 PMC, 2024), but translation to human cognitive endpoints is unconfirmed.
- Evidence status: **Gap** — to be populated by VG-3927 Phase 2 data as it emerges

**2. Biomarker efficacy (mechanistic evidence of disease modification)**
- Endpoints: amyloid PET (plaque burden), CSF sTREM2 (target engagement — already demonstrated: ~50% reduction at 25 mg in Phase 1), p-tau-181/217 (tau phosphorylation), NfL (neurodegeneration), GFAP (astrocytic response)
- Current evidence: VG-3927 Phase 1 demonstrated dose-dependent CSF sTREM2 reduction and CSF/plasma ratio of 0.91; VHB937 (Novartis) reduces SPP1/sCSF1R/CCL3 dose-dependently in Phase 1
- Note: sTREM2 as a pharmacodynamic biomarker is interpretively complex — higher baseline CSF sTREM2 correlates with *slower* cognitive decline (Ewers et al., *Sci Transl Med*, 2019), making sTREM2 *reduction* paradoxical as a positive signal. The value tree captures this ambiguity and flags it for ongoing evidence monitoring.

**3. Convenience and access (differentiator vs. antibody therapies)**
- VG-3927: oral, once-daily dosing
- Lecanemab: IV infusion every 2 weeks
- Donanemab: IV infusion every 4 weeks
- VHB937: IV (frequency TBD)
- For elderly AD patients (mean age 65-80, often with mobility limitations and caregiver dependency), oral dosing is a material benefit that affects adherence, health system burden, and patient quality of life.

**4. Mechanism differentiation (potentially additive to anti-amyloid therapy)**
- VG-3927 activates microglia to enhance phagocytic clearance of amyloid via TREM2-mediated pathways — mechanistically distinct from and potentially complementary to antibody-mediated amyloid clearance (lecanemab, donanemab)
- Combination hypothesis: TREM2-activated microglia may phagocytose antibody-opsonized amyloid more efficiently, providing additive efficacy (Ulland & Colonna, *Nat Rev Neurol*, 2018)
- This is a hypothetical benefit — no clinical combination data exist — but it is a scientifically grounded positioning advantage that the value tree must capture and track as combination studies are considered

**5. Safety profile differentiation (structural advantage over antibody-based TREM2 agonists)**
- No Fc domain: eliminates the Fc-mediated effector functions (perivascular macrophage activation, complement-dependent vascular damage) that are the most probable mechanistic drivers of AL002's 29% ARIA-E rate
- No receptor internalization: preserves surface TREM2 density, maintaining physiological signaling rather than depleting the target
- No sTREM2 binding: avoids the sink effect of antibody binding to soluble TREM2
- Evidence status: pharmacological basis is strong; clinical confirmation pending Phase 2

---

**RISKS BRANCH:**

**1. ARIA-E / ARIA-H (the primary safety concern for the TREM2 agonist class)**
- AL002 INVOKE-2: ARIA-E 29%, ARIA-H 27% — occurring *without* amyloid clearance (*Front Aging Neurosci*, 2025)
- VG-3927: no Fc domain provides a strong mechanistic basis for expecting lower ARIA rates, but this is unconfirmed in a clinical AD population. Phase 1 (n=115) was not powered to detect ARIA.
- APOE4 genotype modulates ARIA risk: APOE4 homozygotes experienced the most severe events in INVOKE-2 and were excluded mid-trial. Lecanemab ARIA-E: 12.6% overall, 32-36% in APOE4 homozygotes (van Dyck et al., *NEJM*, 2023). Donanemab ARIA-E: ~24% overall (Sims et al., *JAMA*, 2023).
- Evidence status: **Critical uncertainty** — this node requires the most intensive evidence monitoring

**2. Chronic microglial activation effects (class-level unknown)**
- TREM2 agonism will chronically activate microglia. The long-term consequences of sustained microglial activation in elderly brains are uncharacterized beyond 76 weeks (INVOKE-2 maximum exposure).
- Stage-dependent risk: in tau-dominant disease, microglial activation may exacerbate tau phosphorylation and spreading (Shi et al., *The Neuroscientist*, 2025)
- TREM2 expression level determines whether agonism produces neuroprotective vs. neuroinflammatory microglial states (*Nature Communications*, 2026)

**3. Drug-drug interactions in elderly polypharmacy population**
- Target population (65-85 years, early-to-mild AD): mean 5-7 concurrent medications including cholinesterase inhibitors (>60% of AD patients), cardiovascular agents, and increasingly, anti-amyloid antibodies
- VG-3927 is a first-in-class oral small molecule — DDI profile in the real-world polypharmacy landscape is uncharacterized beyond Phase 1

**4. APOE4 genotype-dependent safety profile**
- APOE4 homozygosity increases baseline cerebral amyloid angiopathy burden, independently increasing ARIA susceptibility (Greenberg et al., *Lancet Neurol*, 2020)
- INVOKE-2 reactively excluded APOE4 homozygotes mid-trial — a reactive exclusion without prospective risk modeling
- VG-3927 Phase 2 must make a proactive enrollment decision for APOE4 homozygotes, informed by structured evidence

**5. Long-term immunomodulation effects (not detectable in 12-24 month trials)**
- TREM2 is expressed on tumor-associated macrophages; anti-TREM2 antibodies are under investigation in immuno-oncology because blocking TREM2 *enhances* anti-tumor immunity (Molgora et al., *Cell*, 2020) — chronic TREM2 agonism could theoretically impair tumor immunosurveillance
- Infection susceptibility in immunosenescent elderly patients receiving chronic microglial modulation is uncharacterized
- These are long-latency risks that will not appear in pivotal trials; the value tree must track them as uncertainty nodes with ongoing literature monitoring

---

**UNCERTAINTIES (neither benefit nor risk — requiring resolution):**

- **First oral TREM2 agonist:** No class precedent for the oral modality. PK/PD, tissue distribution, and off-target effects of an orally bioavailable, brain-penetrant TREM2 agonist are without comparison.
- **VG-3927 Phase 1 sample size:** n=115 is too small for definitive safety characterization, particularly for low-frequency events like ARIA.
- **Combination with anti-amyloid antibodies:** Untested. Mechanistic rationale for additive efficacy exists; risk of additive ARIA is unknown.
- **sTREM2 biomarker directionality:** Does pharmacological sTREM2 reduction mean the same thing as natural sTREM2 variation? The platform tracks this interpretive question as the evidence evolves.
