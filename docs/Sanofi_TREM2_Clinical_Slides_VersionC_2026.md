**Narrative premise:** The translational journey from TREM2 R47H genetic discovery (2013) to a marketed TREM2 agonist follows a defined pipeline: genetic validation, mechanistic elucidation, preclinical proof-of-concept, first clinical attempt, pharmacological differentiation, and re-entry. At each step, critical evidence was generated — and critical gaps were left open. VG-3927 now sits at the inflection point between Phase 1 success and Phase 2 design. The question is not whether the biology is sound — it is whether the evidence architecture connecting each translational step is sufficient to design a Phase 2 that succeeds where INVOKE-2 failed.

**Step 1 — Genetic validation (2013):** TREM2 R47H was identified independently by two groups (Jonsson et al., *NEJM*, 2013; Guerreiro et al., *NEJM*, 2013) as one of the strongest genetic risk factors for Alzheimer's disease since APOE4. Heterozygous R47H confers OR 2.92 (Jonsson), with later meta-analyses across 32,598 subjects estimating OR 4.11. Homozygous R47H carries a predicted OR of 97.1 (*NEJM*, 2023) — comparable to one APOE-epsilon-4 copy. R47H causes partial loss of function: approximately 55% reduction in LDL binding affinity and impaired phagocytosis of Abeta-lipoprotein complexes. Complete biallelic TREM2 loss-of-function causes Nasu-Hakola disease — fatal early-onset frontotemporal dementia with leukoencephalopathy and bone cysts.

*Evidence gap remaining:* Population-specific R47H variant frequency data have not been systematically compiled for clinical trial site selection; the APOE4 x TREM2 genotype compound interaction has never been modeled quantitatively for benefit AND risk simultaneously.

**Step 2 — Preclinical mechanism (2017):** Keren-Shaul et al. (*Cell*, 2017) identified the Disease-Associated Microglia (DAM) phenotype — a neuroprotective microglial state characterized by enhanced phagocytosis, plaque compaction, and barrier formation around amyloid deposits. DAM transition occurs in two stages: Stage 1 is TREM2-independent; Stage 2 is TREM2-dependent. This provided the mechanistic rationale for TREM2 activation as a therapeutic strategy. Ulland & Colonna (*Nat Rev Neurol*, 2018) consolidated the evidence that TREM2 loss-of-function impairs microglial response to amyloid pathology.

*Evidence gap remaining:* Microglial activation is stage-dependent — early activation (amyloid-dominant disease) is protective, while late activation (tau-dominant disease) may exacerbate pathology (Shi et al., *The Neuroscientist*, 2025). This directionality has never been resolved in a clinical population. TREM2 expression level independently determines agonist efficacy (*Nature Communications*, 2026), introducing patient-level heterogeneity not captured in preclinical models.

**Step 3 — First clinical attempt: INVOKE-2 (2024):** AL002 (anti-TREM2 monoclonal antibody, Alector/AbbVie) entered Phase 2 with robust preclinical data. INVOKE-2 enrolled 381 early AD patients over 48-96 weeks. Result: no benefit on CDR-SB primary endpoint; no amyloid PET reduction at any dose; ARIA-E in 29% and ARIA-H in 27% of treated participants — rates comparable to anti-amyloid antibodies but occurring *without amyloid clearance*. APOE4 homozygotes experienced the most severe events and were excluded mid-trial. Denali/Takeda subsequently discontinued DNL919, citing a "narrow therapeutic window."

*What this demonstrated:* The failure was mechanism-specific, not target-specific. AL002 binds the TREM2 stalk, promotes receptor internalization, depletes surface TREM2, and may paradoxically trigger pro-inflammatory signaling. Its Fc domain engages perivascular macrophages. These are properties of the molecule and modality, not of TREM2 as a target.

**Step 4 — Preclinical differentiation: VG-3927 (2024):** VG-3927, developed by Vigil Neuroscience (acquired by Sanofi August 2025 for approximately $470M), demonstrated in 5xFAD mice approximately 40% reduction in plaques and insoluble Abeta-42 over 6 weeks, with induction of DAM-like gene expression signatures (VG-3927 PMC, 2024). Critically, VG-3927 is a first-in-class oral small-molecule TREM2 agonist with no Fc domain, no sTREM2 binding, and no promotion of receptor internalization — addressing three of the four most likely mechanistic drivers of AL002's failure.

*Evidence gap remaining:* The same preclinical model class (5xFAD) that predicted AL002 would work predicted VG-3927 would work. The preclinical evidence base has not been systematically assessed for translational reliability in this mechanism class.

**Step 5 — Phase 1 success: VG-3927 (January 2025):** 115 participants, favorable safety, dose-dependent CSF sTREM2 reduction (~50% at 25 mg), and a CSF-to-unbound-plasma ratio of 0.91 — demonstrating exceptional BBB penetration. Phase 2 at 25 mg once-daily in Alzheimer's disease is planned.

*Evidence gap remaining:* Phase 1 was not powered for ARIA detection. The safety profile in a comorbid, elderly AD population with polypharmacy is uncharacterized. The pharmacodynamic meaning of sTREM2 reduction is ambiguous: higher CSF sTREM2 is associated with slower cognitive decline (Ewers et al., *Sci Transl Med*, 2019), and the sTREM2 biomarker is confounded by antibody binding in prior studies (Haass).

**The translational architecture that does not yet exist:**

The genetic signal is robust (OR 2.92-4.11). The biological rationale is sound (DAM transition is TREM2-dependent). The first clinical attempt failed for mechanism-specific reasons that VG-3927's pharmacology addresses. Phase 1 data are encouraging. But the evidence base connecting VG-3927's preclinical promise to a well-designed Phase 2 protocol — one that integrates genetic stratification, preclinical transposition confidence, failure-mode analysis, biomarker validation, and patient selection — does not exist in integrated form. Each piece of evidence lives in a different silo: genetics, preclinical pharmacology, clinical data, pharmacovigilance, epidemiology. No structured integration connects them.

## Slide 3: Predictive Benefit-Risk Modeling: How BR-PREDICT Bridges Preclinical Promise to Clinical Design

**Headline:** Three Concrete Analytical Workflows — Preclinical Transposition, Pharmacogenomic Interaction Modeling, and Counterfactual Clinical Simulation — That Bridge VG-3927's Preclinical Data to Phase 2 Design Decisions

**Content:**

**Analytical Workflow 1: Preclinical Transposition with Reliability Scoring (WP2)**

VG-3927 showed approximately 40% plaque reduction and insoluble Abeta-42 reduction in 5xFAD mice over 6 weeks, with induction of DAM-like gene expression signatures (VG-3927 PMC, 2024). This is compelling preclinical data. It is also preclinical data from the same model class that predicted AL002 would reduce amyloid in humans — which it did not.

WP2 calculates a **reliability score for the 5xFAD model in neurodegenerative disease** by comparing historical preclinical-to-clinical concordance rates across all programs that have used 5xFAD mice and proceeded to clinical development:

```
Reliability Score (5xFAD, Alzheimer's Disease, microglial-modulating mechanism) =

  Concordance_efficacy:   Fraction of 5xFAD efficacy predictions confirmed clinically
                          (includes AL002 [negative], aducanumab [partial], bapineuzumab
                          [negative], solanezumab [negative], VHB937 [pending])

  Concordance_safety:     Agreement between 5xFAD NOAEL/LOAEL and clinical safety
                          findings across matched programs

  Concordance_PK:         Correlation of preclinical and clinical PK parameters with
                          inter-species allometric correction

  Model-specific adjustment: Small molecule vs. biologic distinction — VG-3927 benefits
                             from simpler cross-species PK scaling without Fc-mediated
                             pharmacology confounds
```

**What this tells Sanofi:** A quantitative answer to the question "How much confidence should we place in the 5xFAD plaque reduction data?" — not as expert opinion, but as a computed reliability metric with historical basis and uncertainty quantification. This is the evidence that no preclinical study report can provide: not what VG-3927 did in mice, but how often what drugs do in this model translates to what they do in humans.

---

**Analytical Workflow 2: Pharmacogenomic Compound Interaction Modeling (WP3)**

The APOE4 x TREM2 genotype interaction is the most critical stratification variable for VG-3927's Phase 2. It has never been modeled quantitatively across efficacy AND safety simultaneously.

The biological basis:
- APOE4 modulates TREM2 processing and shedding kinetics, altering the membrane-bound-to-soluble TREM2 ratio that determines signaling potency (Yeh et al., *Neuron*, 2016)
- APOE4 homozygosity increases baseline cerebral amyloid angiopathy (CAA) burden, independently increasing ARIA susceptibility (Greenberg et al., *Lancet Neurol*, 2020)
- TREM2 R47H causes partial loss-of-function — a molecular glue mechanism that potentiates endogenous signaling may have differential efficacy in R47H carriers (where baseline signaling is impaired) versus common-variant carriers (where baseline signaling is intact)
- INVOKE-2 excluded APOE4 homozygotes mid-trial after serious ARIA events — a reactive exclusion without quantitative risk modeling (*Front Aging Neurosci*, 2025)

**WP3 builds genotype-stratified benefit-risk profiles** using integrated data from:
- INVOKE-2 published safety and subgroup data (APOE-stratified ARIA rates)
- Anti-amyloid antibody ARIA databases: lecanemab ARIA-E 12.6% overall, 32-36% in APOE4 homozygotes (van Dyck et al., *NEJM*, 2023); donanemab ARIA-E 24.0% (Sims et al., *JAMA*, 2023)
- TREM2 genetic epidemiology: R47H prevalence, APOE4 co-occurrence frequencies, population-specific genotype distributions from gnomAD
- Real-world vascular event rates by APOE genotype from CPRD, Optum, and MarketScan

**Output:** A compound genotype risk matrix: predicted benefit-risk profile for each APOE genotype (e2/e3, e3/e3, e3/e4, e4/e4) x TREM2 genotype (common variant, R47H heterozygous) combination — providing the quantitative basis for Sanofi's Phase 2 enrollment and stratification decisions.

**Analytical Workflow 3: Counterfactual Clinical Scenario Simulation (WP6)**

WP6 uses structural causal models (SCMs) initialized from the knowledge graph (WP5: Neo4j + RDF, >100K entities, >1M relations) to simulate interventions using do-calculus counterfactual reasoning. This enables pre-trial hypothesis generation that is anchored in structured evidence rather than expert intuition:

**Simulation 1:** *"What would VG-3927's B-R profile look like in a cohort enriched for TREM2 R47H carriers?"*
- Input: R47H partial loss-of-function parameters, VG-3927 molecular glue potentiation mechanism, historical TREM2 agonist data
- Output: Predicted efficacy differential (R47H carriers vs. common variant), predicted safety differential, power calculation for enriched vs. unselected designs
- Utility: Determines whether R47H enrichment is scientifically justified despite the prevalence constraint (~1-2% of AD)

**Simulation 2:** *"What is the projected safety differential for VG-3927 vs. VHB937 based on their mechanistic differences?"*
- Input: VG-3927 (oral small molecule, no Fc, no internalization, brain-penetrant) vs. VHB937 (IV antibody, IgSF-binding, stabilizes surface TREM2, dose-dependently reduces SPP1/sCSF1R/CCL3; Novartis Phase 2 recruiting)
- Output: Comparative ARIA risk, peripheral myeloid activation risk, CNS exposure-response modeling
- Utility: Competitive positioning and Phase 2 safety monitoring design informed by mechanism-level comparison

**Simulation 3:** *"What would the benefit-risk profile look like if VG-3927 were combined with lecanemab in amyloid-positive early AD?"*
- Input: VG-3927 mechanism (enhanced microglial phagocytosis), lecanemab mechanism (antibody-mediated amyloid clearance), additive ARIA risk from dual microglial/vascular perturbation
- Output: Projected combination efficacy, additive ARIA probability, optimal sequencing strategy
- Utility: Informs the combination strategy dimension (currently ABSENT in the evidence base) and generates testable hypotheses for future combination protocol design

These simulations generate pre-specified stratification criteria and testable hypotheses that can be embedded directly into the Phase 2 protocol — converting counterfactual reasoning into prospective clinical design elements.
