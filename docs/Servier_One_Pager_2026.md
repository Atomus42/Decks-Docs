### The Challenge

Servier's oncology transformation is succeeding — EUR 2.21B in oncology revenue (+54.6% YoY), 8 marketed products, 34 oncology pipeline projects, and a growing multi-jurisdictional footprint across FDA, EMA, ANSM, and 150+ countries. But every approval compounds the pharmacovigilance burden. TIBSOVO alone carries 5 approved indications, each requiring separate safety evidence synthesis and periodic reporting across multiple authorities. VORANIGO achieved multi-jurisdiction approval in under 18 months. The evidence assembly workload — sourcing, extracting, normalizing, and structuring data from clinical trials, literature, RWE, and regulatory submissions — is growing non-linearly. This phase of the assessment cycle consumes 60-70% of total cycle time. The constraint is not assessor expertise. It is the evidence preparation that precedes their judgment.

---

### The AI Gap

Servier has invested decisively in AI across the drug lifecycle: Insilico ($888M, discovery), Owkin (translational), Aitia (causal AI), Google Cloud (enterprise), Veeva RIM (regulatory operations). **No AI capability exists for pharmacovigilance, safety signal detection, or structured benefit-risk evidence assembly.** This is the gap ArcaScience fills — downstream of evidence generation, upstream of regulatory submission.

---

### What ArcaScience Does

ArcaScience is a **decision infrastructure layer** for benefit-risk intelligence. The platform uses **24 task-specific, clinician-trained AI models** in an eight-stage auditable pipeline (INGEST - CLASSIFY - SECTION - EXTRACT - RELATE - NORMALIZE - LINK - TEMPLATE) to structure evidence from heterogeneous sources into regulatory-ready packages (PSUR, PBRER, RMP, CTD 2.5). Every output traces to its source document, section, and extraction model. No generative component — the system extracts and structures, it does not fabricate. Human experts interpret and decide.
