## A.2 Critical Diagnosis: What Is Wrong

### Problem 1: The Generic Deck Is Not a Sales Deck — It Is an Investor Pitch

The current deck is built for fundraising and general awareness, not for enterprise conversion at a specific account. It leads with market sizing ($13.2B TAM, $2B SAM), exit landscape comparables, revenue projections ($600K → $33M), and competitive matrices. **None of this belongs in a Sanofi-facing account deck.** Sanofi's VP-level stakeholders do not care about ArcaScience's exit strategy or EBITDA projections. They care about whether ArcaScience solves their specific problems.

**Verdict: The generic deck must be entirely restructured for Sanofi. Not adapted — rebuilt.**

### Problem 2: The Sanofi Board Deck Is Good But Mis-Targeted for This Audience

The Board Deck (Sanofi_Board_Deck_2026.md) is well-written and strategically sophisticated. However:
- It was prepared for **Pius Hornstein (Digital) and Lionel Bascles (CSO)** — senior executives focused on digital transformation and clinical operations.
- The current target audience is **10 different stakeholders** across translational R&D, preclinical safety, PV, patient safety, data strategy, benefit-risk, and AI in clinical trials.
- The Board Deck's "five-year partnership" framing and portfolio-scale infrastructure proposal are appropriate for C-suite expansion conversations, but **too high-altitude for functional leaders** who need to see how ArcaScience fits their specific workflows.
- The Board Deck does not sufficiently address **tolebrutinib** (mentioned obliquely), **Dupixent expansion complexity**, or **the HS project** as a specific proof point.

**Verdict: The Board Deck is useful source material but needs significant reworking for this audience.**
