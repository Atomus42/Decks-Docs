### The Challenge

Sanofi manages ~82 clinical-stage projects with 25+ mid-to-late-stage readouts over the next two years. Every pipeline decision — advance, pause, terminate — depends on benefit-risk judgment. But the evidence that informs those judgments is growing faster than teams can assemble it manually: 700,000+ adverse event versions annually (growing 20%/year), fragmenting across clinical trials, real-world evidence, literature, and multi-agency regulatory intelligence. Evidence assembly — not expert judgment — is the rate-limiting step, consuming 60-70% of total assessment cycle time. After the 2025 late-stage setbacks, comprehensive and timely evidence synthesis is not optional. It is the governance standard the Board has mandated.

---

### What ArcaScience Does

ArcaScience is a **decision infrastructure layer** for benefit-risk intelligence. The platform uses **24 task-specific, clinician-trained AI models** in an eight-stage auditable pipeline (INGEST - CLASSIFY - SECTION - EXTRACT - RELATE - NORMALIZE - LINK - TEMPLATE) to extract, normalize, link, and structure evidence from heterogeneous sources into regulatory-ready packages (PSUR, PBRER, RMP, CTD 2.5). Every output traces to its source document, section, and extraction model. ArcaScience does not make decisions or generate text. It structures the evidence so that Sanofi's experts can make better-informed decisions faster.
