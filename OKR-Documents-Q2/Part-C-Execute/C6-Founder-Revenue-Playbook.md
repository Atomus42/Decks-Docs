# C6 — Founder Revenue Playbook

> CEO (Tom) is the primary revenue driver. This playbook ensures he spends ≥ 15 hours/week on revenue and doesn't become the bottleneck.

---

## Time Allocation (6-Week Sprint)

| Activity | Hours/Week | Priority | Non-Negotiable? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outbound prospecting (Top-10 + biotech) | 5 h | P0 | Yes |
| Discovery calls + demos | 4 h | P0 | Yes |
| Proposal writing + negotiation | 3 h | P0 | Yes |
| Pipeline review + forecast (B2, B3, B5) | 2 h | P0 | Yes |
| i-Demo launch coordination (Wk2–4) | 2 h | P1 | Yes (Wk2–4 only) |
| LinkedIn / content / brand | 1 h | P2 | No — cut if needed |
| Internal (product, board, ops) | 3 h | P2 | No — cut if needed |
| **Total revenue hours** | **≥ 15 h** | | |

**Rule:** If revenue hours < 15/week → CEO restructures calendar. Cancel internal meetings first.

## Top-10 Enterprise Deal Playbook

| Step | When | Action | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wk1 | Research account: org chart, PV team, active programs, recent news | 30 min per account |
| 2 | Wk1 | Warm intro via investor, advisor, or Vassili KOL network | 1 email/LinkedIn per account |
| 3 | Wk1–2 | Cold outreach: 3-touch sequence (email → LinkedIn → email) | 15 min per touch |
| 4 | Wk2 | Discovery call: validate MEDDPIC, map pain, identify champion | 45 min |
| 5 | Wk3 | Demo: BRA platform + chem module, with Vassili for clinical Q&A | 60 min |
| 6 | Wk3–4 | Proposal: SOW + pricing + timeline + success criteria | 2 h per proposal |
| 7 | Wk4–5 | Negotiate: terms, multi-year, chem module bundle | As needed |
| 8 | Wk5–6 | Close: signature, payment terms, onboarding schedule | 1 h |
