# C5 — Customer Expansion Playbook

> Turn existing BRA customers into chemical-analysis module buyers and tier upgrades.

---

## Expansion Triggers & Motions

| Trigger | Signal Source | Action | Offer | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Heavy BRA usage (> 10 BRAs/month) | APM dashboard | Proactive call: "You're power users — let's discuss expanding" | Tier upgrade or seat expansion | CEO + Charbel |
| Multiple departments | User analytics | Multi-thread into new department | New department license or enterprise upgrade | CEO |
| Chemical-analysis interest | Demo request or feature inquiry | Schedule chem module demo | Chem Module Add-On (€30k–€50k/yr) | CEO + Vassili |
| i-Demo launch attendance | Event attendee list cross-ref with customers | Same-day follow-up | i-Demo Chem Bundle (€50k–€75k/yr) | CEO |
| Contract renewal < 90 days | Calendar | Renewal + expansion conversation | Multi-year + chem module bundle | CEO |
| Positive NPS or champion referral | Customer feedback | Ask for referral → warm intro to new account | Referral incentive (if applicable) | CEO |

---

## Expansion Conversation Framework

| Step | Question | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "How many BRAs has your team completed this month?" | Gauge adoption |
| 2 | "Which steps take the most time?" | Identify automation pain |
| 3 | "Are you doing any compound-level analysis separately today?" | Open chem module conversation |
| 4 | "Would having ADMET and mechanism data inside the BRA workflow save time?" | Position value |
| 5 | "We're launching the chemical-analysis module — would a demo be useful?" | Soft ask |

---

## Expansion Revenue Targets

| Metric | Target | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Expansion conversations initiated | ≥ 3 | CEO |
| Chem Module upsells closed | ≥ 1 | CEO |
| Tier upgrades closed | ≥ 1 | CEO + Charbel |
| Referrals generated | ≥ 2 | CEO |
