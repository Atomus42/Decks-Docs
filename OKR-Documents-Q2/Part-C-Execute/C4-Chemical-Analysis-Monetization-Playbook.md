# C4 — Chemical-Analysis Monetization Playbook (NEW — Replaces Disease-Specific Playbook)

> How to position, price, and sell the chemical-analysis module as an add-on to the BRA platform.

---

## Positioning

### The Pitch (30 seconds)

> "Our BRA platform now includes chemical-analysis capabilities — compound search, ADMET prediction, mechanism of action, bioactivity analysis, and drug-target binding — all integrated directly into the benefit-risk workflow. Instead of switching between 5 different tools, your team gets compound-level intelligence inside the same platform where they do their BRA."

### Why Chemical Analysis Matters for BRA

| Without Chem Module | With Chem Module |
|---|---|
| BRA based on clinical endpoints only | BRA enriched with compound-level data |
| Manual ADMET lookups from separate databases | Automated ADMET profiling in-workflow |
| No mechanism-of-action context in B&R Assessment | Mechanism + pathway data feeds risk scoring |
| Decision-Support lacks molecular evidence | Drug-target binding informs benefit-risk trade-offs |

### Target Buyer Personas

| Persona | Title | Pain Point | Chem Module Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PV Director** | VP Pharmacovigilance | Manual safety signal assessment | ADMET + bioactivity automates safety profiling |
| **R&D Lead** | Head of Drug Development | Fragmented tools across teams | Single platform for clinical + chemical analysis |
| **Regulatory Affairs** | Director Regulatory | Incomplete BRA dossiers | Compound-level evidence strengthens submissions |
| **Chief Medical Officer** | CMO | Risk oversight gaps | Drug-target binding + mechanism fills risk context |

## Pricing & Packaging

| Package | What's Included | Price | Who It's For |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chem Module Add-On** | 5 sub-modules callable from BRA platform | €30k–€50k/yr | Existing BRA customers (any tier) |
| **i-Demo Chem Bundle** | Chem module + 2 validated case studies + demo walkthrough | €50k–€75k/yr | New prospects at Tier 2+ |
| **Enterprise Chem Package** | Chem module + custom case studies + dedicated onboarding | €75k–€100k/yr | Top-10 strategic accounts |

### Anti-Custom Rules

1. **No custom compound analysis for < €50k** — we ship the module, not bespoke studies
2. **Case studies are curated, not custom** — use the 3 validated case studies for demos
3. **Roadmap requests go to product** — do NOT commit to specific compounds in the sales process
4. **Custom pricing requires CEO + Charbel sign-off** — no field discounts
