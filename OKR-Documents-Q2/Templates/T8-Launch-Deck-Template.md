# T8 — Launch Deck Template

> Use this template to prepare any product launch presentation. Adapted for the i-Demo Chemical Analysis launch but reusable.

---

## Slide Structure (12–18 slides)

| Slide # | Title | Content Type | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cover | Title + subtitle + date + ArcaScience logo | — |
| 2 | Why This Matters | 3 bullets: market pain, our solution, what's new | 1 min |
| 3 | The Problem | Specific pain points for target audience | 2 min |
| 4 | Our Solution | Platform overview + new module | 2 min |
| 5 | Live Demo | Screen walkthrough (or video) | 5–10 min |
| 6 | Case Study 1 | Compound → Analysis → BRA Integration → Insight | 3 min |
| 7 | Case Study 2 | Different compound, different therapeutic area | 2 min |
| 8 | How It Works | Architecture diagram: module → BRA pipeline | 2 min |
| 9 | Pricing & Packages | Table: tiers + add-ons | 1 min |
| 10 | What Customers Say | Testimonial or early-access feedback | 1 min |
| 11 | Getting Started | 3 steps: demo → trial → contract | 1 min |
| 12 | Q&A | Contact info + booking link | 5 min |

---

## Design Guidelines

| Element | Guidance |
|---|---|
| **Theme** | Dark background (navy/teal), white text, clinical-tech aesthetic |
| **Font** | Inter or similar sans-serif |
| **Slides** | 1 headline + 3–5 bullets max. No walls of text |
| **Tables** | Compact, 3–5 rows max per slide |
| **Visuals** | Subtle icons, no stock photos. Platform screenshots for demo slides |
| **Aspect Ratio** | 16:9 |
| **Export** | PDF + PPTX |

---

## Pre-Launch Checklist

- [ ] All slides have a punchy headline (< 10 words)
- [ ] Demo flow tested and timed
- [ ] Case study data validated
- [ ] Pricing approved by CEO
- [ ] Booking / CTA link functional
- [ ] Dry run completed (presenter + Q&A host)
