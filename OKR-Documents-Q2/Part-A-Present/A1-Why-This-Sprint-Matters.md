# A1 — Why This Sprint Matters

> Use this section for team kickoff, investor updates, and board presentations.

This is a **proof sprint**. We have a working platform, an emerging i-Demo product, and a pipeline of Top-10 pharma targets. In 6 weeks we prove three things:

| Proof Point | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Production-grade BRA platform** | All 5 pipeline steps (Context, B&R Assessment, Data Analysis, Reporting, Decision-Support) live in Prod, F1 ≥ 90 %, < 1 h human work per BRA, 99.5 % availability |
| **i-Demo Chemical Analysis launched** | Chemical-analysis module (ADMET, mechanism, bioactivity, drug-target binding) publicly launched, ≥ 3 validated case studies, launch event executed |
| **€500k signed with Top-10 + biotechs** | ≥ 3 of 10 named strategic accounts closed, weighted pipeline ≥ €2M, ≥ 2 multi-year deals |

If we prove all three, we are commercially validated. If we prove two, we have strong momentum. If we prove one, the sprint failed.

**Sprint Narrative:** *"Finalize the BRA platform to production-grade, launch the i-Demo chemical-analysis module publicly, and close ≥ €500k of signed contracts with biotechs and our Top-10 strategic targets — all in 6 weeks."*
