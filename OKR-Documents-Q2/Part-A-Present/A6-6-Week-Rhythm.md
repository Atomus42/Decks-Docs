# A6 — 6-Week Rhythm: Finalize → Launch → Close

| Phase | Weeks | O1 (Finalize Platform) | O2 (i-Demo Chem Launch) | O3 (€500k Revenue) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **FINALIZE** | **1–2** | All 5 pipeline steps in Prod; F1 ≥ 85 %; < 2 h human work; 99 % uptime; scope frozen | ≥ 2 chem sub-modules deployed; case study compounds selected; launch date confirmed | Pipeline restaged to 3x; Top-10 outreach (meetings booked); 10+ outbounds/day; pricing sheet finalized |
| **LAUNCH** | **3–4** | F1 ≥ 90 %; < 1 h human work; end-to-end smoke test green; Reporting + Decision-Support polished | All 5 sub-modules in Prod; ≥ 1 case study validated; demo asset live; launch event executed (Wk4); lead capture active | 3–5 demos delivered to Top-10; 2–3 proposals sent; first deal at S4+; biotech long-tail blitz |
| **CLOSE** | **5–6** | All DoD criteria met; platform hardened; board metrics compiled | ≥ 3 case studies validated; ≥ 50 leads captured; post-launch nurture running | ≥ 3 Top-10 signed; ≥ 3 biotech logos; €500k target; ≥ 2 multi-year; pipeline ≥ €2M |

---

## Phase Gates

| Gate | When | Criteria | If Not Met |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Finalize Gate** | End of Wk2 | All 5 BRA steps in Prod; F1 ≥ 85 %; launch date confirmed | Scope-cut Reporting or Decision-Support to V0; delay launch by 1 week max |
| **Launch Gate** | End of Wk4 | i-Demo event executed; ≥ 1 case study validated; ≥ 2 Top-10 proposals sent | Extend launch window to Wk5; CEO enters 100 % close mode regardless |
| **Close Gate** | End of Wk6 | €500k signed OR clear path to close within 2 weeks post-sprint | Post-mortem: document what broke, carry forward to Q3 OKR |
