## Five Pricing Rules (updated for Q2)

1. **Never sell platform without a chemical-analysis demo** — show the chem module in every demo even if not purchased
2. **Never do custom work for < €50k** — price consulting at €2,500/day
3. **Annual contracts only** — no monthly, no quarterly
4. **Multi-year discount:** 10 % (2yr), 15 % (3yr) — use to close Top-10 faster
5. **Pilots:** 3 months at 40 % of annual, with defined success criteria upfront
