# A7 — Risk Matrix: What Could Kill the Sprint

| # | Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pipeline too thin entering Wk3** | High | Fatal | CEO: 15+ outbounds/day Wk1–2. If pipeline < 2x by Wk2 → cancel all non-revenue meetings |
| 2 | **Top-10 sales cycles exceed 6 weeks** | High | Severe | Pre-qualify ruthlessly. Kill any Top-10 deal without champion by Wk3. Shift volume to biotech long-tail |
| 3 | **Chem-analysis module not production-ready by Wk3** | Medium | Severe | Scope-cut to 3 core sub-modules (Compound Search, ADMET, Mechanism). Ship 80 % quality over 100 % late |
| 4 | **F1 scores stall below 90 %** | Medium | High | Weekly "Fixing" sessions. Calibration sprints. Accept 85 % with documented improvement plan |
| 5 | **i-Demo launch generates noise but no leads** | Medium | High | Pre-seed 20+ invitees from Top-10 contacts. Direct outreach post-event within 24 h |
| 6 | **Custom work trap on Top-10 deals** | Medium | Severe | "Platform + Chem Module first." No custom work < €50k. Triage with CEO + Charbel |

## Cut List (in order of priority if spread too thin)

1. Cut Drug-Target Binding sub-module (5th sub-module) — ship post-sprint
2. Cut 3rd case study — launch with 2 validated, deliver 3rd post-launch
3. Cut biotech long-tail stretch targets — focus on Top-10 closes
4. Cut post-launch nurture automation — do manual follow-up instead
5. **NEVER cut:** Pipeline reviews, demo prep, Thursday "Fixing" sessions, launch event
