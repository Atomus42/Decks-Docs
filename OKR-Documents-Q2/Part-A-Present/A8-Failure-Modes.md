# A8 — Failure Modes: The Five Ways We Lose

| # | Mode | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"Great launch, no revenue"** | i-Demo event gets buzz, 100+ leads, but zero convert to signed contracts. Marketing win, commercial failure. Board loses confidence in GTM |
| 2 | **"Platform isn't ready"** | O1 slips — Reporting and Decision-Support steps not in Prod. Demos show incomplete product. Prospects stall. i-Demo launch postponed. Entire sprint collapses |
| 3 | **"Top-10 mirage"** | All 10 strategic accounts in "discovery" but none close. Long cycles, slow procurement, no urgency. €500k depends on biotech long-tail alone — not enough volume |
| 4 | **"Custom work quicksand"** | Top-10 deals devolve into bespoke consulting. Looks like €500k but it's services, not product revenue. Margins crater, team burns out |
| 5 | **"Launch fatigue"** | Team so focused on i-Demo launch prep that platform finalization (O1) and pipeline building (O3) get deprioritized. Sprint ends with a nice event and nothing else |
