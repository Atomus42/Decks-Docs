## B.2 Formalize the i-Demo → Pipeline Handoff

The i-Demo launch (O2) is designed to generate leads that feed O3 pipeline. Make the handoff explicit:

1. **Lead capture form** includes: company name, role, therapeutic area, interest level (1–5)
2. **Within 24 h:** CEO reviews leads, assigns to Top-10 or Long-Tail pipeline
3. **Within 48 h:** Personalized follow-up sent (meeting request or demo booking)
4. **Within 7 days:** All high-intent leads (interest ≥ 4) at S1 (meeting booked) or disqualified
